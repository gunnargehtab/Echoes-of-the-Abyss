# The Ledger 5 — Tolerance

> The fifth mission of the Consortium campaign ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §4), specified.
> Written to the pattern [mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) sets and the Ledger
> documents continue: everything here is authored — the forces, the water, the beats, the
> numbers and the text — and code transcribes this document.

**Setting:** The Holding's Underworks — the pre-Collapse vent works the vertical city stands
on, 214 PC, seventeen years after Kell ([world-map.md](world-map.md);
[timeline.md](timeline.md)).

**Mission id:** `ledger-tolerance` — namespaced by campaign, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §1.

---

## 1. What the Underworks Are

The Holding is the first entry in the registry, and it is a retrofit like everything else the
Rift owns ([timeline.md](timeline.md)): the vertical city stands on the Surface Age's vent
works — galleries and castings poured before year zero, under an overhang of city that has
been growing downward for two centuries. The registry calls this fabric the Underworks. The
engineers call it the root, and the root has failed.

At the turn of the tide, a casting poured before the Collapse cracked at 2,000 m. The
Underworks are flooding upward — slowly, in stages, the way pressure does everything — and
the cascade has two ends. Down: the root aperture, the crack itself, at a depth no working
hull in the Holding is rated for. Up: **Sector Vayle**, the berth-sector whose bulkhead frame
stands where the cascade reaches the city, eleven hundred berths at 1,050 m, evacuating now,
with two hundred and forty still inside at the breach alarm.

The works yard holds tungsten for exactly one casting, and the gauge is the mercy and the
cruelty of the thing at once: the Consortium standardised its apertures two centuries ago, so
the one plug the yard can pour fits the root's crack and fits Sector Vayle's bulkhead frame,
and cannot be in two places. Everything the register is — the pricing, the flat voice, the
refusal to look away — was built for this table, and this mission sits the player at it.

**Seventeen years ago this was Varr-Kest's table** ([timeline.md](timeline.md), 197 PC — the
Kell entry; [characters.md](characters.md)). She signed the order that flooded a sector to
save the habitat, and she has read the names every first tide since. She is in the room for
this one, on the channel, and she does not tell the player what to do, because the whole
mission is that nobody can.

## 2. Whose Hulls the Player Commands

**The player commands the breach writ: the Holding's emergency column — an armed escort, the
casting barge, and one pour of tungsten.**

The escort is armed because the wall is loud today and the Drift attends loudness
([bestiary.md](bestiary.md)): a containment failure is the largest acoustic event the Holding
has produced in seventeen years, and the packs that shadow the grid have already turned. The
writ prices them as weather, per standing doctrine. It prices everything else on this water
per second.

**Engine bound, stated so nobody corrects it into a bug.** Two parties and a court slot: the
column, and the Holding itself — whose assets in the water are two sounds, an alarm and a
complaint. The Drift is not a party.

### What the column does not carry

1. **A second casting.** The gauge was cut from the root's survey and the yard's tungsten
   poured once. The writ says so in its first breath, because the whole mission is downstream
   of it.
2. **A PR-3 hull.** The Consortium buys its way down ([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md)
   §3), and the Holding's refit berths are on the wrong side of the breach. What the column
   has is PR-2 and the ledger; the mission is what that costs.
3. **Active sonar, unlocked and beside the point.** The array is carried and nothing here
   needs finding: both apertures are charted, the packs announce themselves, and a ping spent
   today is a ping the player wanted, not one the mission asks for.

## 4. The Ledger Below the Line

The system this mission teaches, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §2: **depth as unhealable
arithmetic** — the crush ledger, read before it is paid.

[systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §2 states the rule the campaign has kept off-stage for
four missions: a hull below its Pressure Rating takes crush attrition that ignores repair,
regeneration, and everything else the Consortium is good at. The numbers, so the player can
do the table's arithmetic ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §4 — *"The arithmetic is not hidden
from you"*):

- **The line is at 1,800 m.** Above it, a PR-2 hull is whole water. Below it, the ledger
  runs at **four points of hull a second** — flat, per second, uninterested in bravery.
- **The barge is three hundred points of hull.** The root run — dive through the throat,
  under the overhang, to the aperture and back, driven clean — is about thirty seconds below
  the line: roughly forty per cent of the barge, spent, permanently, for one delivery. Driven
  badly it is the barge.
- **Descent is fast and loud; ascent is slow and silent** ([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md)
  §2). The ledger runs on the way down, at the aperture, and all the slow way back up to the
  line — the climb is where the arithmetic gets personal, and every player will watch the
  unhealable segment of that bar the whole way.

The Underworks are **roofed**: the overhang admits water only between 1,900 and 2,100 m, so
the root aperture cannot be approached from above, hovered over, or cheated — reaching it
means the dive, through the throat, below the rating, on purpose
([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §2: a roofed passage is a route you pay the loud
descent to use). Sector Vayle's frame, by contrast, is whole water all the way — a longer
haul, a louder neighbourhood, and not one point of crush. That asymmetry is the choice, in
metres.

### The SIG budget

**SIG budget: 70** — the loudest in the campaign so far, and not inverted for once: the
breach is already the loudest thing on the wall, the packs have already turned, and the writ
does not price noise today. It prices seconds.

## 6. The Choice

**One casting. Two apertures. The mission does not soften it, average it, or take it back.**

The exclusivity is authored, not emergent: the moment the casting is set in either aperture,
the other objective fails and its every announcement is retired — fired by the delivery
itself rather than by any clock, the condition-fired arrangement
[mission-exposure.md](mission-exposure.md) §4 spends, extended here with the choice group it
needed for the campaign's heaviest table. There is no build order, no second pour, and no
reading of the rules under which both columns fill. Three facts, stated flatly, the way the
writ states them:

- **The root is the habitat.** Unsealed, the cascade climbs the Underworks and the Holding's
  lower berths begin controlled abandonment — not a detonation, a diminishing: the city that
  owns everyone's air, smaller, poorer, and insolvency eleven years closer. Sealing it costs
  the ledger: the dive, the overhang, and forty per cent of the barge, if the run is clean.
- **The section is the people still in it.** Two hundred and forty behind Vayle's frame when
  the alarm sounded, and the evacuation is slower than the water. Sealing the frame is whole
  water and a long haul — no crush, no dive, and no version of it in which the root is also
  reached.
- **The arithmetic is not hidden from you.** Two hundred and forty lives against the city's
  foundation. Varr-Kest ran this table seventeen years ago with a stroke of a pen and 1,900
  names; the campaign gives the player the same table with a barge and a clock, and her only
  contribution is to be present while they run it — which is, the player may notice, more
  than anyone gave her.

Either delivery is the mission succeeding. That is the point, and the register's readings at
§8 treat both with the same flat respect. What the mission refuses to provide is a version of
the tide in which the choice was not made.

## 7. What Answers

**The packs, and the water, and nothing else.** No rival navy reaches the Holding's own wall
in force — this mission's antagonist is pressure, in both senses the Rift uses the word.

The packs are real pressure: two of them, drawn by the breach, committing to the loudest hull
in reach on the doctrine the campaign taught in mission 1 — and the flagship run correctly is
still the answer, which is the Klaxon posture load-bearing at the exact moment the player is
poorest. A pack that commits to the casting barge on the Vayle haul, or catches the root run
at the throat with the ledger already spent, is how either delivery actually fails.

The water is the clock: the cascade's stages are authored beats, audible as the flood alarm
climbing its registers, and the writ closes at 17:00 when the water stops where it has
reached. The last ninety seconds are preceded, as ever, by the oldest warning the campaign
has — a pack, loud, arriving ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §10).

## 8. The Objective

**Pour the casting. Set it in one aperture. Live with the tide.**

### Results

| Result | Condition | The reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Both sealed** | Unreachable, and authored so the ladder is honest | "Both apertures read sealed. The registry notes that the writ funded one casting, and files the report for review rather than for reading." |
| **One order signed** | The casting set in either aperture | "One order was signed. The water stops where it has reached, and the count is taken. The Board convenes at the next bell; the reading below is the whole of the minutes." |
| **No seal set** | The whistle, with the casting unset | "No seal was set. The root goes in the third tide and the section in the first, and the register, which does not strike assets, begins its longest entry since Kell." |

Beneath the middle reading, the close assembles which order it was — each aperture's
objective carries its met and unmet lines, in the arrangement
[mission-shift-change.md](mission-shift-change.md) §8 built — and the two unmet lines are
written with the same care as the met ones, because the register's refusal to editorialise is
the nearest thing to grief it has ([mission-asset-recovery.md](mission-asset-recovery.md)
§8).

### The failure, and the sounds that precede it

The close is not a timer wearing a costume: the cascade has been audible since the first tick
— the breach alarm at Vayle in one register, the root's complaint under the overhang in
another, and both climbing as the stages land. The final stage is called by the alarm a full
minute out, and the packs' last press begins at 15:30, ninety seconds ahead of the whistle
([campaign.md](campaign.md) §10).

## 10. What It Teaches

One system, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10: **the crush ledger** — depth as unhealable
arithmetic, read in advance, spent on purpose, and felt on the one bar in the game that
never refills. Load-bearing by the last five minutes: the root run is only survivable with
the ledger pre-computed, and a player who reaches the aperture on instinct instead of
arithmetic comes back up — if they come back up — with the mission's whole lesson on their
health bar.

What this mission deliberately does not teach:

- **The Abyssal round trip as an economy** — mission 6, *Prospect*, where the descent stops
  being a crisis and becomes the business model.
- **A way to have both.** The campaign's third rule of losing ([campaign.md](campaign.md)
  §2): three missions are unwinnable as fights and winnable as something else. This one is
  unwinnable as a totality and winnable as a decision, which is the Consortium's version of
  the rule, and Varr-Kest's biography in mechanism form.

## 12. The Briefing

The breach writ, read to the column at the yard. Signed for the Ninth Board, with the Chair
present. The Consortium's register is defined in [culture.md](culture.md) §3.

> A casting poured before year zero failed at the turn of the tide. The Underworks are
> flooding in stages. The cascade reaches Sector Vayle's frame above and the root aperture
> below, and the yard's tungsten pours once. The gauge fits both apertures. That is not a
> provision of the writ; it is a fact about the concern's standards, and the writ declines to
> apologise for either.
>
> Below the line at eighteen hundred metres, a hull spends four points a second of what does
> not heal. The root run is roughly thirty seconds below the line, driven clean. The barge is
> three hundred points. The arithmetic is stated so that nobody performs it for the first
> time under the overhang.
>
> Two hundred and forty berths were occupied in Vayle at the alarm. The evacuation proceeds
> and will not finish. The root carries the Holding, which carries the count you already
> know, because everyone does.
>
> The writ funds the pour, the escort, and one delivery. It does not state a preference. The
> Board has considered whether that is a mercy or an abdication and has minuted the question
> without resolving it.
>
> The Chair is on the channel. Exposure is authorised. Sentiment is not. Signed for the
> Board.

### Objective readings, in play

- *Pour the casting. The gauge fits both apertures; the arithmetic does not.*
- *The root is the habitat. What it costs to reach is on the ledger, per second, and does not heal.*
- *The section is the people still in it. The frame takes the same gauge.*

### The voices on the channel

**Executor Odile Varr-Kest — 00:45**

> This is the Chair. I am on the channel and I am not in your chain of command for the next
> seventeen minutes; the writ put the casting in your yard, not in mine. I have run this
> table once. I will not describe it. Proceed.

**Underworks Warden Cass Ohlen — 02:30**

> Below the line your hulls spend what does not heal, at four a second, and the casting does
> not care which aperture it seals. That is the whole of my advice. I have been asked twice
> what I would do and I have declined twice, and I am declining now for the third and last
> time.

**Ohlen, at stage two — 11:00**

> Stage two is in the frame. The alarm you are hearing is the last one the section carries.

**Varr-Kest, if the root is sealed — fired by the delivery**

> Entered. The section is written down. The count will be taken when the water stops, and I
> will read it on the first tide, with the others.

**Varr-Kest, if the section is sealed — fired by the delivery**

> Entered. The root is written down. I signed the other version of this order seventeen years
> ago; the register will note the difference and draw no conclusion. Neither will I, in your
> hearing.

Each line fails [culture.md](culture.md) §3 for the other three factions, which is that
document's own test (§6): Varr-Kest's refusals to describe, conclude, or command are the
language of instruments carrying more weight than the instruments were rated for; Ohlen's
triple declination is a man keeping the one thing the register cannot price, which is his
opinion. The Commune could not stay out of the choice, the Knights could not stay flat about
it, and the Directorate could not put the agency where every line here puts it: in the room,
on the player, priced.
