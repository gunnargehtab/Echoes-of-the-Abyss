# Echoes of the Abyss — Factions

## Bathyarch Consortium

Industrial megacorp dominating deep-sea mining. Brutalist, heavy, utilitarian.

### Playstyle

Heavy industry, strong economy, slow but powerful units.

### Superweapon

Thermal Lance — melts enemy bases with concentrated heat.

### Silhouette Language

- Blocky, rectangular, reinforced
- External pipes, ballast tanks, cranes
- Thick armor plates with visible bolts
- Slow, heavy silhouettes with low center of mass

### Materials

- Pressure-forge steel
- Tungsten plating
- Rusted iron
- Hazard-striped industrial composites

### Color Palette

- Iron Grey (#6E6E72)
- Hazard Yellow (#F2C94C)
- Rust Orange (#C56A2D)
- Deep Steel Blue (#1F2A3A)

### FX & Animation

- Welding sparks
- Steam vents
- Cavitation trails from heavy engines
- Slow turret rotation, mechanical clunks

### UI Identity

- Angular industrial font
- Yellow warning frames
- Sonar-grid backgrounds
- Heavy metallic button borders

### Environmental Presence

- Mining rigs
- Vent-drill platforms
- Cargo depots
- Floodlights illuminating dark trenches

## Pelagia Commune

Eco-radical bio-collective living in kelp forests. Organic, flowing, bioluminescent.

### Playstyle

Fast, stealthy units; biological tech; high mobility.

### Superweapon

Bloom Surge — disables enemy sensors with organic spores.

### Silhouette Language

- Curved, manta-like shapes
- Smooth, grown-not-built hulls
- Fins, tendrils, coral-like protrusions
- Elegant, stealthy silhouettes

### Materials

- Algae composites
- Coral plating
- Biopolymer membranes
- Bioluminescent nodes

### Color Palette

- Algae Teal (#2AB8A6)
- Soft Green (#7ED957)
- Pearl White (#F2F2F2)
- Deep Kelp Blue (#0D3B4C)

### FX & Animation

- Soft glowing trails
- Organic torpedo spores
- Pulsing membranes
- Gentle, fluid movement

### UI Identity

- Rounded soft font
- Transparent green overlays
- Bioluminescent accents
- Ripple animations on selection

### Environmental Presence

- Floating kelp cities
- Coral towers
- Algae farms
- Bioluminescent gardens

## Abyssal Directorate

Authoritarian trench-dwellers using bio-engineered pressure creatures. Dark, predatory, oppressive.

### Playstyle

Swarm tactics, cheap units, trench-adapted hulls.

### Superweapon

Trench Awakening — summons abyssal creatures.

### Silhouette Language

- Spiked, jagged, insectoid
- Multi-segment hulls
- Chitin armor plates
- Swarm-like silhouettes

### Materials

- Abyssal chitin
- Bio-pressure hulls
- Hardened bone composites
- Blackened steel

### Color Palette

- Abyss Black (#05070A)
- Crimson (#8A1C1C)
- Abyss Blue (#0A1F3F)
- Sickly Violet (#5A2A6A)

### FX & Animation

- Sonar distortion waves
- Dark fog clouds
- Pulsing red biolights
- Aggressive, twitchy movement

### UI Identity

- Blocky military font
- Red alert frames
- Harsh contrast
- Sonar-jammer effects on notifications

### Environmental Presence
