Units — Prototype roster

**Glossary:** See [Glossary](glossary.md) for authoritative term definitions (SIG, PF, HYD, PR, Resolution Tiers, Active Sonar, Silent Running, Echo Marks).

This is a prototype unit roster. Numbers are intent for prototyping; refine after playtests.

Light Scout (Pelagia-friendly)
- Role: Recon / map control
- SIG: 6 (very low)
- PR: 1 (shelf/mid-water)
- Speed: High
- Notes: Fragile, excels in kelp and thermal veins

Corvette
- Role: Strike / raiding
- SIG: 28
- PR: 2
- Speed: Medium-high
- Notes: Versatile, balances cost and noise

Cruiser
- Role: Fleet anchor
- SIG: 55
- PR: 2
- Speed: Medium
- Notes: Heavier sensors; produces SIG when active

Abyssal Submersible (Directorate)
- Role: Deep operations
- SIG: 22
- PR: 3 (abyssal-capable)
- Speed: Medium
- Notes: Born to depth; no refit required for abyssal pressure

Hadron Spire (Structure)
- Role: Projected depth access
- SIG: 80 when firing (directional)
- PR: 2
- Notes: Grants temporary PR+1 to nearby units via Sounding Spire resonance

Design notes
- All units list SIG and PR to clarify depth/noise interactions.
