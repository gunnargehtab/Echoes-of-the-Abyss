Echoes of the Abyss — World Overview

Setting

Echoes of the Abyss takes place in the Pelagion Rift, a vast underwater region formed after the Salinity Collapse destabilized global ocean chemistry. Humanity retreated into deep‑sea habitats, forming isolated city‑states, industrial mining colonies, and bio‑engineered enclaves.

Core Themes

Industrial underwater dystopia

Claustrophobic pressure zones

Bioluminescent ecosystems

Resource scarcity and political conflict

Abyssal trenches filled with hostile fauna

Biomes

Thermal Veins

Geothermal hotspots powering industrial operations. Bright magma glow, steam plumes, basalt formations.

Kelp Forest Plateaus

Bio‑rich regions dominated by algae, coral towers, and bioluminescent flora.

Abyssal Trenches

Pitch‑black pressure zones with jagged rock walls, sonar distortion, and abyssal creatures.

Resonance Fields

Crystal‑rich magnetic zones emitting violet light and sonic ripples.

Coral Ruins

Remnants of pre‑collapse cities overtaken by coral and algae.

Atmosphere & Tone

Deep, oppressive, industrial

Strong contrast between light and darkness

Bioluminescent highlights for readability

Fog layers to emphasize depth
