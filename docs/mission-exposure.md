# The Ledger 4 — Exposure

> The fourth mission of the Consortium campaign ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §4), specified.
> Written to the pattern [mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) sets and the Ledger
> documents continue: everything here is authored — the forces, the water, the beats, the
> numbers and the text — and code transcribes this document.

**Setting:** The First Trench's western margin — the shallow edge of the trench country, where
the Directorate's working ground meets water the Consortium can reach, 214 PC
([world-map.md](world-map.md)).

**Mission id:** `ledger-exposure` — namespaced by campaign, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §1.

---

## 1. What the Margin Is

The First Trench is the shallowest of the nine — the top step of the Directorate's country —
and its western margin is where their working economy comes nearest the surface world's reach:
rendering yards on the rim, freight screws on the axis, the whole industrial breath of a
society that publishes nothing. The margin's water carries it all, because the trench paints
its own rim: PF 1.6 down the worked ground, and three days of cohort economy standing in the
water as hum and residue for anyone with the ears and the nerve to come and read it.

Underwriter Baen Tull's problem is the reason anyone comes. The Board's war model prices the
Consortium, the Commune, and the Knights, because all three leave records the register can
buy. It does not price the Directorate — no debt, no wage, no published anything — and it does
not price the Mouth at all, and Tull's private conviction, two grades below the authority to
say it, is that a model missing those two rows is not a model
([characters.md](characters.md)). The Division cannot ask for the Mouth. It can ask for the
nearest measurable thing: what the deep's own stewards spend, and at what tempo, attending it.

So: a survey. Three quiet hulls, under the layer, along the margin, reading somebody else's
economy off the seabed. Deniable while it is unclassified. Priced the moment it is not.

**The layer is the door, both ways.** The margin lies below 1,200 m, so the survey works on
the Directorate's side of the thermocline — deaf to home, invisible from it, and alone with
the best ears in the game ([systems-echo.md](systems-echo.md) §3). Mission 2 taught the layer
as shelter. This mission walks through it and closes it behind you.

## 2. Whose Hulls the Player Commands

**The player commands the Division's field survey: three corvettes, stripped, silent-fitted,
and unarmed.**

Unarmed is the point, stated in the charter's own register: a deniable survey is an unarmed
one. A gun found on a chartered survey hull converts a filing dispute into an incident, and
the Division prices incidents. The player spends this mission with the two tools the campaign
has been rationing — silence and hearing — and none of the ones it taught first. The Klaxon
does not come down here. That is why the Division does.

Tull rides the lead hull. It is his model, his charter, and — although the mission never says
so — his father's water: Halvard is on the west wall to the north, at this water's own depth
([world-map.md](world-map.md) §3), and the paperwork said *exposure*
([characters.md](characters.md)).

**Engine bound, stated so nobody corrects it into a bug.** Three parties and a court slot: the
survey, the trench watch, and the worked ground itself — whose assets in the water are sounds.
The Drift is not a party.

## 3. The Survey

| Hull | Count | Stats | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Corvette, survey fit | 3 | SIG 28 / 28 · HYD 50 · PR-2 ([units.md](units.md)) | Fast enough to outrun anything the trench fields, ears enough to read residue (HYD above the mark's forty floor), and quiet enough that silence is a real state rather than a gesture |

### What the survey does not carry

1. **No weapons.** Struck at the charter, hardpoints and magazines both, with the reason on
   the panel: *struck — a deniable survey is an unarmed one*. The extraction, if it comes to
   one, is made of speed: a corvette outruns a trench cohort's hulls two to one, and the
   charter priced that ratio before it funded the fuel.
2. **No active sonar.** Carried — mission 3 put it on the manifest for good — and locked
   dark for this charter: *sealed under charter — a transmission is a signature*. One ping
   down here is Classification of the survey by every listener in the trench, instantly, at
   the survey's own invitation. The button the campaign just taught is the button this
   mission is about not pressing.
3. **No construction.** Nothing is built in somebody else's country.

**Silent Running is the working posture**, for the first time in the campaign — the button
mission 1 taught as a mistake and mission 2 taught as a berth trick is, in this water, simply
how the work is done. The survey creeps, listens, and moves between readings dark. What
silence costs here is time, and time is what the watch's patrol clock is made of.

## 4. The Tolerance

The system this mission teaches, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §2: **exposure as a budget**
— the player's own audibility, read as the enemy reads it, spent like the resource it is.

The instrument is the exposure readout ([systems-echo.md](systems-echo.md) §9): the snapshot
already tells a player how well the rest of the map resolves their force, and this mission is
built on that number the way mission 2 was built on the hum. The rule is the Division's, in
the charter, and it is cumulative:

**Thirty seconds of Classification or better, across the whole survey, for the whole
charter.** Not thirty seconds at a stretch — thirty seconds *total*, because the Directorate's
log is added up at the end of a watch, not watched, and so is the Division's
([mission-aptitude.md](mission-aptitude.md) §5 states the same procedure from the other side
of the Rift; the two organisations would be horrified to learn they account alike). Tier 1
and Tier 2 are free, all mission: a smudge is weather, a bearing is a fish. Classification is
an *entry*, and entries accumulate, and the ledger does not round down.

The arithmetic, so the player can do it at the table: against a trench watch's ears in open
margin water, a survey corvette under way is classified from roughly two kilometres; struck
silent, from under one; silent inside the hollow's vent ground, from under six hundred
metres. The watch walks its beat on its own clock. Every reading is therefore a placement
problem — where to stand, how loud, for how long — and the budget converts each mistake into
a number the player watches climb and cannot claw back.

### What spends at twenty, and what at thirty

The consequences are the survey's own procedure, authored ahead of time and fired by the
tally rather than by any clock — the first beats in the campaign that key on a *condition*
rather than a tick:

- **At twenty seconds entered: the warning.** The Division's guidance, read out once, in the
  register that can make "you have ten seconds of deniability left" sound like an actuarial
  table.
- **At thirty: the recall.** The charter is spent. Tull files the recall, the watch — which
  has now held Classification on something long enough to stop calling it weather — turns
  onto the survey's water, and the mission becomes an extraction. **This is not a mission
  failure** ([glossary.md](glossary.md), *Mission Outcome*): what was read is read, what was
  not stays a gap in the model, and the close prices both. A partial outcome is an outcome.

### The SIG budget

**SIG budget: 20** — the first genuinely quiet budget in the Ledger, and the campaign's
pattern inverted twice over: missions 1 and 3 budgeted a working loudness, mission 2 a
working hum, and this one budgets what the Consortium hates spending most, which is nothing.
The playtest adversary, for once, is the player who runs loud — the Klaxon instinct three
missions built, arriving in water that prices it.

## 6. The Readings

**Six points, authored along the worked ground, each a sound with a sentence behind it. Four
bound the model; the Division does not accept single sourcing and does not require a census.**

The mechanism is Attendance's, borrowed whole and aimed outward
([mission-attendance.md](mission-attendance.md) §6): resolve a point at Tier 2 or better while
it sounds, and the reading is *entered* — the counter is the objective, and the close reads
back what the model actually received, entered lines and gaps alike, in the transcript
arrangement. What the survey is reading, in the fiction, is the margin's living hum and its
standing residue — three days of economy, exactly as [systems-echo.md](systems-echo.md) §7
promises the Rift keeps records — authored as placed sounds because a mission owns its own
water (§13 carries the honest note on that).

| Point | What it is | What the model gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The rendering row | Biomass works on the rim, three shifts | The cohorts' protein floor, priced |
| The freight screws | The axis's haulage tempo | Logistics throughput — the number Tull calls the pulse |
| The intake stalls | Cohort induction, heard as maintenance | Demographic replacement rate, which the register prices and does not name |
| The draw plant | The margin's power breath | The energy budget of attendance itself |
| The listening dome | A Cantor's idle hum | What it costs to hear everything — the one expense the Directorate wears openly |
| **The sixth point** | **Not in the model** | Something below the yards returns the survey's own machinery noise, shifted, on a period no chart carries. The Division's guidance does not cover it. It is entered anyway |

The sixth point is the campaign turning, quietly, twelve minutes into its actuarial mission:
the first time a Ledger player hears the thing Item 9 is about, it is a line in somebody
else's economy that refuses to be one. The mission does not explain it
([culture.md](culture.md) §6). Tull enters it, because entering things is what he is for, and
the entry is the seed of everything he does in mission 7.

## 7. The Watch

**Two hulls, one beat, walked on the Directorate's clock — and the clock is published
nowhere, because the Directorate does not file.**

The watch is authored transits, the audit pair's arrangement without the courtesy: mission 2's
listener filed its plan because an audit is a document; this one is a *watch*, and its beat
must be learned by watching it, from cover, with the budget running. It walks the worked
ground's length, pauses where cohort procedure pauses, and hears exactly as well as
[units.md](units.md) says it does, which is better than anything else in the game.

- **Before the recall, the watch hunts nothing.** A smudge is weather; a bearing is a fish.
  The survey's entries accumulate in a log the watch itself will not read until the shift
  ends — the same cumulative procedure, running on both sides.
- **At the recall, the watch turns.** Thirty seconds of Classification is, under cohort
  procedure, no longer weather: the watch comes onto the survey's last classified water, and
  the relief is signalled early. It does not need to catch the survey. It needs the survey
  to leave, which is — under their law as under the Division's — the correct outcome for
  both parties.
- **At the watch change, everything gets worse.** The relief comes up the trench at 17:00
  with the rim pack riding its wake, loud, and the margin stops being empty water. The
  survey window was always the gap between two watches; the mission's clock is the
  Directorate's roster.

## 8. The Objective

**Take the readings. Come back over the layer with them.**

**The readings.** Four of the six points, entered — resolved at Tier 2 or better while they
sound. The counter is the objective, and it is on the panel from the first tick.

**The return.** The record crosses the layer in the survey's own hulls, held in duplicate
because the Division does not accept single sourcing: two of the three hulls home is the
record home. A reading unreturned is a reading unpriced, and the close treats a survey that
never comes back as exactly what the file would call it.

**The tolerance.** Thirty seconds, cumulative, at Classification or better — a budget, not a
fail state. Spent, it recalls the charter and turns the watch; unspent, it is the one line in
the file the Division reads twice.

### Results

| Result | Condition | The reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The interval closes** | Four or more points entered, and the record home in duplicate | "The model is bounded. Four columns close, the Division initials the interval, and the Board's arithmetic acquires the one property it has lacked, which is a floor. The sixth entry is filed under review. It will stay there." |
| **A gap in the model** | The record home, under four points | "The record returns. The interval does not close. What was read is priced; what was not is carried as assumption, flagged, and the flag is the Division's way of saying the word the register does not have." |
| **The survey is unpriced** | The record does not come home | "No record returns. The charter is written down, the water is marked unpriceable, and the Division notes that the two previous surveys of this class to return no record are both still carried as open files." |

Beneath whichever reading the run earns, the close enters the six points one by one — entered
or gap, each in its authored sentence — and the tolerance's own line, spent or unspent. The
epilogue is a page of Tull's model, assembled by play.

### The failure, and the sounds that precede it

The hard close is the watch change at 18:00, and it is audible the way this campaign makes
everything audible: the relief comes up the trench for a full minute before it arrives, with
the rim pack loud on its wake from 16:45 — seventy-five seconds of rising arrival in front of
the whistle ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §10). A survey still under the layer at the change is
in the next watch's water with a spent story, and the file has a reading for that too.

## 10. What It Teaches

One system, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10: **exposure as a budget** — the player's own
audibility read as the enemy reads it, accumulated, spent, and priced. Load-bearing by the
last five minutes: the final readings are taken with the budget nearly spent and the watch
change closing, and the player who managed the ledger badly takes them as an extraction under
recall.

What this mission deliberately does not teach:

- **Depth as a bleed** — the margin bottoms at the trench's rim and the survey is rated for
  every metre it visits. Mission 5 opens the crush ledger.
- **The Mouth** — the sixth point is heard, entered, and not explained. Explaining it is
  nobody's mission, in this campaign or any other ([culture.md](culture.md) §6).
- **Fighting the Directorate** — the survey cannot shoot and the mission never wants it to.
  The trench watch written from inside is [campaign.md](campaign.md) §6's business.

## 12. The Briefing

The survey charter, read at muster. Issued by the Risk & Actuarial Division, over Tull's
signature — the first Ledger briefing not signed for the Board, and the difference is
audible. The Consortium's register is defined in [culture.md](culture.md) §3.

> The Division requires field readings of the trench margin's working economy. Six points are
> charted. Four close the interval. The model this bounds is the one the Board's arithmetic
> stands on, and the Division observes, without further comment, that at present it stands on
> an assumption.
>
> The survey is chartered, unarmed, and deniable. Deniability is a consumable. It is spent by
> Classification: thirty seconds of it, cumulative, across the charter, at which point the
> charter is recalled and the survey's business becomes coming home. The first twenty seconds
> are yours to allocate. The last ten are the Division's, and the Division's guidance at
> thirty is unambiguous.
>
> Under the layer you are deaf to the concern and the concern is deaf to you. That is not a
> defect in the charter. It is the charter: what cannot hear you cannot be asked about you.
>
> The record returns in duplicate or it is not a record. Two hulls home is the interval; one
> is an anecdote; none is a file the Division has opened twice before and not yet closed.
>
> Exposure is budgeted. Nothing further is authorised. For the Division.

### Objective readings, in play

- *Take the readings. What is heard is entered, and enough closes the interval.*
- *Return over the layer in duplicate. A reading unreturned is a reading unpriced.*
- *Spend Classification like the consumable it is. The ledger runs from the first entry, and the Division does not round down.*

### The voices on the channel

**Underwriter Baen Tull — 01:00**

> The Board prices three navies and a hole. I am not down here because I like the water. I am
> down here because a model with a hole in it is a comfort, and the concern can no longer
> afford comfort at Board rates.

**Tull, on the sixth point — entered with it, and read back at the close, because an entry is
where an underwriter keeps his voice**

> Point six returns the survey's own machinery noise at us, shifted, on a period the model
> has no column for. It is entered. The model can be embarrassed later; the record cannot be
> taken twice. — B.T.

**The Division's guidance, at twenty seconds — fired by the tally**

> Twenty seconds of Classification are entered against the charter. Ten remain. The Division
> reminds the survey that the remainder is not a reserve; it is a margin, and margins are for
> arithmetic, not for spending.

**Tull, at the recall — fired by the tally**

> Thirty. The charter is spent and I am recalling it, which the record will show I did by the
> book and without editorial. Survey: home, in duplicate, at your best speed. The book has
> nothing further to say down here and neither do I.

Each line fails [culture.md](culture.md) §3 for the other three factions, which is that
document's own test (§6): Tull prices his own fear as a solvency problem, which no other
register would let him do; the guidance line turns ten seconds of remaining deniability into
a lecture on the difference between a margin and a reserve; and the recall is a man leaving
the most important water of his life on schedule, in writing, because the schedule is the
only authority he has ever been given.
