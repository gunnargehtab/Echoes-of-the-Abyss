# The Second Chord 6 — The Rim Deposits

> The sixth mission of the Knight campaign ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §7), specified and built —
> and the campaign's convergence slot ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §8), the first of the two the
> Knights take. One of the fourteen documents that complete the bible's campaign, written to the
> pattern [mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) sets and the Chord documents continue:
> everything here is authored — the forces, the water, the beats, the numbers and the text — and
> code transcribes this document.

**Setting:** The Rim — the Mouth's northern edge, 4,410 m below everything, on D+2 of the rim
week of 214 PC, the second tide after the concern's writ turned north
([world-map.md](world-map.md); [campaign.md](campaign.md) §8;
[mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) §1).

**Mission id:** `chord-rim-deposits` — namespaced by campaign after `chord-the-three`, per
[campaign.md](campaign.md) §1.

**Premise, for the entry that offers it:** *The crystal the Second Chord needs exists in exactly
one place, on an attended rim, under two nodes the Order raised to reach it, and the Choirmaster
authorises the plan.*

**This is the first mission in the bible that teaches projected depth access, and the last one
that could.** [mission-aptitude.md](mission-aptitude.md) §10 flagged the Sounding Spire's PR+1 as
a *campaign* gap rather than a mission one — the Knights' whole depth doctrine, and no mission in
[campaign.md](campaign.md) §7 teaching it — and [mission-standing-wave.md](mission-standing-wave.md)
§3 flagged it again and left it flagged, because a canyon whose floor the party can already reach
fills nothing by accident. This is the mission where the Order needs eight hundred metres it is
not certificated for, on ground somebody else is counting, with two nodes and no refit. The gap
closes here or it does not close.

## 1. What the Rim Is, From the Order's Side

The rim is the depression's edge, carrying the richest resonant-crystal deposits ever surveyed
([world-map.md](world-map.md); [mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) §1). The Order does not
want the rim. It wants what is under three of its faces, for one instrument, and its own
arithmetic has said for three years that there is nowhere else to get it — and the cycle is at
thirty-nine hours and shortening, which is why the arithmetic has a date on it
([campaign.md](campaign.md) §8; [world-map.md](world-map.md); [timeline.md](timeline.md), 213 PC).
This document never explains the Mouth, describes it, or approaches it
([culture.md](culture.md) §6): the lip is as far as the chart goes, as it was for the concern,
and the map declines to author the depression.

**The week, on the judge's calendar, and every rim document states it.** D was the concern's
survey and the plateaus' sowing under a bed on the western lip
([mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md); [mission-second-seeding.md](mission-second-seeding.md)).
D+1 was the Directorate's column walking the lip, with the Order present as Prospect's
reconnaissance and as a party of three hulls measuring the eastern faces from 1,750 m — no refit,
no Spires, nothing raised and nothing cut, and one sounding from above the line that the watch
entered and was not asked to answer ([mission-first-arrival.md](mission-first-arrival.md) §5, §9).
This is D+2. D+3 is the Chord ([mission-second-chord.md](mission-second-chord.md)).

Three facts about this water decide the mission, and none of them is a fence.

**The crystal is eight hundred metres under the Order's rating.** The terraces' floor is 2,600 m,
the Abyssal band begins at 1,800 m, and PR-2 is what a Knight hull is born with
([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §3). After *The Three* the Ninth has no refit crystal at
all, so the Order does not buy the metres the way the concern did — it projects them, out of two
Sounding Spires raised at this tide's turn, and pays in noise
([mission-the-three.md](mission-the-three.md) §3).

**The rim is attended, and the nodes were counted while they were still rising.** Two 150-second
sites at SIG 70 went up at the turn under a listening dome and twelve Choristers
([mission-first-arrival.md](mission-first-arrival.md) §11). Nothing here begins as a surprise;
what begins is a correction, and the Directorate's restraint on D+1 and its correction on D+2 are
one law applied to two different sets of facts.

**The lip carries and the terraces scatter, and the two are four hundred metres apart.** PF 1.60
on the whole southern kilometre against PF 0.70 on the crystal bench above it
([environments.md](environments.md)): everything the raid does is heard on the lip, and the lip's
own hulls stand 400 m below the ground they will walk up onto.

Nobody in this mission is wrong ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §2 rule 1). The rim is attended and a
node that stood into the watch is corrected. The Order needs the crystal and the crystal is here.
The plateaus are reading the rim for what could live on it and have already planted. It is the
ground the mission stands on, and nobody on the channel says it.

## 2. Whose Hulls the Player Commands

**The player commands the raid: six hulls in Order colours, PR-2, no refit, working under two
nodes the Order paid for with the last of its lattice money.**

Voice Ren Kalliso commands in the water. Choirmaster Ivane Sull authorises from the Ninth, and
the authorisation is the first sentence of the briefing: the raid plan that has been on her desk
since 214 PC opened, declined four times, is signed here ([characters.md](characters.md);
[campaign.md](campaign.md) §7). What changed is named once and not explained — the nineteen are
entered ([mission-nineteen.md](mission-nineteen.md) §2) and the window is shorter than the one she
wrote to the houses ([mission-the-three.md](mission-the-three.md) §12) — because until this tide
the Order would not spend itself at the rim with nineteen of it unentered. This is the line she
had not crossed, and this is the mission where she crosses it. She speaks once more, at 06:00, about a garden, and does not discuss it.

**Engine bound, stated so nobody corrects it into a bug.** Four parties and a court slot: the
raid on the player's slot flying `Faction.Hadron`; the plateaus on `Faction.Pelagia`; **one**
Directorate party carrying the watch, the 9th Trench Cohort, the dome and the twelve Choristers
together; and the rim's attendants, which carry a Directorate faction value because a party must
carry one and whose only assets in the water are two sounds that answer no classification
([mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) §5). The court slot is reserved and empty. The Drift
is not a party, and what rises at 14:30 rises from it.

The one party for everything Directorate is not tidiness. Every mission slot is hostile to every
other slot, so twelve armed Choristers on a slot of their own would open fire on the 9th's
submersibles standing 200 m from the nearest of them — and on the dome 447 m from it — and the
mission would spend its first minute manufacturing a Directorate civil war (§13).

## 4. The Rented Rating

The system this mission teaches, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §2: **the Abyssal round trip with
the Order's instrument inside it** — Prospect's descent, work and ascent, re-run with a rented
rating in place of a bought one ([mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) §4;
[economy.md](economy.md) §7).

1. **The node grants one rating, six hundred metres wide, to its own commander's hulls — and it
   sings at eighty for exactly as long as that grant is load-bearing.**
   `STRUCTURE_AURAS.SOUNDING_SPIRE` is 600 m and PR+1, measured horizontally, same slot only; the
   Spire is *active* when some allied hull inside it is genuinely below its own rating
   ([units.md](units.md): "80 when active"). So the raid is loud in exact proportion to how many
   of its hulls are alive underneath it, and it goes quiet the instant the last one climbs out.
   That is the tell the mission ends on and the reason the budget is 80.

   **And two nodes are not an interval.** They stand 1,154 m apart, well inside the 1,500 m the
   pairing rule spends, and they do not pair. That rule fires *at the moment a node completes*
   and asks for a node already standing, finished and unpaired inside the range
   ([mission-standing-wave.md](mission-standing-wave.md) §4) — and these two were raised
   together, at one turn of one tide, as two grants and not as a Standing Wave, so at the instant
   either of them completed there was no completed node beside it to take. The pairing is decided
   once and never re-decided, so it stays decided the way the turn decided it: no corridor, no
   line of sonic damage across the terraces the cutters work, no PF 2.00, and no Spire standing at
   eighty with nothing underneath it. The Order sites a corridor when it wants a corridor, and it
   did not want one here; [mission-conclave-chord.md](mission-conclave-chord.md) seats six
   prebuilt Spires on one party with pairs of them inside the same range, and they hum at 30 all
   tide for the same reason. Nothing pairs in the runtime today in any case — the pairing pass is
   unbuilt ([mission-standing-wave.md](mission-standing-wave.md) §13) — so this is a fact about
   the world the document has to state rather than a behaviour it has to suppress.
2. **The descent is heard by everything and the cut is heard longer.** 1,400 m to 2,600 m is
   1,200 m at 45 m/s — 26.7 seconds at a SIG floor of 72, Track to the dome-lifted ear from
   3,014 m through open water. Then five cuts of four minutes each at SIG 65 (Standard throttle
   45 plus the crystal premium 20, `HARVEST_THROTTLE` and `RESOURCE`), which through the
   terraces' 0.70 is Classification to that ear from 3,035 m. The descent announces; the cut
   moves in.
3. **The Directorate corrects the node, not the hull.** Six Choristers are 20 damage a second
   each at 450 m — 120 a second, and 1,800 HP of Sounding Spire is fifteen seconds of that. The
   tick the node falls, every Knight hull inside its 600 m is one band under its rating and takes
   `4 × 1²` = **4 HP/s**, unhealable: 105 seconds for a Corvette, 300 for the Voice. The way out
   is 800 m of climb at 15 m/s — 53.3 seconds, and the bleeding stops at the first metre above
   1,800 (§13) — or 554 m of walk east into node-two's six hundred, which a Corvette covers in
   6.5 seconds and pays 26 HP for. A deep raid must succeed, retreat, or die, and this is the
   mission where all three sentences are timed.
4. **The load rides the hull, and above the line the rating is the Order's own again.** A rigged
   load is monotone and a carrier that dies takes it down with it (`MissionLift`). The climb from
   2,600 m to the Staging line is 1,100 m and 73.3 seconds of silence, during which a loaded
   cutter is a bearing to a dome and nothing else can be done about it.

**The answer the mission does not hide.** The Voice's gun reaches 900 m and a Chorister's reaches
450, so three escort hulls at 143.3 damage a second take a wave of six — 1,200 HP — in 8.4 seconds
from water the wave cannot answer from. It costs noise (a Knight Corvette firing in the cone is
38, Classification to the dome-ear from 2,170 m), it costs time, and
[mission-nineteen.md](mission-nineteen.md) has already said what a lost hull costs. The twelve
arrive in two waves three minutes apart on the mission's clock.

### The SIG budget

**SIG budget: 80** — the node's own active figure, and the fifth reading the campaign has given
the same rule: the prologue's twenty is a ceiling, Asset Recovery's sixty-five a working level,
Attendance's eight a description, Aptitude's twenty-eight a direction, and this one is **the
instrument singing**. The Order cannot be under it while it works, because the number is emitted
by the party's own tool on the party's behalf and there is no throttle on it
([mission-standing-wave.md](mission-standing-wave.md) §3's finding, one mission on).

Playtested against two adversaries: the player who tries to cut quietly, and finds the node does
not know how; and the player who fights the cohort *under* the node rather than leaving with what
is already aboard, and discovers that the node is the thing being shot and the hulls are the
thing that dies when it stops.

Each load is a `MissionLift`: four minutes of held presence inside the region at a SIG floor of
65, progress paused while the hull is elsewhere and resumed when it returns, stopped outright by
Silent Running. Three cutters and five loads is deliberate arithmetic — **two of the three stand
on their face and take two loads off it** — and the two are not a sequence.
`MissionRuntime.applyLifts` walks the whole lift table on every pass and accrues each lift whose
carrier is standing inside its region, independently: one progress counter per lift id, and no
per-carrier bookkeeping anywhere. So `cutter-a` holding the fourth face accrues `load-one` and
`load-two` at the same time and rigs both at four minutes, and the fourth and fifth faces are one
hold each rather than two. The cut opens between 01:28 and 01:36 and all five loads are aboard by
about 05:30, with the first correction standing from 04:38: what the raid works under fire is the
last fifty seconds of a hold it has nearly finished, and not a second cut it chose to begin.

**Two loads together are not two cutters.** The keystone counts carriers and not loads (§8), so a
hull that comes home with both of a face's cuts still reads as one, and the Chord is still two
faces' work — which is why the table above spreads five loads over three hulls and why the
shortest honest route home is two of them. §13 records the finding, and what serialising a pair
would cost.

**And a rectangle is not a room.** A `MissionRegion` is a plan-view rectangle and the runtime's
lift test is two-dimensional, so the format cannot require a cutter to be *at* the crystal to
cut it: a player who holds 1,750 m over the faces rigs all five loads with the nodes idle at 30
and never enters Abyssal water at all. Nothing in the mission refuses that, and the document
declines to pretend otherwise — the descent is the fiction the beats and the briefing carry, the
node's own silence is the tell that nobody is under it, and §13 names the field that would make
the depth a condition rather than a courtesy.

**Where the correction stands, and why the node is what it shoots.** A scripted hull that is
moving holds its fire, and a stationary one takes the *nearest* live enemy inside its range
(`combat.ts`). The first wave stops between the two nodes, 180–335 m from node-one; from those
six points a cutter sitting on the fourth face is 400 m or more away and the node is nearer. The
second wave stops north of node-two, 150–269 m from it, on the water a loaded cutter climbs out
through; from there a cutter on the sixth face is 320–461 m away and the node is nearer again. So
the doctrine holds: the Directorate corrects the node. **A Knight hull that puts itself nearer
than the node is the thing corrected instead**, which is the raid's own geometry turned against
it: the fourth face's eastern edge is 150 m from one of the first wave's stops and the sixth
face's north-western corner is 100 m from one of the second's, and a cutter that works either
corner has volunteered.

**And the cutters shoot back whether or not the player says so.** Auto-acquire runs on any armed
hull that is stationary and not silent, and a hull cutting a face is stationary by definition, so
a cutter with a Chorister inside its 550 m opens fire on it — at SIG 38 in the cone, Classification
to the dome-lifted ear from 2,170 m — without an order. The escort's decision in §4 is whether to
answer a wave; the cutters' is already made by where the wave stopped.

**Nobody is wrong.** The cohort is correcting two structures that stood into a watch on ground it
has attended for two centuries; the raid is cutting the only crystal that exists for the only
instrument that matters. Both are true in the same 500 m rectangle, which is what a convergence is
([campaign.md](campaign.md) §8).

## 8. The Objective

**Bring the rim's crystal off the rim. Two loads above the line are the Second Chord; five are

the distance are the climb, the nodes going idle behind it, and the basin on the line — and a raid
that has all five aboard by 05:30 leaves nine minutes early, which is the same system read from
the other end: the instrument stops because there is nobody left under it.

Underneath it, the campaign's real subject: the Order is spent. *Aptitude* said six is a small
number to work with, *Nineteen* said six is a small number to have, *The Three* counted the
crystal once and found three Spires in it, and this mission spends two of the three and brings
home a number of hulls. The count at the close is the argument and no line in the mission says so.
