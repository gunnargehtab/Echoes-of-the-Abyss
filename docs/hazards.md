# Echoes of the Abyss — Environmental Hazards

## Geothermal Vent Eruptions

Periodic eruptions dealing AoE damage and knockback. Bright red/orange telegraphing.

## Toxic Brine Clouds

Green fog causing DoT, reduced visibility, and accuracy penalties.

## Abyssal Pressure Zones

Constant pressure damage, sensor failure, projectile slowdown.

## Kelp Entanglement Fields

Movement reduction, stealth bonuses, large-unit immobilization.

## Resonance Storms

Sonic shockwaves, magnetic interference, unpredictable projectile paths.

## Abyssal Creature Migration

Large creatures moving across fixed paths, causing heavy collision damage.

## Chemical Spill Zones

Severe DoT, armor degradation, explosive chain reactions.

## Cold Shock Currents

Movement slowdown, energy weapon penalties, hull integrity reduction.
