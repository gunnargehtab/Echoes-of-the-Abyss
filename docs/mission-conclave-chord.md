# The Second Chord 4 — Conclave

> The fourth mission of the Knight campaign ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §7), specified and since
> built. One of the fourteen documents that complete the bible's campaign, written to the pattern
> [mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) sets and the documents after it continue:
> everything here is authored — the forces, the water, the beats, the numbers and the text — and
> code transcribes this document.

**Setting:** the Third Chapter-House's outer formations, the shallow Resonance Fields on the east
slope, 214 PC, six tides after the committal at the Rest ([world-map.md](world-map.md);
[habitats.md](habitats.md) §5; [characters.md](characters.md)).

**Mission id:** `chord-conclave` — namespaced by campaign, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §1, which
is the whole reason it is a different mission from the Directorate's `attending-conclave`.

**Premise, for the entry that offers it:** *A concern comes to core the Third's outer formations on
the tide of an appointed interval, and the Chapter-Master with the standing to call the Order
together does not.*

**This is the third of the three missions [campaign.md](campaign.md) §2 rule 4 promises.** The
prologue's is an evacuation and [Thin Water](mission-thin-water.md)'s is a retreat; this one is the
refusal, and the rule's own sentence is now spent. The document earns the label by arithmetic rather
than by claiming it — §4 states the hull totals and the two guns' ranges, and the fight is lost in
those figures before anybody fires. What is winnable is a Chapter-Master not doing a thing, made
physical.

## 1. What an Interval Is

A **conclave is not a gathering** ([habitats.md](habitats.md) §5). Nine houses cannot convene: the
houses are instruments and do not travel, and a chapter that crossed the Fields to sit in a room
would be spending the one thing it has none of. A conclave is *sounded*. A chapter-master strikes
their own house's chord where they stand and the lattice carries it — and the lattice is the outer
formations, the same six a tuning party corrects once a year
([mission-aptitude.md](mission-aptitude.md) §1). **The annual sounding is not housekeeping. It is
what keeps the Fields able to hold a conclave at all.**

A call is a struck chord at an **appointed interval**, and the count is the sequence the nine
answer in. A house that lets its interval pass has abstained, and everybody hears the abstention,
because the Fields were listening at that moment for exactly that. A Knight's silence is a formal
concept and not an absence ([culture.md](culture.md) §3); a conclave is where that distinction
acquires consequences.

**Standing is maintenance.** A chapter-master may call if their house's lattice is in tune this
year — if the outer formations will carry a struck chord as a *call* rather than as *drift*.
Standing cannot be bought and can be lost by neglect. The Third has it because its wrights have kept
six formations corrected for thirty years and a party of six hulls sounded all six of them this year
([mission-aptitude.md](mission-aptitude.md) §8, the first two readings). **Chapter-Master Halden
Vrey has the standing and does not use it** ([characters.md](characters.md)), and not calling is not
nothing here: he is heard not calling, at every interval where the Third could have been.

Three facts about this water decide the mission.

**The lattice is a thing that can be cut.** Since the tuning the concern's survey has filed, and the
outer Fields are now a coring field in the Board's registry. [Aptitude](mission-aptitude.md) §12's
*"we have never written down that this water is ours and they have written down that it is not"* has
become *they have written down that it is theirs*. A works column comes to core three formations on
the tide of an appointed interval, and a house whose formations are broken has no voice in the next
count.

**The room is off this map.** A chord is struck in the hall at the house's heart — the one room
where nothing is struck and nothing is spoken, of which a conclave is the single exception. The
Third's chord is a kilometre and a half east of the Approach and this rectangle does not hold it.
**So Vrey's not-striking happens in a room the party never hears**, which is the mission's shape as
much as its subject: the player is the physical form of an omission, and the omission is inaudible
by rule and by geometry.

**The interval is at 14:00 and lasts sixty seconds.** It is not a deadline the mission invents: it
was appointed before the concern filed anything, the Order will be listening at it whatever is
standing, and it passes at its own time whether or not anybody is alive to be quiet in it.

## 2. Whose Hulls the Player Commands

**The player commands the same six hulls under Voice Ren Kalliso that tuned this lattice a year
ago, put back on the same ground for the opposite reason.**

Kalliso is thirty-one, the Order's best field officer and its most ordinary person
([characters.md](characters.md)). Six tides ago she read nineteen names over a trench floor she
could not reach ([Nineteen](mission-nineteen.md)); she said them aloud once and does not say them
here. She speaks exactly once, to nobody, as she does in every Chord mission. If the player strikes
the lattice **it is her hull that struck it**, and §8 prices that as the Voice calling a conclave in
the Chapter-Master's name without his word.

Chapter-Master Vrey is on the chapter channel from the house and does not command the party: he sets
the interval, says one thing at 08:00 that is the mission and one at 13:30 that is the last
instruction, and reads the interval at the close. Choirmaster Ivane Sull is on the lattice at the
Ninth and not in the water; she says one word at 14:00 whatever is standing, and one sentence at
14:30 that sends the campaign to the First and then to the rim.

**Engine bound, stated so nobody corrects it into a bug.** Two parties and a court slot: the Third's
party on the player's slot carrying the Hadron faction value, the works column carrying the
Bathyarch value, and the court slot empty as every literal reserves it. The six formations are
**structures on the player's own party**, which is what makes them shootable and what makes them the
player's to lose. The Drift is not populated: `fauna` is off and no creature is authored.

name.

### What the party does not carry

1. **Torpedoes are struck**, for arithmetic rather than doctrine. Six launchers with a magazine of
   two apiece is twelve shots at 700 damage ([systems-combat.md](systems-combat.md) §5) — 8,400
   against 4,500 hit points of column, delivered from a 3,200 m run against a 900 m gun. A party
   with torpedoes sinks both Cruisers before either stands, and [campaign.md](campaign.md) §2 rule
   4's label becomes false. The reason attached is the Order's own: *nothing is launched on the
   tide of an interval*.
2. **Construction is locked** — *nothing is raised in a house's water on the tide of an interval*.
   The lattice is prebuilt and the Order builds nothing here, so the lock costs nothing and says
   something.
3. **Active sonar is available**, handed over in [Nineteen](mission-nineteen.md) per
   [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10's mission-3 rule and not taken back. It is priced rather than
   fenced: a ping is SIG 95 omnidirectional, and at 14:00 that is heard by the whole east slope and
   read by the ceiling. It is a stroke in the Order's register and **not** in the format's — it does
   not feed the `sound` tally — so it costs `the-quiet`, which is a reading, and not `the-rest`,
   which is a keystone. §8 says so under both rows, and §13 prices what that leaves unpriced.
4. **Silent Running is present and, for the first time in this campaign, right.**
   [Aptitude](mission-aptitude.md) §4 priced it out — a cruising Knight pointed away is quieter than
   a silent Knight pointed at you — and that arithmetic has not changed. What has changed is that
   the mission asks for a *scalar* and not a bearing: the Voice idles at 55, the ceiling is 28, and
   no angle fixes that. Under silence the Voice sits at 3–8 ([systems-echo.md](systems-echo.md) §6),
   weapons disabled, at 24.75 m/s. **The refusal is the button**, and it is the one that turns the
   guns off.

## 4. The Interval

The system this mission teaches, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §2: **nothing new**, which is
[campaign.md](campaign.md) §7's own word for this row. Every number below has been taught already —
[Aptitude](mission-aptitude.md)'s facing, [Standing Wave](mission-standing-wave.md)'s node,
[Nineteen](mission-nineteen.md)'s count of hulls — and all this mission does is turn the three of
them to face the player at once. Four movements.

**1. The lattice is standing, and standing is nine and a half seconds each.** A formation is 1,800
hit points. Three cutter Corvettes at 50 damage on a 1.2 s cycle put down **125 a second**; the
escort Cruiser at 150 on a 2.5 s cycle puts down 60, and 67.2 under the Klaxon while it emits 85
(+12 % above SIG 60). Together that is **192.2 a second, and a formation lasts 9.4 seconds** once
the column stands over it; three cutters alone take 14.4. **So the defence is not at the cut. It is
at the column's legs**, and the legs are in §5 with their arrival times worked out.

**2. Facing is still the only cover, and it decides who files you, never who shoots you.** The
figures are [Aptitude](mission-aptitude.md) §4's, unchanged and not restated: against the escort's
HYD 65 in PF 0.70 water a Knight Corvette is classified at **1,414 m bow-on, 734 m beam-on, 335 m
stern-on**, and a sounding at 80 at 2,726 / 1,414 / 646. Then the sentence that makes this mission
different from mission 1: **auto-fire does not consult a tier.** A gun takes the nearest live hostile
inside its range on [systems-combat.md](systems-combat.md)'s own licence — weapon ranges sit inside
the distance at which a combat hull is audible, so *in range implies heard* — and a Knight Corvette
500 m from a cutter is shot at whether it is bow-on or stern-on. **The doctrine decides whether the
concern files you**, the tolerance and Bramm at thirty seconds, and nothing else. A player arriving
from [Aptitude](mission-aptitude.md) with the flank as a habit learns that a habit is not a shield.

**3. The fight is lost, in figures, before it starts.** From 09:00:

| | Hull | Damage a second | Gun |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The column, from 09:00** | 2 × 1,200 + 5 × 420 = **4,500** | 2 × 67.2 + 5 × 41.7 = **342.7** | **900 m** |
| **The party** | 1,200 + 5 × 420 = **3,300** | 60 + 5 × 41.7 = **268.3** | **550 m** |

Concentrated in sequence, the party is gone in **9.6 seconds** and the column in **16.8**. A Knight
Corvette under the escort and two cutters — 150.5 a second — lasts **2.8 seconds**. The escort under
all six Knight guns lasts 4.5, and that figure is unreachable, because reaching it means crossing
**350 metres of a Cruiser's fire before a Corvette's gun bears at all**. Torpedoes are struck (§3),
and the column stops to work, which means it also stops to shoot: a scripted hull under a move order
holds its fire (§13), so the column is a target while it walks and a gun when it stands, and §5's
legs are authored so that the column is never walking whole: the escort's three walks are covered by
three standing cutters, the cutters' three by a standing Cruiser, and from 09:27 the relief stands
over the Drone and does not move again.

**Six of you, six voices, and this time they are one number.** A hull lost in that fight is a voice
the Third cannot stand at 14:00. [Aptitude](mission-aptitude.md) §12's *"if I have to choose between
them, I have chosen"* was rhetorical; here the two counts are welded, which is
[Nineteen](mission-nineteen.md)'s arithmetic charged to a house.

**4. The rest.** At 14:00 the Third is **quiet — twenty-eight across the party, the Voice under
silence — and the tone on the Voice's hull is not struck.** Both halves are refusals, both are
buttons the player has carried since mission 1, and nothing else is asked in the last thirty
seconds. *A rest is written down, it is played, and it is not an absence* — the Order's own sentence
for a silence somebody meant ([culture.md](culture.md) §3;
[mission-aptitude.md](mission-aptitude.md) §8) — and this mission is that sentence with a clock on
it.

### The SIG budget

**SIG budget: 28**, and it is the campaign's fifth reading of the same rule. The prologue's twenty
is a ceiling, Asset Recovery's sixty-five a working level, Attendance's eight a description, and
[Aptitude](mission-aptitude.md)'s twenty-eight is *in the cone* — a figure with a bearing attached
and no meaning without one. **This twenty-eight is that same integer read as a flat scalar**,
because the Order is not being heard *by* somebody at a bearing: it is being listened for, at an
appointed moment, by eight houses at once, and direction is no help against the whole compass.
Twenty-eight is a Knight Corvette at cruise, so five hulls clear it by standing still or by moving;
the Voice does not clear it at all and has to be silenced.

It is **not** a silence order in the format's sense — no `arrayTag`, so the ledger never runs and
nothing is withdrawn ([mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) §4) — but an objective the party
meets at the close or does not, read out rather than ranked (§8). Playtested against a player who
fights the column at 14:00 and finds out the two counts were one, and against a player who strikes
the Drone at 06:00 to hear what happens: the other way to lose, and the more interesting one.

they carry. The six points are [Aptitude](mission-aptitude.md) §6's to the metre, because they are

Read the last row against [Aptitude](mission-aptitude.md) §6: *a drone is the sustained bottom note
the other voices are tuned against*, so a lattice missing its Descant has a thin top and **a lattice
missing its Drone is not a lattice**. It is the same keystone the tuning had, now standing in a
Cruiser's fire.

**The lattice is the party's armour, and this is emergent rather than authored.** A gun takes the
*nearest* live hostile in range, and a Sounding Spire is a hostile: position, owner, hull points, and
nothing in the auto-acquire loop excludes a structure (§13). Across the three authored stops a
standing formation is 150–250 m from its cutters, and a Knight hull in the same cell is 250–650 m off
in plan and another 250 m above them — **the column shoots the formation and not the hull.** The tick
a formation comes apart, the hull standing its voice becomes the nearest thing in the water.
Defending the lattice and standing the voices are the same act, by arithmetic, and nothing has to
say so.

**The two hundred and fifty metres between the Approach and the Fields is a term nobody has spent,
either.** Gun range is measured in three dimensions while detection is horizontal
([systems-combat.md](systems-combat.md) §4), and the column works at 1,700 m over ground the party
crosses at the Approach's 1,450: a hull that does not descend keeps **490 m of a cutter's 550 and
865 m of the escort's 900**, and every cell figure in the table above is a plan distance. It costs
nothing — nothing crushes here and no predicate reads depth — and it is not free to give up, because
a dive is 45 m/s at a SIG floor of 72 ([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §2) and the ceiling at
fourteen is twenty-eight.

**And interposing does not work, which the player finds out early and cheaply.** A Corvette that
sets out at 00:00 stands at the Bass at 00:43, nearly two minutes before the cutters arrive. It is
then the nearest hostile: 125 a second, 420 hit points, **3.4 seconds** — 2.2 once the escort adds
67.2 at 03:18. That is the opening lesson and it costs a fifth of the Third's voice.

## 7. What Is Heard

**The Fields ring, a works order arrives on a channel, and the first thing the Third learns it learns
about itself.**

The Fields ring for the reason they always ring ([world-map.md](world-map.md)), and this tide six of
them are the player's own, humming at 30 apiece because their depth grant is inert (§3). To the

Six markers, one per voice, shipped only from 13:30 because a marker travels with the objective that
names it — the Drone's reads *The Drone. The chord does not certify without it.* **Four things a
reader should be able to check:**

**Revealed at 13:30, and not scored before it.** `revealAtTick` withholds an objective from the panel
*and* from derivation ([Intake](mission-intake.md) §9), which is the latch this mission wants: a hull
that happened to be at the Bass at 04:00 has not stood the Bass at the interval. A `say` beat shares
the tick, as the format requires.

**But an `extract` latches, and the document will not pretend otherwise.** Only `quiet` and `survive`
are standing; a met `extract` is never re-derived. So each voice row reads *a hull of the party was
inside this cell at some tick between 13:30 and the close* — sixty seconds of window, not sixty
seconds of standing, and a Corvette that clips the Alto's corner at 85 m/s has stood the Alto.
**That is a weaker sentence than §6's and it is the one the format can carry**; §13 holds the row
that would carry the stronger one, and the objective text tells the player what to do rather than
promising what is measured.

**`the-rest` is failed by a conditional beat, not by a predicate, and its clock is authored a tick
short of the close.** No predicate can say *nothing has happened*, so the row is an `endure` run out
over the mission's whole length and a conditional beat on `{ kind: 'sound', count: 1 }` sets it
`Failed` the moment the first sounding completes. The tick order holds it in both directions:
conditional beats fire after every tally and immediately before derivation, derivation skips a
`Failed` row, and the `resolve` is deferred until after derivation on its own tick. A tone completed
on the 14:30 pass therefore fails the rest before the endure could read Met, and an endure due on the
resolve pass reads Met beneath a conclusion. **Due on the resolve pass, and not on the resolve tick,
which is not the same figure**: an `endure`'s clock is stamped the first time the runtime derives the
objective, which is one mission tick — twelve sim ticks — after zero, so a row authored at the round
`T(14, 30)` comes due one pass *after* the close and never reads Met in any tide the mission can have.
Authored at `T(14, 30) - 12` it comes due on the close's own pass, which is where the argument above
needs it. The offset is arithmetic and not taste, and §13 files it as the thing the per-mission test
has to hold. **The tally is monotone from tick zero and has no window**, which the document prices
rather than patches: a formation sounded at 05:00 is a call at 05:00, and a stroke nobody appointed
is worse than one somebody did. **And the tally counts soundings and nothing else.** `sound` is a
count of the authored `soundings` this party has completed and of no other emission the party makes,
so a ping — SIG 95 omnidirectional, the loudest thing six Knight hulls can do — is not a sounding,
cannot complete one, and cannot fail the rest. That is right in the register as well as in the code:
the rest is *nothing is struck in the Third's name*, and a ping calls nothing and is entered against
nobody. It is loud, and loud is the other row.

**The ceiling is a party figure, and the Spires do not breach it.** `quiet` reads the peak over the
hulls carrying the role and never the player's whole slot — [mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md)
§4's argument about the court's own array, reached from the other side. Six formations humming at 30
would otherwise fail a ceiling of 28 from tick zero, in the one mission where that is fatal to the
subject. **A ping does breach it**, and is the only thing in the party's hands that breaches it on
purpose: an active ping puts the hull at 95 for three seconds ([systems-echo.md](systems-echo.md)
§5), which is the ceiling three and a half times over at the one moment eight houses are listening
for exactly this house. `the-quiet` is where that lands and the only row it lands on — and
`the-quiet` is a reading rather than a rung, which is a decision §13 makes and defends, and §13 is
also where the document says plainly what that decision leaves unpriced.

## 10. What It Teaches

**Nothing new**, which is [campaign.md](campaign.md) §7's own entry for this row and is a design
position rather than a shortfall. Three missions have handed the player three things and this one
asks for all three inside sixty seconds:

- **Facing** ([Aptitude](mission-aptitude.md)) — which decides whether the concern *files* you and
  nothing at all about whether it shoots you, because auto-fire does not consult a tier. A doctrine
  has a domain, and this is where the player finds its edge.
- **The node** ([Standing Wave](mission-standing-wave.md)) — a Sounding Spire is an instrument with
  hull points. Mission 2 taught it as something you raise; this is the first mission where the
  player *has* six and where somebody else can take nine and a half seconds over one.
- **The count of hulls** ([Nineteen](mission-nineteen.md)) — six hulls, the tide after the Order
  entered its dead. This mission names nobody and does not need to: it welds the hull count to the
  voice count and lets the player do the subtraction.

Underneath, what the mission is actually for: **an omission with a mechanism under it.** Vrey has the
standing and will not use it, and the player is what that decision looks like from outside — six
hulls at six points, under a ceiling, with the one loud thing they own left alone. A refusal is
usually a line of dialogue; this one has an objective table, a keystone, and a way to fail it by
being helpful. And it is played on ground the player has already worked: a year ago this lattice was
an examination, this tide it is the Third's vote, and it can be lost to nine and a half seconds of
somebody else's works order. **Standing is maintenance** ([habitats.md](habitats.md) §5), and the
campaign has now shown both halves of that sentence.

What this mission deliberately does not teach:

- **The Order's crystal, counted** — mission 5, [The Three](mission-the-three.md), where the ledger
  is stated once and the two rim documents cite it.
- **Projected access — depth the Order rents rather than owns** — mission 6,
  [The Rim Deposits](mission-rim-deposits.md), closing the campaign-level gap
  [Aptitude](mission-aptitude.md) §10 and [Standing Wave](mission-standing-wave.md) §3 both flagged.
  This map cannot teach it: every metre is Mid-Water and every hull PR-2, so the grant says nothing.
- **Resonance Collapse, and the emission** — mission 7,
  [The Second Chord](mission-second-chord.md), where the sounding this mission asks the player to
  refuse is held for thirty seconds at a hundred.
