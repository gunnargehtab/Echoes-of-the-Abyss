# The Second Chord 5 — The Three

> The fifth mission of the Knight campaign ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §7), specified and now
> built — and one of the fourteen documents that complete the bible's campaign. Written to the
> pattern [mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) sets and the four Chord documents
> before it continue: everything here is authored — the forces, the water, the beats, the
> numbers and the text — and the code that transcribes this document moved no figure in it
> (§13).

**Setting:** the First Chapter-House, 2,900 m, at the Fields' southern edge where crystal country
breaks toward the trenches, 214 PC, the tide after the conclave interval
([world-map.md](world-map.md) §3; [habitats.md](habitats.md) §5; [characters.md](characters.md)).

**Mission id:** `chord-the-three` — namespaced by campaign after `chord-conclave`, per
[campaign.md](campaign.md) §1.

**Premise, for the entry that offers it:** *Twelve minutes at 2,900 m: the Choirmaster is taken to
the First, where the Chord still stands and three people who have not spoken since 178 PC are still
writing.*

**This is the mission where Silent Running stops being cover and becomes a courtesy.**
[mission-aptitude.md](mission-aptitude.md) §4 priced it out by arithmetic — a cruising Knight
pointed away is quieter than a silent Knight pointed at you.
[mission-standing-wave.md](mission-standing-wave.md) §3 said it was worse than wrong, because the
two loudest things in that mission had no throttle to pull.
[mission-conclave-chord.md](mission-conclave-chord.md) §3 is where it first comes right, and it
comes right as a refusal — thirty seconds of a scalar no bearing fixes, at the end of a fight. Here
it is the whole mission, held for eight minutes in a room where the courtesy is absolute, and the
reason it is correct this time has nothing to do with hiding. Nobody in this water is hunting
anybody. The hush is a thing the Order owes a house, and this is the first time the format has been
asked to price a courtesy rather than a cover.

## 1. What the First Is

The deepest of the nine chapter-houses, at 2,900 m, where the Resonance Fields break toward trench
country ([world-map.md](world-map.md) §3). The Order founded deep and grew shallower, so the First
stands nearest the Mouth, which is the Order's whole doctrine in one architectural fact
([habitats.md](habitats.md) §5). The **First Chord** was raised here in 178 PC and aimed down the
trench axis — the one alignment in the Rift where PF 1.60 water carries a transmission that far
([environments.md](environments.md)) — and it transmitted, and the reply arrived forty-one seconds
before it should have ([timeline.md](timeline.md)). Three technicians never regained speech. They
are alive, at 2,900 m, and they have not stopped writing.

The house is three rooms; this mission enters two of them and hears the third. **The Chord** still
stands, still aimed, and — the fact the Order does not say out loud — still *tuned*: the house's
wrights have kept it playable every season since 178, which makes it not a monument and not a ruin
but an instrument in working order that nobody is permitted to sound. **The hospice** is where
somebody is kept in tune who can no longer sound: three cells opening off the chord, attended by
apprentices who share the Three's hours and transcribe. **The sealed room** is thirty-six years of
writing in three hands, a case a season, dated and stacked in a cut dry room, and the Choirmaster
reads there alone ([habitats.md](habitats.md) §5; [factions.md](factions.md)).

Choirmaster Ivane Sull was one of those apprentices. She was in the room in 178 PC, at ten years
old, a year past her own test at nine; she kept her voice and does not know why
([characters.md](characters.md); [factions.md](factions.md), entry at nine). She is forty-six in
214, so she has been coming back for thirty-six years to a room she has never entirely left, and
the document says that once, here, and does not say it again.

**Three facts about this house decide the mission, and not one of them is a threat.**

**Nothing in it is broken.** An aimed instrument in tune, a dry archive, three attended people, and
a house whose wrights corrected the lattice this season regardless of what the Chapter-Master who
built it now wants ([habitats.md](habitats.md) §5). There is no hostile party, no fauna — below
2,700 m the column is empty of animals entirely, which is the Mouth's business and not the Drift's
([bestiary.md](bestiary.md) §4) — no hazard, no crush and no timer that fails. The mission's whole
job is to let the player stand in a house where nothing is broken and nothing is safe.

**It is entered by diving, four times, deeper each time.** The Fields' foot falls to 2,700 m and
stops; the way in is roofed at 2,600, the chord at 2,700, the hospice at 2,750 and the sealed room
at 2,800. Roofed water is water nobody reaches by accident ([habitats.md](habitats.md) §8), and
descent is 45 m/s at a SIG floor of 72 ([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §2). Every door in this
house is an order somebody gave, and the house hears every one of them.

**Its silence is the hush, and the hush is the room.** The chord is the one hall in a chapter-house
where nothing is struck and nothing is spoken, so that the crystal can be heard settling
([habitats.md](habitats.md) §5). A Knight's silence is a formal concept and not an absence
([culture.md](culture.md) §3). A scene set in a hush is quiet or it is a shove
([habitats.md](habitats.md) §10), and this mission is the first in the bible to put the player's own
throttle inside one.

The Order does not discuss the First. What follows is the building's account of itself and not a
Knight's.

## 2. Whose Hulls the Player Commands

**The player commands the Choirmaster's escort: four hulls at the foot of the Fields, one of which
is her.**

**Choirmaster Ivane Sull is in the water, aboard, for the first time in the campaign.** She has been
a voice from the Ninth since [mission-standing-wave.md](mission-standing-wave.md) and a sentence at
the close of [mission-conclave-chord.md](mission-conclave-chord.md) — *"Heard. — The window is what I
wrote to the houses. It is shorter now."* — and this is the first Knight briefing spoken from inside
the party rather than from a house. Her hull is the mission's tender: it does not move without ears,
it is unarmed, and it carries the only person permitted to open the season's case.

**Voice Ren Kalliso commands the escort**, as she has commanded every Knight party the campaign has
fielded. She has never stood in the First. She speaks once, to nobody, in the chord, which is what
she does in every Chord mission and the only mission in which the room she says it in is the point.

**Chapter-wright Aldis Fenn speaks for the house, four times** — three on the clock and once when
the Choirmaster reaches the room. B. 2,900 m, sixty-eight, one of the First's five surnames, forty
years on the Chord and he has never left the Fields — the same face [habitats.md](habitats.md) §5
gives the Third in Anselm Vrey, given here to the deepest house because somebody has to say the
times aloud. He is the Order's register at its most archaic: he enters times and refuses to enter
meanings, and outside his mouth this document does the same.

**Chapter-Master Vrey is not in this mission**, and **the Three do not speak and are not shown.**
Vrey built the First Chord's lattice with his own hands and wants it shut down
([characters.md](characters.md)); the house corrected it this season anyway, and Fenn says so at
03:00 without knowing or caring that it is an argument. The Three's cells sound, faintly, and what
they write is never read out — not in this document, not in a line, not in a reading
([habitats.md](habitats.md) §5; [culture.md](culture.md) §6).

**Engine bound, stated so nobody corrects it into a bug.** Two parties and a court slot. The
player's party carries the Knight faction value on the player's slot; **the First is a second Knight
party on a second slot**, seated by the mission, because an emitter on the player's own party is one
the player can never hear (`missions.test.ts`) and the house has to be audible. Hostility in this
engine is `Owner.slot`, so those two parties are formally enemies; nothing on either side can fire —
the house has no hulls and the player's four carry no `armed` flag and have weapons locked besides —
and §13 carries the row rather than leaving a reviewer to find it. The Drift is not a party: no
fauna are seeded and none is authored.

### The refit, and the Order's crystal

All four hulls are **PR-3 by refit**, authored per hull as `MissionUnit.pressureRating` — the
Hadron baseline is PR-2 and every metre of water this party occupies is Abyssal, so without the
certificates the mission would be four hulls bleeding four points a second in their own house
([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §1, §3; `echo.ts`, `crushAttritionPerSecond`). The Order
**projects** access and buys instant refits paid in Resonance ([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md)
§3), and this is the one document in the campaign that counts what that costs.

**The ledger, stated once, here.** At the top of this tide the Ninth holds **600 crystal** — the
whole of the Order's domestic Resonance, cut at 2.2× everyone's yield per node out of the Fields'
thin veins ([economy.md](economy.md) §6), and already short by what the Fifth's two nodes were paid
out of it. A PR-3 certificate is **60 crystal a hull** — half a Spire, because a certificate is a
Spire's grant fitted to one hull instead of to six hundred metres of water. **A certificate is cut
for a descent and is spent on it** — the Resonance is consumed in the fitting and the rating goes
back to the roster's at the close — which is why the Order has four PR-3 hulls this morning and none
at the rim. So:

- Four certificates, 4 × 60 = **240**, spent this morning.
- **360 left.** A Sounding Spire costs 120 crystal beside its 750 nodules
  (`STRUCTURE_STATS[SoundingSpire]`), so 360 is **three Spires and not a fourth**.
- The nodules for those three are the stipend's, not the ledger's: 750 apiece at 1.0 nodule/s is
  twelve and a half minutes each ([economy.md](economy.md) §6).

**After this tide the Order has no refit crystal and reaches depth only by projection.** That is the
arithmetic that sends [The Rim Deposits](mission-rim-deposits.md) to the rim under two raised nodes
rather than under four more certificates, and it is why [The Second Chord](mission-second-chord.md)
can say *the last* of exactly one Spire. Both rim documents cite this paragraph and neither restates
it, per the campaign's own rule that a budget is stated once. The 60 is new canon and TUNABLE; the
120 and the 750 are the roster's.

### What the escort does not carry

All seven of `MissionAbility`'s names are locked, each with the house's reason attached for the HUD
([ui-ux.md](ui-ux.md) §7). Three literals lock all seven — the prologue (`sorrowgate.ts`),
[First Arrival](mission-first-arrival.md) (`firstArrival.ts`) and this one — so the count is not
the argument; the pairing is. This mission and the prologue are the same argument at opposite ends
of the campaign: a room where nothing is aimed. The rim's seven are a different sentence — the rim
is attended, not asked, and not lied to — which is why the pair that says anything here is still
the prologue's.

1. **Weapons, torpedoes, mines, depth charges, noisemakers** — *nothing in the First is armed, and
   nothing in it is aimed but the Chord.* The hulls carry no `armed` flag either, so the lock is the
   second half of a statement the spawn already makes.
2. **Active sonar** — *a ping in the chord is a stroke, and nothing is struck in the chord.* The
   button has been in the player's hands since [Nineteen](mission-nineteen.md)
   ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §10), which is what makes withholding it here a courtesy rather than a
   rung: this is the first Knight mission that takes the ping away after giving it.
3. **Construction** — *the First is finished.* There is nothing to build with in any case: the
   ledger above is prose, `startingNodules` is 0, and the map carries no resource field.

**Silent Running is present, and here it is the whole mission rather than its last thirty seconds.**
§4 is the whole of why, and the arithmetic is short: a Knight Corvette idles at 28 and the Voice at
55, the hush's ceiling is 8, and the button is the only thing on this map that gets under it — a
Corvette to **5.3** and the Voice to **7.6**, because `silentRunningSig` places a hull inside the
3–8 band by its own idle figure rather than at the band's ceiling (`acoustics.ts`). Four tenths of a
point of margin on the loudest hull the row measures, and no other way to buy it.

## 4. The Hush

The system this mission teaches, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §7's own row, is **nothing at all**.
It is the campaign's one mission with no new mechanism in it, and the document says so plainly
rather than smuggling one in. What it does instead is hold one built button down for the whole
twelve minutes, in four movements.

**1. Silent Running is the courtesy, and the ceiling is 8.** `SILENT_RUNNING` puts a hull between 3
and 8 SIG, costs 45% of its speed and disables its weapons, and cannot be locked (`constants.ts`,
`SILENT_RUNNING`). A Knight Corvette that simply stops emits 28; the Voice idles at 55. Under the
button they emit **5.3** and **7.6**: `silentRunningSig` scales the band by the hull's own idle
figure — `3 + 5 x min(1, sigIdle / 60)` (`acoustics.ts`) — so only a hull idling at 60 or more ever
reads the eight. The hush's predicate is `quiet` at **ceilingSig 8**, which is a number no hull on
this map can reach any other way — the first ceiling in the bible that is not a discipline but a
mode. The prologue's twenty was a ceiling the flight could hold at cruise; the galleries'
twenty-five was a written order a Submersible clears by idling; twenty-eight at the Third was a
Corvette's own cruise figure. Eight is the button, and the Voice keeps it by four tenths of a point.

**2. The hold is the escort, and it puts them where the room is.** `escortRadiusM` is **600 m**: the
`'tender'` moves only while an `'escort'` is inside that radius, measured horizontally, and its
orders are cleared every pass it is not (`runtime.ts`, `applyEscortHold`). The sealed room is a
500 × 500 m cut off the hall's east end; an escort standing at the hall's east end, (2500, 2250),
holds **every metre of the room inside the hold — the farthest corner is 559 m**. So the geometry
does what no predicate can: the escort waits at the mouth of the room and does not go in, not
because a rule forbids it but because there is nowhere else it needs to be (§8, §13).

**3. The dive is the door, and the house hears every one.** Four ceilings — 2,600, 2,700, 2,750,
2,800 — each entered by a deliberate dive at `DEPTH.DESCENT_SIG` 72
([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §2). The First Chord listens at HYD 45, and 72 through the
Fields' 0.70 water is a **contact from 3,596 m, a bearing from 2,791 and a classification from
2,028**. The party's first dive happens 1,550 m from the Chord. **The house knows who has arrived
before anybody says so**, which is the courtesy Fenn's first line acknowledges and the reason the
mission opens loud and then stops.

**4. What the house hears is entered as a time.** Twice in twelve minutes the water does something.
At **07:40 the Chord sounds, unstruck**, SIG 12 for twenty seconds. At **08:40 the axis is heard**,
SIG 3 for twenty seconds, sixty seconds after — the return's own figures, inherited whole from
[mission-attendance.md](mission-attendance.md) §6: three, twenty seconds, a minute of precursor, and
a refusal to say what it is. Both are attendable emitters and both are entered as times. **Nothing
in this document connects them.** The Directorate would enter the gap as well; the Order keeps no
gaps, and that one-line difference between two liturgies is the only thing this mission says about
the fact that two orders which have never shared a page have been writing down the same thing.

### The SIG budget

**SIG budget: 8** — a description rather than a ceiling, which is
[mission-attendance.md](mission-attendance.md)'s kind of number and its exact figure: the two
quietest missions in the bible are a Directorate watch that does not move and a Knight escort that
does not sound, and both come out at eight. It is not what the party emits — the Choirmaster's hull
sits at 55 beside it all mission and is in breach of nothing. It is what the *escort* emits once it
is in the chord, and the whole of the mission's difficulty is that holding still is a decision
somebody has to keep making for eight minutes.

It is playtested against a player who exceeds it, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10, and the
interesting adversary is not the careless one. It is **the player who keeps the Voice live to listen
better**, on the reasonable theory that a command hull with its systems up hears more. It does not:
HYD is unchanged by Silent Running — "throttling engines does not unplug the hydrophones"
([units.md](units.md)) — so the Voice at 55 hears exactly what the Voice at 8 hears, and pays for it
with the one row the mission scores.

**The First Chord is a `MissionStructure`, `StructureKind.SoundingSpire`, prebuilt at (2000, 2250),
2,900 m**: SIG 30 idle, HYD 45, 1,800 HP (`STRUCTURE_STATS`). Two consequences, both stated rather
than discovered.

- **It is the house's ears.** HYD 45 is what hears the party's descent from 3,596 m and classifies it
  from 2,028 (§4). Nothing in the format reads that hearing — the house's account of what it heard is
  Fenn's lines and the emitters' windows, not a `MissionSweep` (§13) — but the number is the reason
  the lines are true.
- **Its depth grant reaches nobody, and that is why the refit exists.** `STRUCTURE_AURAS.SOUNDING_SPIRE`
  grants PR+1 within 600 m **to hulls on the granting structure's own slot** (`auras.ts`,
  `spire.slot !== slot`). The First's party has no hulls, and the player's are on a different slot,
  so the Chord grants precisely nothing to anybody. A chapter-house's own hulls live under its
  instrument; this party is not the house's, and the four certificates in §3 are what it costs to
  visit somebody else's. The Spire also never goes active: `world.spireActive` is set only when a
  hull riding the grant is genuinely below its own rating, so **the First Chord hums at 30 for
  twelve minutes and never sings at 80**. An instrument in working order that nobody is permitted to
  sound is, mechanically, exactly that.

The three cells are the writing, and **they carry no `reading`**, which is the format's own way of
saying a thing is heard and not read: an emitter without a reading is not attendable, contributes to
no count, and appears in no epilogue. At Tier 3 an emitter's contact carries position and depth and
**no kind and no faction** (`types.ts`, `MissionEmitter`; `world.ts`, `spawnEmitter`), so the
strongest thing the player can ever hold on a cell is a scratch at a place. That is the whole of
what the format will give, and it is exactly what [habitats.md](habitats.md) §5 asks for.

**Naming follows [culture.md](culture.md) §4.** Knights carry the family name always and titles
precede it, because the families are finite: Choirmaster Ivane Sull, Voice Ren Kalliso, Chapter-wright
Aldis Fenn. The Three are not named, here or anywhere, and the document is not being coy — nobody in
the Order uses their names, and a document that supplied three would be making the room smaller.

**Nobody is wrong in this mission because nobody else is here.** [campaign.md](campaign.md) §2
rule 1 usually constrains how an opponent is written. This is the one mission in the campaign with no
opponent at all, and the rule turns into its stranger form: everything in this water is behaving
correctly, including the house, including the axis, and the discomfort is entirely the player's.

**The Choirmaster reads alone, and the format cannot make her.** No predicate says *no other hull
entered this region* — every predicate is a query over the player's own force and none of them is a
negative ([mission-standing-wave.md](mission-standing-wave.md) §13's wall, reached from a new side)
— so the mission does not ask for one. It arranges the water instead: the room is 500 m wide, the
hold is 600 m, and an escort at the hall's east end covers all of it at 559 m. **A player who takes
the whole escort into the room has not broken a rule; they have walked four hulls into a dry archive
at 2,900 m for no reason, and the house enters the times.** That is the correct amount of
enforcement for a courtesy, and §13 records it as a decision rather than an oversight.

**What the Three write is never read out.** Not in this document, not in a `say` beat, not in an
objective's text and not in a reading. Sull comes out of the room at 10:30 and says one sentence —
*"I have read the season's case. It is the same hand."* — and the sentence is chosen because it
carries the only fact the campaign needs (the writing continues, and continues in the same three
hands) and nothing about what the writing is. She knows the transcripts match the Directorate's dream
records word for word; she has told nobody ([characters.md](characters.md)); **she tells nobody
here.** [mission-item-nine.md](mission-item-nine.md) §10 built the shape — the player is in the room
where the text is read and never hears the text — and this is the Order's Item Nine, six missions
later and in the other direction: unsealed to exactly one person, who then does not say it.

**The hospice is heard and is never entered.** Three cells at 4 SIG on a six-second period, two
seconds on: a Voice in the hall's west end at (1600, 2250) stands 350 m from the nearest cell, which
is a **contact from 808 m and a classification from 456** — well inside, and a classification on an
emitter is a place and a depth and nothing else. The player will hear the scratching for twelve
minutes, will be able to resolve it to three points, and will never be told a single thing about it.
The mission is the campaign's fourth wall walked up to from the Order's side, and the wall holds
because the format's own contact rules hold it.

## 7. What Is Heard

**Six things, and two of them are entered as times.**

- **The house itself.** The First Chord at 30 SIG, continuously, from the middle of the map. The
  Voice holds it at **contact from 2,618 m** through the Fields' 0.70 water and **2,846 m** through
  the chord's 0.80, and at bearing from 2,032 / 2,209 — so the house is audible from the moment the
  party crosses the foot and is the only thing on this map that would still be audible if nobody
  moved. A chapter-house's ambient signature is a chord ([habitats.md](habitats.md) §8), and this is
  the deepest one.
- **The party arriving, at 72.** Eight seconds of descent from 2,300 to 2,650 m, then 1.1 s more
  into the chord's water at 2,700 and 4.4 s to the floor at 2,900. Heard by the Chord from 3,596 m,
  classified from 2,028. Ascent is 15 m/s and silent, and nobody ascends in this mission.
- **The escort, once it stops.** A silent Voice at 7.6 is a contact to the Chord's HYD 45 inside
  958 m and a classification inside 540; a silent Corvette at 5.3, 768 and 433. So the house does
  not stop hearing the escort — a house at 2,900 m hears everything in its own hall. What it stops
  hearing is a hull *under way*, which is the only thing this register calls a shove and the only
  thing it enters. That is what §8's met reading means by *heard nothing else of the party*.
- **The three cells.** SIG 4, two seconds in every six, unbounded, from the hospice. Contact at
  808 m to the Voice, classification at 456; a Corvette at 686 and 387. Never attendable, never
  entered, never read.
- **The Chord, sounding, unstruck — 07:40, twenty seconds, SIG 12.** Bearing to the Voice from
  **1,246 m** in the chord's own water, which is further than any part of the house, so every hull in
  the building hears it whatever it is doing. Nobody struck it. Fenn enters the time.
- **The axis — 08:40, twenty seconds, SIG 3, at 3,050 m in the trench 400 m south of the Chord,
  fifty metres off its floor.** The path from the hall to the point crosses 250 m of Coral Ruins and
  150 m of Abyssal Trench, a mean PF of about 1.10, which puts **bearing at 639 m** — so a hull in
  the hall's south half has it and a hull at the room's mouth, 640 m out, is on the line. A Corvette
  standing at the hall's south edge is 150 m from the point and holds it at Track. **The axis is not
  entered as anything. It is entered as the time.**

And what is absent: no fauna, no hazard, no third faction, no combat, no mark, no ping and no
ascent. [mission-aptitude.md](mission-aptitude.md) §7 filled the water with sound and made almost
none of it actionable; this mission does the same inside a building, with one consequence that one

## 8. The Objective

**Take the Choirmaster to the room, and keep the chord's hush while she reads.**

Three rows. Two of them decide the count and neither is a keystone; the third is the house's, and is
read out and never ranked.

**Roles.** `'tender'` — the Choirmaster's hull, one hull, and the literal must use the escort hold's
own word (`types.ts`, `MissionRole`), which is why this document calls it *the Choirmaster's hull*
in prose and never *the tender*. `'escort'` — the Voice and the two Corvettes, three hulls.

**Regions.** `sealed-room` 2500, 2000, 500, 500 · `the-chord` 1500, 2000, 1000, 500 · `the-approach`
1500, 750, 1000, 1250 · `the-foot` 0, 0, 4000, 750.

**1. `the-room`** — *"The Choirmaster reads alone. The room is dry and the cases are sealed a season
at a time."*

- Predicate: `{ kind: 'extract'; role: 'tender'; region: 'sealed-room'; count: 1 }`
- **Terminal**, no keystone. Revealed at 00:00. Marker `the-room` at (2750, 2250), radius 250,
  labelled *"The sealed room. The Choirmaster reads there alone."*
- Reading, met: *"The season's case was read. Nothing of it is entered."*
- Reading, unmet: *"The case was not read this season. It will keep; it has kept thirty-six years. I
  will not."*

  which is why a row read *at the close* needs a late reveal. This one is not that: reaching the
  room is a thing that happened, and the party is seated 2,089 m away at the foot, so nothing
  latches at tick zero.

**2. `the-hush`** — *"The chord is where the house keeps its silence. The escort is silent in it:
eight, and nothing struck."*

- Reading, met: *"The hush was kept. The house heard the Choirmaster and heard nothing else of the
  party, which is the whole of what an escort is for in this house."*
- Reading, unmet: *"The escort was heard in the chord. A hull under way in the chord is a shove, and
  the house has entered the time."*
- `quiet` reads the peak emitted SIG over the named role and never `own.peakSig` (`predicates.ts`),
  which is what lets the Choirmaster's hull sit at 55 beside a ceiling of 8 without breaching it.
  That is not a loophole; it is the objective. **The house is entitled to hear the Choirmaster. It
  is not entitled to hear anybody else**, and the met reading says exactly that.
- It is **standing** (`predicates.ts`, `isStanding`), so it is re-derived every tick and read at the
  resolve. §13 carries what that costs.

**3. `the-house-hears`** — *"The house hears what comes to it. What is heard is entered as the
time."*

- Predicate: `{ kind: 'attend'; count: 2 }`
- **Non-terminal**, read out at the close and unable to touch the ladder

### What the union cannot say here

Two rows, and neither is asked for as a build. **The escort did not enter the room** is
inexpressible — there is no negative region test — and §6 argues the geometry is the better answer.
**The hush was held throughout** is inexpressible too: `quiet` is a standing snapshot rather than an
integral, so a party that shoved at 06:00 and went silent at 11:59 reads Met. §13 carries both, with
the smallest shape that would fix the second, and neither stops the mission being played.

### The failure that is not here

**Nothing in this mission can be lost.** No hull can be destroyed — the water holds no hostile, no
animal and no hazard; every metre of it is inside the party's refit, so crush is 0 everywhere; and
the Lid's line at 150 m is 2,150 m shallower than the shallowest hull. Nothing can be broken.

**And the count can still read Lost**, which is the Order's own distinction between a loss and a
discourtesy, made mechanical. [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10's sixty-second telegraph is not owed
here because the close is a `resolve` with `conclusion: true` — a tide ending rather than a failure
state (`types.ts`; `missions.test.ts`) — and the document does not lean on the exemption to hide
anything: the hush is on the panel from 00:00, the ceiling is a mode the player either has on or has
not, and the room is 2,089 m away with twelve minutes to cross it. **It closes as a conclusion,
nothing here can be lost, and that is the whole reason it is unbearable.**

## 10. What It Teaches

One system, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10, and this mission's is **nothing** — the campaign
table says so and the document takes it literally. What lands across the twelve minutes is one
button turned the right way and three things the player already had:

- **The first three minutes** are the dive and the first hush: the approach roofed at 2,600, the
  order at 72, the house hearing it, and then three hulls going to 3–8 in a room where 28 is a shove.
- **The middle four** are the hold: the Choirmaster's hull moves only with ears inside 600 m, so the
  escort ends up at the mouth of the room without ever being told to stand there.
- **The last five** are load-bearing exactly as §10 asks: the Chord sounds unstruck at 07:40 and the
  axis is heard at 08:40, and the escort is silent for both or it is not. The row is standing, and it
  is read at 12:00.

Underneath it, the campaign's real subject, which is the same trick [campaign.md](campaign.md) §2
rule 2 plays on every faction: **the Order is told nothing about what this house costs it.** Four
certificates at sixty apiece, three Spires left in the ledger and not a fourth, and a Choirmaster who
spends a tide of her short window going to read a case she has read thirty-six times. Nobody says
*we cannot afford this*. The arithmetic is in the briefing and the player does it themselves.

And a third thing, which is why the mission is called what it is. **The Three are the title and are
never in it.** They do not speak, are not shown, are not named and are not read; what the player
gets is three points at 4 SIG in a room off the chord and a sentence at 10:30 saying the hand has
not changed. A player who works that out is holding the campaign's whole shape a mission early, and
the game has told them nothing.

What this mission deliberately does not teach:

- **Projected depth access** — the Sounding Spire's PR+1 is the Knights' answer to the deep, and
  [mission-aptitude.md](mission-aptitude.md) §10 flagged the campaign-level gap that no §7 mission
  taught it. This mission does not close it: the Chord is a Spire on somebody else's slot and grants
  nobody anything (§5). It is closed at the rim, in [The Rim Deposits](mission-rim-deposits.md),
  paid for by the ledger in §3.
- **The raid, and the crystal** — mission 6. This mission counts the money and does not spend it.
- **The transmission** — mission 7. There is an aimed instrument in tune in the middle of this map
  and the mission's one rule about it is that nobody touches it.
- **Permanent loss** — [Nineteen](mission-nineteen.md)'s, and nothing can be lost here to teach it
  with.
