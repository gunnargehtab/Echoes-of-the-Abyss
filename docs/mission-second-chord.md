# The Second Chord 7 — The Second Chord

> The seventh mission of the Knight campaign ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §7), specified and built —
> and the campaign's ending ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §9): not canon, not ranked, not merged, and
> not a fight. One of the fourteen documents that complete the bible's campaign, written to the pattern
> [mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) sets and the Chord documents continue: everything
> here is authored — the forces, the water, the beats, the numbers and the text — and code
> transcribes this document.

**Setting:** The Rim — the Mouth's northern edge, 4,410 m below everything, on D+3 of the rim week
of 214 PC, one tide after *The Rim Deposits* ([world-map.md](world-map.md) §5;
[campaign.md](campaign.md) §8; [mission-rim-deposits.md](mission-rim-deposits.md) §1).

**Mission id:** `chord-second-chord` — namespaced by campaign after `chord-rim-deposits`, per
[campaign.md](campaign.md) §1.

**Premise, for the entry that offers it:** *The crystal is set into a node on the lip, the lattice
is spent to buy thirty seconds, and the Choirmaster comes down to play them.*

**This is the last mission the bible carries, and the one place the format's poverty is the
design.** Every other document in the queue asks for something the engine has not got and says how
small the shape would be ([mission-standing-wave.md](mission-standing-wave.md) §13's build
predicate; [mission-convocation.md](mission-convocation.md) §13's commander ability). This one asks
for two — Resonance Collapse and the Second Chord itself — and then arrives at a third gap it does
**not** want closed: the format cannot show a reply, and neither can this mission
([campaign.md](campaign.md) §7, "You transmit. The reply is not shown"). The tone is held for
thirty seconds at the loudest figure the model has, the mission runs its length whatever came back,
and the last forty seconds are eighteen minutes of silence being the ending.

## 1. What the Second Chord Is

The First Chord was raised at the First Chapter-House in 178 PC, aimed down the trench axis, and
transmitted into the Mouth. The reply preceded the transmission's expected return by forty-one
seconds and three technicians never spoke again ([timeline.md](timeline.md), Anomaly Log;
[factions.md](factions.md)). The Second Chord is the Order's answer to that: an instrument built to
transmit something *structured*, which needs more resonant crystal than exists in Knight territory
and therefore needs the rim ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §8). Sull has the crystal as of the last
tide. This is the tide she plays it.

This document never explains the Mouth, describes it, or approaches it
([culture.md](culture.md) §6; [campaign.md](campaign.md) §2 rule 3). The lip is as far as the chart
goes, the tone is aimed at water the map declines to author, and what comes back — if the word
*back* is even the right one — is not in the mission, not in the epilogue, and not in narration.

**The week, on the judge's calendar, and every rim document states it.** D was the concern's survey
and the plateaus' sowing under a bed on the western lip
([mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md); [mission-second-seeding.md](mission-second-seeding.md)).
D+1 was the Directorate's column walking the lip, with the Order present as Prospect's
reconnaissance and as a measuring party over the eastern faces at 1,750 m
([mission-first-arrival.md](mission-first-arrival.md) §11). D+2 was the raid: two Sounding Spires
raised on the eastern terraces at the tide's turn and entered by the watch as they rose, the
correction filed, and two loads of rim crystal carried above the Staging line
([mission-rim-deposits.md](mission-rim-deposits.md)). This is D+3, and it is the last one.

Three facts about this water decide the mission, and none of them is a fence.

**A third node stands on the lip, and it is the last one the Order will ever raise.** `the-chord`
at 4800, 3150, at 3,000 m — 559 m from `node-two` and therefore inside the grant chain the raid
came home through. It went up in the tide between under node-two's cover, and it cost the last 120
crystal on the Ninth's ledger ([mission-the-three.md](mission-the-three.md) §3). It is the only
Spire this campaign calls *the last*, and the word is arithmetic rather than rhetoric.

**The Directorate entered it when it rose and did not correct it.** A node with nothing under it is
a silence, and the rim is attended, not policed ([mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) §12).
The Chord stands 320 m from the listening dome and 602 m from the nearest Chorister's seat — the
Order raising its instrument inside the Directorate's hearing, and the Directorate writing it down.
The instant a Knight hull stands under it below its own rating, the node sings at 80 and the
silence stops being one.

**The interval is a time, and it belongs to the lip.** The cycle is at thirty-nine hours and
shortening ([timeline.md](timeline.md), 213 PC). The lip's next return sounds at the sixteenth
minute and forty seconds of the mission clock and holds for twenty seconds, and the Directorate's
own transcripts would have predicted it to the hour and never published it
([mission-attendance.md](mission-attendance.md) §2). **The return is not the window. The interval
Sull appoints is the seventeenth minute**, which the Order reads off the return the way it reads
off any precursor, and the tone is held inside it (§6). Nobody in this mission says the two facts
are the same fact.

Nobody here is wrong ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §2 rule 1). The cohort holds the rim and corrects
what stands into its watch, and is right to. The Order believes it is discourteous not to reply and
has spent everything it has arriving at the sentence. Two hulls of the plateaus are still on the
western terraces, having asked nothing of anyone, and a bed of theirs stands on the western lip.
And somewhere in the Underway this week a Board has read the registration of this field into its
own record and moved on ([mission-item-nine.md](mission-item-nine.md) §6, item six). The Order does
not know that, nobody on this rim mentions it, and the document goes no further.

## 2. Whose Hulls the Player Commands

**The player commands the Order's last works party: six hulls, PR-2, no refit, carrying two loads
of rim crystal down through a lattice that will be spent at sixteen minutes.**

Voice Ren Kalliso commands everything below the Staging line until half past fifteen. Choirmaster
Ivane Sull is aboard her own hull at the Staging and is **held there by the mission** until the
interval's approach — the format's own `releaseTick`, and the document's "the Choirmaster waits
above the fight". She collapses the lattice at sixteen on the world's clock, comes down through
water the Order has just made lethal to itself, and plays.

Kalliso has not refused an order from Sull ([characters.md](characters.md)), and she does not
refuse this one. **The mission's second record is the player's, not hers**: the tone is a button on
the Choirmaster's hull, and a Chord standing whole and unstruck at eighteen minutes is read as the
interval passing — *which is the same thing said courteously*, and is priced identically to the
transmission by construction (§8).

**Engine bound, stated so nobody corrects it into a bug.** Five parties and a court slot: the Order
on the player's slot flying `Faction.Hadron`; the plateaus on `Faction.Pelagia`; **one** Directorate
party carrying the watch, the 9th Trench Cohort, the dome and the twelve Choristers together; the
rim's attendants, emitter-only, carrying a Directorate faction value because a party must carry
one; and the lip, a second emitter-only party holding one return
([mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) §5). The court slot is reserved and empty. The Drift is
not a party, and what rises at 16:30 rises from it.

The one party for everything Directorate is the same finding
[mission-rim-deposits.md](mission-rim-deposits.md) §2 records: hostility is `Owner.slot` and every
party is an enemy of every other, so twelve armed Choristers on a slot of their own would open fire
on the watch, which their corrections stop 304 m from — and on the dome, which the second one stops
112 m from. On this rim the point is inherited rather than live, because **every Directorate hull
here is weapons-cold** (§5, §13): the mission still seats them on one slot, because a rim that is
attended by four separate Directorate parties is four separate lies about who is standing with
whom. The attendants and the lip are safe as separate parties because `combat.ts` refuses to
auto-acquire a static emitter (§13).

3. **The array, carried and unfenced.** Active sonar has been in the Order's hands since *Nineteen*

4. **Torpedoes, carried.** Two per Corvette at 700 damage. The dome is 1,200 HP and dies to two of

## 4. The Lattice, Spent

The system this mission teaches, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §2: **Resonance Collapse, and a
thirty-second emission at 100** ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §7's row). Four movements, and the
fourth is the ending.

**1. Three nodes are a lattice, and the lattice is the rating.** Two on the terraces at 2,600 m and
one on the lip at 3,000 m, each granting PR+1 to the Order's hulls within 600 m, horizontally,
same slot only (`STRUCTURE_AURAS.SOUNDING_SPIRE`). `node-two` to `the-chord` is 559 m, so a hull
passing from the raid's water to the Chord's is never uncovered. Every Knight hull in the Abyssal
band on this map is alive because a structure says so, and none of them is certificated for a
single metre of it.

**And none of the three is a pair.** `node-one` to `node-two` is 1,154 m, `node-two` to `the-chord`
559 m and `node-one` to `the-chord` 1,235 m — all three inside the 1,500 m the pairing rule spends
([mission-standing-wave.md](mission-standing-wave.md) §4) — and there is no corridor on this map:
no line of sonic damage across the water the carriers walk down, no PF 2.00, and no Spire standing
at eighty with nothing under it. The terrace pair was decided at the turn, raised together as two
grants with neither finished beside the other, and *decided once and never re-decided* means it
stays decided that way ([mission-rim-deposits.md](mission-rim-deposits.md) §4); `the-chord`
completes a tide later beside two nodes whose pairing has already been settled as none, and takes
what a third node takes, which is nothing. In the literal it is shorter still — all three are
prebuilt, and a prebuilt structure never completes, so the rule is never offered any of them
([mission-conclave-chord.md](mission-conclave-chord.md) §3) — and the pairing pass is unbuilt in
any case ([mission-standing-wave.md](mission-standing-wave.md) §13). **What the Collapse spends at
sixteen is two grants and not a standing corridor**, which is why §7 prices all three nodes at 30
idle and the Order's own instrument never turns its weapon inward.

**2. The lattice can be collapsed once, and collapsing it spends the nodes.** Resonance Collapse is
*simultaneous detonation of a crystal lattice: no damage, total system failure, everything caught
stunned, deafened and at Tier-0 for twelve seconds* ([factions.md](factions.md)). "The Knights do
not kill you; they remove you from the conversation." What that sentence costs, said in this
mission's own arithmetic: the two terrace nodes go, and from that tick **every Knight hull at
1,800 m or deeper and outside the Chord's 600 m takes 4 HP/s, unhealable** (`4 × 1²`,
`crushAttritionPerSecond`) — 105 seconds for a Corvette, 300 for a Cruiser. The Order removes
itself from the water everywhere except under the instrument. The way out is 850 m of climb from
the terraces (56.7 s at 15 m/s, 227 HP) or 1,250 m from the lip (83.3 s, 333 HP), or 600 m of walk
into the Chord's grant, which a Corvette covers in 7.1 seconds and pays 28 HP for.

**3. The emission: thirty seconds at a hundred, bow to the Mouth.** The tone is a `MissionSounding`
— 400 m, held, bow-on, at a SIG floor of 100 — and 100 down the lip's 1.60 axis is Contact at
**11,809 m** to a dome-lifted ear, 11,016 m to a Submersible, 9,316 m to a Cruiser and 7,907 m to a
Corvette. It is the loudest sustained thing the bible has ever authored and it is aimed south. You
announce yourself to the entire Rift, and **a tone you interrupt is a tone you have not played**: a
broken hold resets to zero (`accrueSounding`), so thirty seconds is thirty seconds and not six
fragments of five.

**4. The reply is not shown, and the tone is a reading rather than a rung.** The outcome ladder
counts what the Order has left — the crystal set, and the Choirmaster, the escort and the carriers
answering. The transmission prints beneath whatever that count earned, met or unmet, ranked by
nobody: *Transmitted. The reply is not entered here.* or the Chord standing unstruck. That is Item
Nine's shape kept from the Order's side ([mission-item-nine.md](mission-item-nine.md) §8) and it is
a property of the runtime rather than of anybody's restraint (§13).

**The answer the mission does not hide, and the correction it actually is.** Six Choristers stopped
200–412 m from the Chord are inside the 450 m their doctrine reaches, and they do not fire, because
**every Directorate hull on this rim is weapons-cold** (§5). That is not a softening; it is the only
sentence the format can write. `combat.ts` auto-acquires the nearest hostile thing carrying position
and hull, and a structure is one of those — so an armed cohort standing 255 m from a Sounding Spire
takes 1,800 HP down in fifteen seconds whatever anybody intended, and the watch, inherited at 206 m
from the Chord with a 650 m gun, does it in twenty from tick zero. There is no way to author a hull
that answers hulls and leaves a node alone (§13). And the node cannot be allowed to fall: every
point of `chord-water` is inside its six hundred, and a PR-2 carrier is only rated for the 3,000 m
the crystal is set at because the node says so. So the rim attends and does not police, which is the
sentence §1 already writes — the correction is a rank of six standing between the Order and its own
instrument, entered, filed and heard, and the pressure it applies is acoustic and geometric rather
than ballistic. **The only guns in this water are the Order's**, they are aimed at nothing, and
[mission-rim-deposits.md](mission-rim-deposits.md) §6's geometry one tide on is a fact about where
the Directorate stands rather than about what it shoots.

### The SIG budget

**SIG budget: 100** — the transmission's own figure, and the first budget in the bible at the
scale's ceiling. This is the sixth reading the campaign has given [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10's
rule: the prologue's twenty is a ceiling, Asset Recovery's sixty-five a working level, Attendance's
eight a description, Aptitude's twenty-eight a direction, the raid's eighty an instrument singing,
and this one is **the number the mission is for**. There is nothing above it. A player cannot
exceed it, and the fact that the scale runs out is the point: the Order has arrived at the loudest
sentence it is able to say.

Playtested against two adversaries. The player who tries to play the tone early finds the row is
not scored before 16:00, the carrier is held to 15:30, and the sounding point is 3,715 m from where
she is sitting. And the player who holds the lip by standing on it and trading with the cohort
under the Chord finds that the escort is what brings the Choirmaster down, and that a party with
nothing left at 15:30 has an instrument, a node, and nobody to play it.

**The dive, and why the document asks for it and the literal cannot.** 1,750 m is a free depth on
every floor of this chart — the Slopes' 2,200, the Terraces' 2,600 and the Lip's 3,100 all admit
it — so the Choirmaster's hull crosses from the Staging to the Chord's water without paying
anything, and then dives 1,250 m at 45 m/s in 27.8 seconds inside the Chord's grant, arriving at
3,000 m at about 17:14. The document asks her to play it from *under* the instrument, at the depth
the Chord was raised to, and it is the right ask in register and in fiction. **`MissionSounding` is
horizontal** — a point, a radius and a cone, and no depth term (`soundingHolds`) — so the literal
cannot require it, and §13 names `depthMaxM?` as the shape if anybody wants it. What the literal
*can* do is make the dive worth taking: after 16:00 the only water on this map that is not costing
a Knight hull four points a second is the six hundred metres around the Chord.

**And the bow is geometry rather than an order.** `Heading` survives a stop and is written by
movement (`components.ts`), so a hull that has come 3.4 km south from the Staging and stopped over
the Chord is already pointed at the sounding point, which is 350 m further south and 32° off the
bearing she arrived on — inside the cone's forty-five, and the only margin in the mission nobody
had to author. The Order does not have to turn to face the Mouth. It arrives facing it.

**The interval is the lip's and not the Order's.** The return sounds at the sixteenth minute and
forty seconds, at SIG 3 for twenty seconds — Attendance's figures exactly
([mission-attendance.md](mission-attendance.md) §6) — and is Bearing to the Voice from 808 m and to
a Corvette from 686 m. **The interval Sull appoints is the seventeenth minute**, which begins the
tick the return stops, and the tone is held from about 17:14 to about 17:44 inside it: the lip
speaks for twenty seconds and the Order answers fourteen after it has finished. She reads the
return as the interval's precursor and is right about both, which is the most the document will
say. Eleven kilometres north of here the same thing comes up the Ninth's axis nine times a cycle
and is entered as a time by people who would have predicted this one to the hour. Nobody in this
mission connects the two.

**The two records, unranked and unrankable.** `the-transmission` is non-terminal, so it cannot touch

carries one, and only terminal rows enter the ladder ([mission-intake.md](mission-intake.md) §5's
neutrality guard, at its heaviest use). The transmitted line and the unstruck line are therefore

empty node is Lost with *Transmitted* printed beneath it**, which is the register's own judgement of
that run and the document says so out loud.

**Four terminal rows, one keystone.** An unmet keystone is Lost whatever else answered; all four met
is Complete — the Chord whole and the Order answering whole; the keystone met with any hull entered
is Partial. **The middle rung is the Order short and never the Order silent**, which is the whole
reason the transmission is not on the ladder.

**Three things this ladder cannot say, said here instead.** First, **that the Chord still stands**:
there is no predicate over what the player holds standing, so a node struck at 03:56 with the
crystal already inside it would read Met (§13, and
[mission-standing-wave.md](mission-standing-wave.md) §13's row, third customer). Nothing on this rim
strikes it — the water is weapons-cold on every slot but the player's (§4) — so the gap is a
statement about the union rather than a hole this tide falls through. It matters anyway, and
mechanically: after 16:00 the Chord's 600 m is the only water on the map a Knight hull is rated for,
so a party that spent the node itself plays the tone bleeding and climbs home bleeding. Second, **which return was heard**: `attend` counts
attendable emitters and names none, so the row is worded in threes rather than pretending to point
at the lip's own. Third, **that the tone was hers**: `sound` names no sounding, and there is exactly
one authored, so the two happen to coincide.

**And a rectangle is not a room.** A `MissionRegion` is a plan-view rectangle and `extract` is a
two-dimensional test, so the format cannot require a carrier to be *at* the node to set the crystal
into it: a player who holds 1,750 m over `chord-water` latches the keystone with the Chord idle at
30 and never enters Abyssal water at all. The same field that would give the sounding a depth would
give this row one (§13), and until it lands the descent is the fiction the beats and the briefing
carry and the node's own silence is the tell that nobody is under it. The rectangle straddles two
floors for the same reason it is drawn where it is — its northern hundred metres lie over the
Terraces' 2,600 m ground and its southern four hundred over the Lip's 3,100 — so the strip that
will actually hold a hull at 3,000 m is everything south of y 3000, and a hull that means to be
under the instrument, and to hear the lip's return at Bearing at the sixteenth minute (§6), stands
in the southern half of the rectangle rather than in the middle of it.

**`the-count` is met by about 00:30 and that is the design**, as it was on D+2: three structures at
SIG 30, one of them 320 m from a Cantor, spend thirty cumulative seconds of Classification before
the party has finished descending. The row is a record, not a rule, and its one conditional line
says so in the Directorate's own register.

One system, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10: **Resonance Collapse, and the emission it buys** —
an instrument the Order can fire once, that does no damage, that costs it the two nodes holding its

Underneath it, the campaign's argument finishing. *Aptitude* said six is a small number to work
with; *Nineteen* said six is a small number to have; *Conclave* spent a lattice's standing on not
calling; *The Three* counted the crystal once and found three Spires in it; *The Rim Deposits* spent
two of the three and brought home a number of hulls. This mission spends the third, spends the other
two to buy thirty seconds, and reads the count. **The Order is never told it cannot be replaced. It
counts its hulls at the end of every mission and the number is the argument.**

What this mission deliberately does not teach:

- **What replied, or whether *replied* is the word.** The resolve at 18:00 and one epilogue sentence
  are the whole mechanism, and the format's inability to show a reply is the only place in this
  document where a gap is the design rather than a debt (§13).
- **Who was right.** A concern registered this field ([mission-item-nine.md](mission-item-nine.md)
  §6, item six), a cohort is holding an attended rim and is correct in every line it speaks, a
  garden stands on the western lip, and the Order has just made the loudest sound in the Rift. The

- **Building the node.** The third Spire went up in the tide between and is prebuilt, for the reason

  600 m wherever the floor is.
- **The Mouth.** Two attendants are entered as bearings and periods and called nothing; a return is
  entered as a time; and the word for calling any of it anything else would be *discourteous*.

**Choirmaster Ivane Sull, the order to descend — 01:00**

> The interval is at seventeen. The lattice comes down at sixteen; I come down at half past fifteen
> and I am over the slopes when it goes. Set the crystal and hold the lip. Descend.

**Cohort-Prime Adze, 9th Trench Cohort — 04:30**

> Correction is filed against the node on the lip. It was entered when it rose and stood into
> nothing; it stands into the watch now. What was set into it is counted. What is under it is
> corrected at what leaves.

**The charting pair, for the plateaus — 06:00**

> We're still here, on the terraces. We'd like it in somebody's record that we asked nothing of the
> rim and it asked nothing of us. We think you're about to ask it something.

**Watch-Speaker, for those below — 08:00**

> The rim is attended. Three nodes are entered. The third was entered when it was raised and was not
> corrected, because a node with nothing under it is a silence, and silence is attended too.

**Voice Ren Kalliso, once, to nobody — 12:00**

> Nineteen were the cadre. Twenty-two years are mine, since nine. Thirty seconds are the Order's. I
> would like it entered that I counted, and that the numbers are not the same kind of number.

**Choirmaster Ivane Sull, at the release — 15:30**

> I am coming down, with ears. Half a minute of slopes and the lattice goes under me.

**Choirmaster Ivane Sull, at the Collapse — 16:00**

> The lattice is spent. Everything below the line and outside the Chord's six hundred is bleeding
> from this tick. Hold the lip.

**Chapter-Master Halden Vrey, on the lattice from the Third — 16:00**

> I hear it. Enter that I said nothing else.

**Choirmaster Ivane Sull, on the way down — 17:00**

> The interval.

### The epilogue, in three readings

**Complete — the Chord is whole, and the Order answers.**

> The crystal is set and every hull answers the count. The lattice is spent, the nodes are spent,
> and what is below the line will come up or not. The Order has done the one thing it was founded to
> do and has nothing with which to do it twice, which Halden will say was the point. What was played
> over the Chord, or not, is entered beneath this, and the reply is not entered here.

**Partial — the Chord, and the Order short.**

> The crystal is set and the Order is short. A name is to be said to a house tonight and it is not
> mine to say; the Order has an instrument on the Mouth's edge and fewer people than it had this
> morning, and thirty thousand who will argue for a century about the trade. It is not canon that
> they are wrong, either way.

**Lost — a node on a lip.**

> Nothing is set. The lattice was spent to reach a node with nothing in it, and the Order is below
> the line at four points a second with a rating it lent itself and has now taken back. The plan was
> mine. Enter that, and enter nothing about the Mouth, because nothing was said to it.

Each line fails [culture.md](culture.md) §3 for the other three factions, which is that document's
own test (§6). Sull spends an entire order apologising for an instrument's physics and never once
for the people under it, and closes on a courtesy — *go and be dry* — which is the whole hospitality
the Order offers; the Consortium would have costed the bleed, the Commune would have put it to the
room, and the Directorate would not have explained the interval at all. Adze files a correction in
the passive and never says who is being corrected. The pair refuses the imperative twice and offers
a distinction where anybody else would put a request. Vrey's six words are a Chapter-Master watching
the lattice he built with his own hands come down on somebody else's rim and declining, formally, to
say anything about it — a refusal that only means something in a register where silence is written
down and played. And Kalliso's is the only line in the mission that counts two kinds of number in
one breath, which is why she says it to nobody.
