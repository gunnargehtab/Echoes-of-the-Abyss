# The Ledger 6 — Prospect

> The sixth mission of the Consortium campaign ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §4), specified —
> and the campaign's convergence slot: the Ledger's arrival at the eleven kilometres every
> ending is fought over ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §8). Written to the pattern
> [mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) sets and the Ledger documents continue:
> everything here is authored, and code transcribes this document.

**Setting:** The Rim — the Mouth's edge, the lip at 3,100 m over a floor 4,410 m below
everything (§11; [world-map.md](world-map.md) §3), in the same week of 214 PC
that three other navies arrive for three other reasons ([world-map.md](world-map.md);
[campaign.md](campaign.md) §8).

**Mission id:** `ledger-prospect` — namespaced by campaign, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §1.

---

## 1. What the Rim Is

At the southern terminus of everything, the floor of the setting: an eleven-kilometre
depression that returns pings before they should arrive, on a cycle that has shortened and is
shortening ([world-map.md](world-map.md); [timeline.md](timeline.md)). Its edge carries the
richest resonant-crystal deposits ever surveyed, and — since this year — attendant organisms
that a Consortium survey team has now filed, twice, as equipment fault
([bestiary.md](bestiary.md)).

The Ledger's arithmetic has been pointing here since mission 1. Ninefold is dying, Face Two
is closed, the Holding is spending castings it cannot re-pour, and the actuarial projection
has eleven years minus everything the campaign has cost. The new field has to exist. The only
candidate is the rim. This mission is the survey that proves it — or fails to — and the writ
says the quiet part in its first sentence, because the register does not have a loud part.

The mission never explains the Mouth, describes the Mouth, or approaches the Mouth
([culture.md](culture.md) §6). The lip is as far as the chart goes. What the depression
thinks of being surveyed is not in the writ, and the one entry that touches the question is
filed under equipment fault, twice, per standing practice.

**Everyone else is already here.** Whichever campaign the player is playing, the other three
arrive ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §8): a Commune charting pair reading the rim for seeding
ground, a Directorate watch attending what they have always attended, and a Knight
reconnaissance courteously measuring the crystal the Second Chord requires. Nobody fights.
Nobody is wrong. The mission is the same eleven kilometres from the Ledger's side, and its
whole convergence is played in contacts, transits, and four registers refusing each other's
frames on an open channel.

## 2. Whose Hulls the Player Commands

**The player commands the prospect expedition: four hulls, refit to the third rating,
unarmed, and a long way from anything that answers to the Board.**

The refit is the Consortium's answer to depth, priced and paid ([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md)
§3): they buy their way down. Every hull in the expedition carries a PR-3 certificate that
cost more than the hull, which is a mission fact and not a roster one — the roster's Cruiser
is unchanged; this Cruiser has been to the yards.

Foreman Corwin Osk leads the expedition. The man who spent the campaign keeping crews off
dying ground is the one sent to prove the ground the concern means to live on, and he took
the commission the way he takes everything: as a shift, with a works order and a return leg.

**Engine bound, stated so nobody corrects it into a bug.** Five parties and a court slot: the
expedition, the three navies' presences, and the rim's own attendants — a party whose only
assets in the water are sounds that answer no classification. The Drift is not a party, and
what rises at the end of this mission rises from it.

### What the expedition does not carry

1. **No weapons.** Struck at the writ: *struck — the writ surveys, the rim is crowded, and
   the Board prices an incident above a field*. Three navies are on the same eleven
   kilometres in the same week; an armed Consortium expedition is a war nobody has declared
   yet, and the Board — for once in the campaign — declines to be the first mover.
2. **No construction.** The works order is the next Board's to sign. This writ proves the
   field; it does not open it.
3. **The array, carried and priced.** Active sonar is unlocked and the writ neither spends
   it nor forbids it. The lip carries like a trench, the basin answers transmissions —
   Sorrowgate taught what answers a ping in deep water, in the campaign's first hour — and
   the survey's own soundings are already the loudest thing the expedition does. A player
   who pings the rim learns what the prologue's underwriter learned, with more warning.

## 4. The Round Trip

The system this mission teaches, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §2: **the Abyssal round trip**
— descent, work, ascent, as one committed shape ([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §5;
[economy.md](economy.md) §7).

1. **The descent is fast and deafening.** Forty-five metres a second at a SIG floor of
   seventy-two: the expedition announces its arrival to the whole rim as a matter of physics,
   and the SIG budget of this mission *is* the descent floor, because the budget is the trip.
2. **The work is loud, held, and aimed.** The survey reads six faces by hand — the sounding:
   within four hundred metres, bow on the formation, twenty seconds held at SIG eighty, the
   survey trade's standard figures ([mission-aptitude.md](mission-aptitude.md) §4 quotes the
   same numbers from the Knights' side, because the standard is older than the quarrel).
   Four faces prove the field. A broken hold resets; the rim does not give partial credit.
3. **The ascent is slow, silent, and the whole point.** Fifteen metres a second, a third of
   the descent, with everything the descent and the work attracted still interested. A deep
   raid must succeed, retreat, or die ([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §5), and a
   survey is a raid that steals information.
4. **Below the layer, the expedition is alone.** The entire mission runs under the
   thermocline: home cannot hear the survey and the survey cannot hear home
   ([systems-echo.md](systems-echo.md) §3). Every decision on the rim is made on the rim.

### The SIG budget

**SIG budget: 72** — the descent's own floor, for the first time in the campaign: the budget
is not a posture or a cover, it is the physics of arriving. The playtest adversary is the
player who believes the trip can be taken quietly, and the mission's first two minutes are
the refutation.

## 6. The Survey

**Six faces on the terraces, charted from the concern's long-range work. Four, read by hand
to survey standard, prove the field.**

The instrument is the sounding ([mission-aptitude.md](mission-aptitude.md) §4's mechanism,
authored here with the same trade-standard figures): a reader corvette within four hundred
metres of the face, bow on it, twenty seconds held at SIG eighty. Each reader is calibrated
to its own bank — three western faces to one hull, three eastern to the other — because
calibration is done at the yards against the charts, and the survey's procedure therefore
names hulls, which is the kind of sentence only this faction's procedure manual would think
to justify.

Twenty seconds at SIG eighty, on a resonance terrace, under the ears of three navies and
whatever the lip's returns belong to: every sounding is a decision about *when* and *facing
where*, and the mission's middle act is six of them, threaded through the visitors' transits
and the player's own tolerance ledger.

**The attendants are attendable.** The two returns on the lip carry entered-and-gap readings
like any authored arrival ([mission-attendance.md](mission-attendance.md) §6's instrument):
resolve one at Tier 2 or better while it sounds and the close carries the entry. The survey
is not asked to. The file the registry keeps for what it hears down there is named in §12,
and the campaign's fourth wall stays where [culture.md](culture.md) §6 nailed it.

## 7. What Answers

**The basin.** The rim's deep water holds what deep basins hold ([bestiary.md](bestiary.md)),
and the week has been loud: four navies' arrivals, the survey's six transmissions at eighty,
and the ordinary industry of a crowded rim. At 20:30 the ledger of all that noise comes due —
something big lifts off the lip's floor, loud, rising toward the terraces, uninterested in
classification and unavailable to it.

It is not an attack the mission expects the player to survive standing still, and not a
fight: the expedition is unarmed, the thing is a Sounder, and the prologue settled what that
combination means in the campaign's first twenty minutes. It is the ascent's starting gun,
ninety seconds ahead of the close ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §10), and the mission's last
lesson in its own system: the round trip is only a round trip if you leave while leaving is a
choice.

## 8. The Objective

**Prove the field. Bring the survey off the rim.**

**The field.** Four of six faces, read to standard. The counter is the objective.

**The ascent.** The expedition — three hulls of four at minimum — north of the slopes and
back in the staging water when the writ closes. The record rides the readers, the readers
ride the column, and a survey that stays on the rim is not a survey; the close treats it as
exactly what the registry would.

**The ledger.** Sixty seconds of Classification or better, cumulative, across the writ — not
a recall this time, and not a rule: a reading. The rim is crowded with the best ears in the
Rift, the survey is loud by trade, and what the close enters is simply whether the concern's
visit is already in three other navies' records, which mission 7 will care about more than
this one does.

### Results

| Result | Condition | The reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The field is proven** | Four faces read, and the column off the rim | "The field is proven. Four faces read to survey standard, the cores of the readings in duplicate, the column returned. The registry opens the page it has kept blank for five years, and the projection acquires a floor. Item Nine acquires an appendix." |
| **The survey returns short** | The column off the rim, under four faces | "The survey returns short of standard. What was read is real and insufficient; the Board is asked to authorise a second descent, and the actuarial note attached to the request is one sentence long." |
| **The rim keeps the survey** | The column does not come off | "The rim keeps the survey. The registry enters four hulls and their certificates against a field it can now neither prove nor forget, and the projection continues, minus everything, as projections do." |

Beneath whichever reading the run earns, the close assembles the tolerance's line — heard, or
unheard — and the attendants' entries or gaps, in the standing arrangement. A proven field
whose proving was classified by three navies is a different asset from a quiet one, and the
Board will learn which it bought at the next mission's table.

### The failure, and the sounds that precede it

The close is the writ's turn-north at 22:00, and everything in front of it is audible: the
basin's riser from 20:30 — ninety seconds of something enormous coming up — on top of a
mission that has been counting its own noise out loud since the descent. Nothing here fails
on a timer the water did not announce ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §10).

## 10. What It Teaches

One system, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10: **the Abyssal round trip** — descent, held
work, ascent, as one committed, audible shape, with the refit as its price of admission.
Load-bearing by the last five minutes definitionally: the last five minutes *are* the ascent,
raced against the basin.

What this mission deliberately does not teach:

- **The Mouth.** The lip is the chart's edge and the mission's. Two returns are filed per
  standing practice, and the standing practice is the whole joke, and the joke stopped being
  funny in mission 4.
- **The war.** Four navies, one rim, and nobody fires: the convergence is the situation, not
  the mission. What the situation becomes is the endings' business, and no campaign's ending
  is canon ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §9).
- **The decision.** The survey proves the field exists. What the concern does about the
  other thing the survey heard is mission 7, and it is not a fight either.

## 12. The Briefing

The survey writ, read at the staging. Signed for the Ninth Board. The Consortium's register
is defined in [culture.md](culture.md) §3.

> The concern requires a producing field inside eleven years or the concern requires an
> orderly dissolution plan, and the Board has reviewed the second document and declines to
> circulate it. The only candidate field is the rim. This writ proves it or does not.
>
> Six faces are charted on the terraces. Four, read by hand to survey standard, prove the
> field. The readers are calibrated to their banks; the procedure names hulls, and the
> procedure is right to.
>
> The expedition is refit to the third rating, certificated, and unarmed. Three navies are
> on the rim this week for three stated reasons, none of which is ours and none of which is
> false. The writ prices an incident above a field, and the Board asks the expedition to
> read that sentence twice.
>
> The descent will be heard by everything. That is the physics of arriving and the writ does
> not apologise for physics. The ascent begins no later than the turn, whatever the count
> stands at, because a survey that stays is a different asset class and the registry has a
> word for it.
>
> Two returns on the lip are on file as equipment fault. The file is standing practice. The
> writ directs the expedition's instruments at the faces, and its attention wherever the
> shift requires, and it does not define the difference. Exposure is authorised. Sentiment
> is not. Signed for the Board.

### Objective readings, in play

- *Prove the field. Read the faces by hand; enough closes the survey.*
- *Bring the column off the rim at the turn. A survey that stays is not a survey.*
- *The rim is crowded and the trade is loud. What is heard of you is entered, and read later, elsewhere.*

### The voices on the channel

**Foreman Corwin Osk — 01:00**

> Works order for the shift: go down loud, read four faces clean, come up slow, and be
> counted at the staging. Twenty-nine years I have run shifts on ground that was dying. This
> is the first one on ground that is not born yet. I find I do not care for the difference,
> and the shift does not care what I care for. Descend.

**The charting pair, for the plateaus — 05:30**

> We're here too — we thought you'd rather hear it from us than from your instruments. We're
> reading the rim for what could live on it. You're reading it for what can be taken out of
> it. The rim doesn't mind either of us yet. We'd ask you to notice the *yet*.

**Watch-Speaker, for those below — 08:00**

> The rim is attended. It was attended before the concern had a registry and it will be
> attended after. What is done on it this week is entered — in an account that is not yours,
> against a debt that is not stated.

**Voice of the reconnaissance, for the Order — 11:00**

> The Order notes the concern's instruments are in tune, and returns the compliment of
> assuming it was meant. What the crystal is for, we will not discuss on an open channel. It
> would be discourteous to the crystal.

**Osk, on the lip's returns — ~15:00**

> That is the lip talking back on its own schedule. Log it as equipment fault. That is not a
> joke; it is the name of the file, and the file is older than your opinion of it.

**Osk, at the riser — 20:45**

> Basin's awake. The shift is over when we are north of the slopes and counted, and not one
> reading sooner. Up. Slow is the fast way. Go.

Each line fails [culture.md](culture.md) §3 for the other three factions, which is that
document's own test (§6) — and this mission is the test run four ways in one channel: the
pair's *yet* is an offer wearing a warning, which only the plural register produces; the
Watch-Speaker's account-and-debt is agency refused in two sentences; the reconnaissance pays
a compliment to a mineral; and Osk closes the campaign's oldest register — the works order —
over the strangest water the concern has ever billed a shift to.
