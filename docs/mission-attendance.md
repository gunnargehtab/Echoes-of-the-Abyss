# The Attending 1 — Attendance

> The first mission of the Directorate campaign ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §6), specified. The
> fourth mission document the bible carries, to the pattern
> [mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) sets: everything here is authored — the forces,
> the water, the beats, the numbers and the text — and code transcribes this document.

**Setting:** the attending galleries, Sufficiency's southern face at the head of the Ninth
Trench, 214 PC ([world-map.md](world-map.md); [characters.md](characters.md)).

**Mission id:** `attending-attendance` — namespaced by campaign, per
[campaign.md](campaign.md) §1.

---

## 1. What a Shift Is

The rite is already canon and this mission does not invent it. An **attendance** is a shift spent
listening down the trench toward the Mouth and recording, without interpretation, what you
dreamt ([culture.md](culture.md) §5). It is compulsory, unpaid, and universally described by
Directorate citizens as the best part of their month. The transcripts run to six thousand pages
since 88 PC, are collected in these galleries, and are published nowhere
([world-map.md](world-map.md)).

The mission's whole job is to make a player sit inside that.

**Nothing attacks the player in this mission**, and unlike Tend the campaign row does not have to
promise it, because the water says it first. Three facts, none of them a fence:

**The Drift cannot descend here.** The galleries stand at 2,750–3,400 m and the axis below them
falls past 4,000 m. The deepest thing in the bestiary is a Sounder, working 2,000 m and pursuing
to 2,700 ([bestiary.md](bestiary.md) §4). Nothing catalogued reaches this water. The one
uncatalogued entry — the Attendants — is found only within 400 m of the Mouth's rim, eleven
kilometres south of the map's edge, and is not here. Tend's safety was a pursuit band; this one
is the same rule read from the other end, and it is why the Directorate settled where it did.

**The trench carries and the trench is a corridor.** PF 1.60 down the axis, no secrets down its
length, only distances ([systems-echo.md](systems-echo.md) §3; [world-map.md](world-map.md)).
The Ninth's axis aims at the Mouth, and sound runs both ways along a trench. The Directorate did
not choose to hear the Mouth. They settled where hearing it was unavoidable, and then became the
people that made them.

**Silence here is policy.** Directorate settlements are quieter than the water around them: the
Rift's universal courtesy is that when someone else is listening you are quiet, and this is the
faction that made a religion of it and the only one that writes the debt down
([culture.md](culture.md) §1, §5). A hull under way in a gallery of listening people is shoving.

Nobody in this mission says any of that either. It is the ground the mission stands on.

## 2. What "One Full Cycle" Is, In Play

The campaign row promises a cycle and the mission cannot deliver one, so this document answers
the question rather than inheriting it.

**The Mouth's cycle is 39 hours**, shortened from 43 in 213 PC, and the 96 PC transcript
predicted the shortening to the hour ([world-map.md](world-map.md);
[timeline.md](timeline.md)). [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10 caps a mission at 25 minutes. No
arrangement of those two numbers produces a cycle.

**A cycle is attended in three watches of thirteen hours, and this mission is one watch —
the third, the one the return closes.** The three watches make one transcript and the Directorate
files them as one document, which is why the campaign row says *one full cycle* and means it: the
cycle is the unit of the record, and the shift is the unit of the work. The player attends the
watch. The record receives the cycle.

Two consequences worth stating so nobody corrects them into bugs:

- **Eighteen minutes stands for thirteen hours**, a compression of roughly forty-three to one.
  Tend's tide is sixteen minutes and nobody harvests a plateau in sixteen minutes either
  ([mission-tend.md](mission-tend.md) §9); the beat table is the mission's clock and the fiction's
  clock is the fiction's.
- **The Mouth's cycle is the one interval in the Rift counted in hours.** Everything else is
  tides ([culture.md](culture.md) §2). That is not an inconsistency to fix — it is the tell. Tides
  are what you live by; hours are what you measure a thing by when you have measured it for a
  hundred and twenty-six years and are still not saying what it is.

## 3. Whose Hulls the Player Commands

**The player commands the watch of a single attendance shift: four Abyssal Submersibles seated in
the galleries, and the Cantorate's dome standing over them.**

The watch is not the shift. The shift is the cohorts in the stalls, who are dreaming, and whose
dreams are the transcript. The watch is the shift's outer ears — four hulls holding the water
quiet and putting a bearing on what arrives, so that what the stalls dream can be entered against
a measurement. The player never commands an attendant, and no order in this mission is given to a
person.

**Engine bound, stated so nobody corrects it into a bug.** One party, one faction, one seat. This
is the first mission document with no second party in the water at all — no delegation, no
survey, no column. The mission seats the player and the Drift is not populated.

| Hull | Count | Stats | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Abyssal Submersible — the watch | 4 | **SIG 22 idle / 28 cruise · HYD 85 · PR-3** ([units.md](units.md)) | The best mobile ears in the game, on the faction whose doctrine is carried by that number and not by a special case. Born to depth: no refit, and PR-3 covers the whole Abyssal band, so nothing on this map crushes them |
| Cantor — the dome | 1 | SIG 35 idle · HYD 80 · **+25 HYD within 1,200 m, capped at 95** ([units.md](units.md)) | The Cantorate's instrument, standing at the gallery face. Structure, not hull; placed, never moved |

### What the watch does not carry

1. **No weapons that matter.** The Submersible is an armed hull and the mission gives it nothing
   to shoot; weapons are locked, as they were struck at Sorrowgate, and for a plainer reason —
   there is no second party. A locked affordance says why it is locked
   ([ui-ux.md](ui-ux.md)); this one says *no party in this water*.
2. **No active sonar**, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10's mission-3 rule, and this is the one
   campaign where the rule is the smaller half of the argument. **The Directorate owns the best
   arrays in the Rift, they are aboard, and they are live.** The abstention is doctrinal: the
   Mouth *returns pings before they should arrive* ([world-map.md](world-map.md)), so pinging it
   is not scouting, it is asking — and the Directorate does not ask. The campaign already shows
   what asking sounds like. The Knights aimed the First Chord down a trench axis in 178 PC, were
   answered, and the First Chapter-House is now half shrine and half hospice with three
   technicians in it who have not spoken since ([world-map.md](world-map.md);
   [timeline.md](timeline.md)). Withholding the button until mission 3 costs this mission nothing
   it wanted.
3. **No economy and nothing to build.** No nodules, no fields, no throttle. The Directorate's
   cohort economy is mission 2's lesson ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §6), and a shift does not
   produce anything.

**The watch begins under Silent Running and the mission never hands the player the toggle.** This
is not the button's teaching mission — Tend is ([mission-tend.md](mission-tend.md) §3), and the
campaigns are playable in either order, so Attendance may not depend on it having been taught.
The shift is authored silent because that is what attending is, and the only thing in the mission
that breaks it breaks it by the depth rules rather than by choice: **ordering a dive breaks
Silent Running, and re-asserting it mid-dive does not buy quiet**
([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §2). The player is not given a switch. The player is given
a decision that happens to throw one.

## 4. Listening, as the Only Verb

The system this mission teaches, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §2: one system, load-bearing by
the last five minutes. The system is **passive listening** — HYD, and where a listener stands —
and it lands in four movements.

1. **The thing being attended is quieter than anything else in the game.** The return carries
   **SIG 3** by the time it reaches the Ninth's head: quieter than a Corvette, quieter than a
   Commune tender, at the floor of the 3–8 band a hull
   under Silent Running emits ([systems-echo.md](systems-echo.md) §6). Nothing in the
   Rift could attend this except a corridor that carries at 1.60 and ears rated 85. **That is the
   Directorate's existence, stated as arithmetic**, and it is why this mission opens the campaign
   rather than describing it.
2. **What HYD buys is resolution, never safety.** Down the axis, an Abyssal Submersible holds the
   return at **contact from 1,230 m and at bearing from 955 m**; inside the Cantorate's dome those
   become 1,320 m and 1,025 m. Off the axis, on a path that leaves the channel and crosses the
   galleries' occluded ground, bearing falls to roughly 800 m. **The whole shift is decided inside
   a band about 275 metres wide** — the gap between hearing that something arrived and being able
   to write down where. Outside it the player has a smudge and nothing to enter; inside it they
   have a transcript line.
3. **Bearing is what a transcript is made of, and classification would not help.** Filing needs
   Tier 2 — direction and rough distance ([systems-echo.md](systems-echo.md) §4) — because a
   directionless smudge records nothing. A listener close enough will reach Tier 3 and Tier 4 on
   the return and **will learn nothing more by it**, because the authored emitter carries a
   position and a depth and no kind and no faction: there is nothing for classification to name.
   That is not a rule this mission adds. It is what the mission format's emitters already are
   (`MissionEmitter`, and the taps of
   [mission-asset-recovery.md](mission-asset-recovery.md) §6), and it happens to be the
   Directorate's taboo rendered exactly: *you may describe it, measure it, dream about it, and
   record the dream; you may not say what it is* ([culture.md](culture.md) §3). The best ears in
   the Rift, pointed at the one thing that does not resolve.
4. **Where a listener stands is three numbers, and one of them is expensive.** North and east are
   free: a silent hull moves at 33 m/s and the shift may spread itself down the axis at no cost
   at all. Depth is not free. Descent is 45 m/s **at a SIG floor of 72**, and ascent is 15 m/s and
   silent ([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §2). The mission's arithmetic is one line:
   **sixty seconds of warning buys nine hundred metres of climb, and bearing reaches nine hundred
   and fifty-five.** A shift can always just barely rise into the next arrival's band, once. It
   can never do it twice, and it can never do it from the floor.

Asset Recovery aims loudness and Tend tends quiet; Attendance does neither. It **spends the
mission not acting**, and the one act available is a dive that is the loudest thing this water has
heard in a century. Same detection maths, third life ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §2, rule 2).

### The SIG budget

**SIG budget: 8** — the lowest in the campaign by a wide margin, and not a ceiling like the
prologue's twenty but a description: it is the top of the Silent Running band, which is what the
watch emits while it is doing its job ([systems-echo.md](systems-echo.md) §6). A shift that never
decides anything sits under it all watch.

It is playtested against a player who exceeds it, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10, because most
will — by dropping silence to cross the axis faster at 28, by diving after the return at 72, by
treating the stalls as a staging area. Exceeding it costs no hull and fails nothing. It is heard,
and here what hears it is a room full of people who were asleep.

## 5. The Silence of the Stalls

The rite's own version of the silence order: **a ceiling of SIG 25 per hull, for the whole
watch**, and its consequence is deafness, never failure ([glossary.md](glossary.md), *Silence
Order*).

Twenty-five is chosen against the hull and not against the fiction. An Abyssal Submersible idles
at 22 and cruises at 28, so **the watch may listen and may not travel**. A hull that drops silence
and holds station is compliant. A hull under way is shoving. A hull descending at 72 is shoving so
loudly that the ledger caps before the dive finishes.

The clauses are Sorrowgate's, restated in the register that invented them
([mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) §4):

1. **The ceiling.** SIG 25, per hull, for the duration. Nothing clamps the player's loudness. It
   is noticed.
2. **The dome.** The Cantorate lends the watch its ears: +25 HYD within 1,200 m of the gallery
   face, capped at 95. The grant, the radius and the cap are the Cantor's, unchanged — this is the
   third mission to use the instrument and **the first in which it is called by its own name.**
   The prologue's civic array was a Cantor borrowed from this faction's technology
   ([mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) §4); here it belongs to the people who built
   it.
3. **Silence-debt.** Every second any watch hull spends over the ceiling adds a second of debt to
   the shift. While the shift is in debt the dome is withdrawn from all four hulls. Debt repays a
   second for a second and caps at **45 seconds**, so one dive cannot black out the rest of the
   watch.

**What the dome is actually worth here, stated because the number is surprising.** On a hull that
already hears at 85, the Cantor's +25 runs into its own cap at 95 and buys **seven per cent of
range** — sixty-nine metres of bearing. The same dome lifts a Corvette from 50 to 75 and is worth
half again as much reach. **The Cantorate's instrument is nearly wasted on the Directorate's own
hull and indispensable to everybody else's**, and the Cantorate's standing rests on it. Nothing in
the mission says so. Korrin has read the number.

So the debt's mechanical price is small and deliberately so, and the mission does not pretend
otherwise. **Its real price is that it is written down.** Silence-debt is informal in three
factions and a written ledger in the fourth ([culture.md](culture.md) §5), and the shift's debt is
read aloud at the close with everything else. The sanction is the record. That is the Directorate
in one mechanism, and it is the same reason the campaign's second mission can be about
reassignment without anybody raising their voice.

**The order does not bind the dome.** The Cantor idles at 35, which is ten over the ceiling the
watch is held to and fifty per cent louder than a hull the rite calls a shove. It is the loudest
thing in the gallery all watch and it is exempt, because the Cantorate does not apply the rule to
itself. The mission does not comment. First Cantor Ossary is present the whole time (§12).

## 6. The Return

**The return comes up the Ninth nine times in the watch. Each arrival is announced from the
stalls sixty seconds before it is heard, and where it will be is not known before that.**

That the arrivals number nine is a measurement, and measuring is the half of the rite that is
permitted. The Ninth Trench is the ninth of nine, the Order keeps nine chapter-houses, and the
Rift is full of nines it does not explain ([world-map.md](world-map.md)). **Nobody in this mission
connects them and neither does this document.** A character may speculate, contradict another, and
be wrong ([culture.md](culture.md) §6); none of them is permitted to be right.

Mechanically an arrival is an authored emitter: a position, a depth, SIG 3, and a window of
twenty seconds. Nine of them, at nine points walking down and back up the axis. Resolve one at
Tier 2 or better from any watch hull and the arrival is **attended** — entered against the stalls'
dream for that window. Resolve it at Tier 1 and there is a smudge and no line. Miss it and the gap
is entered too, which is the transcript's own convention and not a punishment invented for a game.

| # | Time | Where, on the axis | Depth | What it asks of the watch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 01:20 | The head, in the dome | 2,900 m | Nothing. It arrives in the stalls' own water and everyone files it |
| 2 | 03:00 | The head | 3,050 m | Nothing, and the player is starting to believe the mission |
| 3 | 04:40 | Upper channel | 3,250 m | The first arrival that rewards a hull placed south rather than seated |
| 4 | 06:20 | Mid channel | 3,550 m | Reachable from the stalls' band. The last one that is |
| 5 | 08:00 | Lower channel | 3,850 m | Marginal from 3,000 m — 850 m of depth against a 955 m band |
| **6** | **09:40** | **The approach** | **4,100 m** | **Out of reach without a dive.** The first of the two the transcript's last line costs |
| 7 | 11:20 | Mid channel | 3,150 m | The return comes back up, and a watch that followed it down is a thousand metres under it |
| **8** | **13:00** | **The sill** | **4,100 m** | The second. Deepest, furthest, and two minutes before the last |
| 9 | **15:40** | The head | 2,850 m | Highest, nearest the stalls, and **1,250 m of climb from the sill against 900 m of warning.** A watch at the floor cannot have it |

Read rows 6, 8 and 9 together, because they are the mission: **the two arrivals you can only have
by going down are the two that put the last one out of reach.** A watch that never changes depth
attends seven of the nine. A watch that dives well attends all nine and runs a debt doing it. A
watch that chases every arrival attends four, spends the watch in transit and in debt, and is not
in the record.

That is the decision the mission is made of, and it is made once, at about eight minutes, in the
quiet before arrival 6 is called.

## 7. What Is Heard

**Almost nothing, and that is the mission.**

The prologue's first movement is empty on purpose and this one is empty for eighteen minutes. The
Ninth at this depth is the quietest authored water in the bible, and everything in it is diegetic,
true, and none of the player's business to act on:

- **Sufficiency, above and behind.** A Directorate city announces itself by subtraction: the
  ambient hush deepens, and then there are red lights in ordered rows
  ([world-map.md](world-map.md); [art-direction.md](art-direction.md)). The gallery face's mix is
  the city's hush with the stalls inside it — breathing, and the occasional turn of a body in a
  berth.
- **The 9th Trench Cohort, working the floor south.** Adze's formation, the deepest standing
  formation in the Rift, going about a rotation that has nothing to do with the shift. Audible,
  distant, and never approaching. The player's first hour of Directorate life includes learning
  that the loudest friendly thing on the map is also the one that will not be coming.
- **The axis tightening.** The sixty-second precursor, and the only sound in the mission the
  player must act on. It is not the return; it is the corridor changing its mind about how it
  carries, a minute before anything arrives.
- **The return, nine times.** SIG 3, twenty seconds, and it does not sound like an animal, a
  machine or a voice. [audio-direction.md](audio-direction.md)'s inversion is at its most extreme
  here: the quieter the player is, the more of the Rift arrives, and the thing at the bottom of
  that curve is the faintest object in the game.
- **The stalls, dreaming aloud.** Not speech. The attendants are asleep, and the galleries are
  built so that a shift can be heard from the face.

No fauna, no combat, no hazard, no second party. The discipline is refusing every temptation to
put something in this water, in a mission whose entire argument is that there is something in this
water and it will not be classified.

## 8. The Objective

**Attend the watch: hold the stalls quiet, and enter what arrives.**

Three readings of one shift, and the count is the only number that decides which:

### Results

| Result | Condition | The reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The cycle is entered whole** | All nine attended | Korrin: "Nine of nine. The watch went to it. That is recorded, and so is what it cost, and the First Cantor will have the second of those read first." |
| **Sufficient** | Five to eight attended | Ossary: "**You were sufficient.** The gaps are entered as gaps. Nobody has ever attended a whole cycle and the record does not ask anyone to." |
| **Not in the record** | Fewer than five attended | Korrin: "Four lines and a watch's worth of travelling. The shift is not in the record. It is not a failure of yours; it is an absence of ours, and it is the first one since 88 PC." |

**"You were sufficient" is the middle reading and it is the highest praise the register has**
([culture.md](culture.md) §3). It is not sarcastic, it is not a consolation, and a partial outcome
is an outcome ([glossary.md](glossary.md), *Mission Outcome*). A player who sat in the stalls for
eighteen minutes, never moved, and filed seven has played the Directorate exactly right and is
told so in the strongest words the faction owns.

**And the whole cycle costs a breach.** Arrivals 6 and 8 are at 4,100 m and cannot be attended
from the stalls' band, so nine of nine requires the dive, and the dive is a shove in a gallery of
sleeping people. The mission ranks the transcript, because the transcript is what a record is for.
The voices rank the manner, and they disagree: Ossary reads the debt, Korrin reads the count, and
the mission does not say which of them is right. That is [campaign.md](campaign.md) §2 rule 1
holding inside a single faction, one mission before the campaign starts spending it.

**The watch ends at 18:00 and the shift is read as it stands.** A conclusion, not a timer failure
— the same argument Tend makes ([mission-tend.md](mission-tend.md) §8) and the same one
[glossary.md](glossary.md) makes about outcomes. The cycle does not end; the watch does, and the
next trench takes it.

**The failure is specific, and it is audible for the whole mission.**
[campaign.md](campaign.md) §10 asks that no mission fail on a timer and that every failure state
be something a player can hear coming for sixty seconds. Both hold, and not by exemption:

- The count is the failure and the count is on the instrument from the first arrival. A player at
  one attended after five arrivals is hearing the shift fail from the middle of it.
- Every single arrival is announced sixty seconds before it is heard — the mission's telegraph is
  not a rule bolted to the close, it is the rite's own structure, fired nine times.
- The last arrival is at 15:40, two minutes and twenty seconds before the watch ends, and it is
  the one that most often decides between four and five.

**Nothing in this mission can be lost except the record.** No hull can be destroyed, nothing
crushes at any depth the map holds, and there is no party to fight. The stake is a page.

## 10. What It Teaches

One system, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10: **passive listening** — HYD, the tier ladder, and
the fact that a listener's only lever is where it stands. It lands in order across the beat table:
the band (01:20, taught by having it), position along the axis (04:40), the band's edge (08:00),
the decision (08:40), the price of having taken it (11:20), and the last arrival the price is paid
in (15:40).

Underneath it, the campaign's real subject: **patience is a thing the player does.**
[campaign.md](campaign.md) §2 rule 2 says the Directorate campaign teaches patience because the
units are slow, and being told they are patient would not work. This mission does not tell anyone.
It arranges the water so that **doing nothing is sufficient and doing something is a shove**, hands
the player eighteen minutes and one button that is not labelled as a button, and reads the count.

What this mission deliberately does not teach:

- **The cohort economy** — mission 2, *Intake*, where the eight per cent who cannot adapt are
  reassigned and the mission does not editorialise ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §6). Attendance is
  the *why* of that mission played first: the campaign wants the player to have loved one
  Directorate shift before it prices one.
- **Cantors, domes and Chorus Call** — mission 3. The dome is present here as a lent instrument
  and a number the player never places, exactly as the prologue's array was.
- **The shallow-water penalty** — mission 4, *Shallow*, where the most feared army in the Rift
  loses to altitude. Nothing on this map is above 2,750 m and the penalty never fires.
- **Fauna, aggro and Biomass** — mission 5. The Drift is not populated here, and the reason is
  the depth rules rather than the mission's convenience (§1).
- **Active sonar** — §3, and mission 3's rule, and the campaign's oldest argument.

One spawn, at the gallery face: 2500, 1000. The Cantor at 2500, 1000, depth 3,000. **No resources,
no hazard sites, no second spawn, and `fauna` off** — the Drift is not seeded and there is no
authored creature either, which makes this the first mission map in the bible with nothing alive
on it but the player.

Every rectangle lands on the 250 m cell grid and paints exactly the metres it reads
([maps.md](maps.md), "How a map is written").

**The benches are the map's one piece of gameplay geometry, and they are not a fence.** They stand
at 3,200 m against a channel floor of 4,100. A hull that strays off the axis onto a bench is
*lifted* — terrain may raise a hull and may never lower one
([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §2) — and getting back down costs a dive, which costs
seventy-two. So the channel is a corridor for movement for the same reason it is a corridor for
sound, and nothing had to be made solid to say so. The trench walls are doing what trench walls
do.

**The Attending Galleries is a mission map and is not in the public catalogue.** One seat, no
resources, not balanced, resolved by mission id and nothing else ([maps.md](maps.md)).

## 12. The Briefing

Spoken by Undermarshal Setha Korrin at the opening of the watch, after the First Cantor's formula.
The Directorate's register is defined in [culture.md](culture.md) §3: passive, impersonal,
structurally humble, and it does not shorten its sentences.

**First Cantor Vehl Ossary, opening the stalls — 00:00**

> The stalls are open. The cohorts are seated. Nothing is expected of the watch but sufficiency,
> and sufficiency is not a small thing to be expected of.

**Undermarshal Setha Korrin, assigning the watch — 00:00**

> The third watch of the cycle is assigned. It will be attended from the galleries, as it has
> been attended since 88 PC, and the record will receive it as one document with the two watches
> before it.
>
> Four hulls are given to the watch. They are seated, and they are not required to do anything.
> That is not a courtesy. The stalls are where the cohorts dream, and a hull under way in this
> water is a hull standing in front of somebody who is listening.
>
> The return will come up the Ninth nine times. Where it will be is not known before it is heard.
> It is heard sixty seconds before it arrives, which is the whole of the notice anyone has ever
> had. What is heard is entered. What is not heard is not entered, and the gap is entered too.
>
> It may be described. It may be measured. What the cohorts dream of it will be recorded as
> dreamt. It will not be said what it is. A log that says what it is, is amended, and the cohort
> that filed it is re-shifted.
>
> Five of nine is sufficiency. The Undermarshalcy does not round up.

### Objective readings, in play

The Directorate states conditions rather than issuing tasks, and every reading is in the passive
or the impersonal:

- *The watch is seated. The stalls are attended.*
- *The axis is tightening. Sixty seconds. Bearing south, and going down.*
- *Entered: arrival four, bearing and depth.*
- *Not entered: arrival six. The gap is recorded.*
- *Attended: five of nine. Sufficiency is met.*

### The voices in the water

**The stalls, calling an approach — sixty seconds before each arrival**

> Nine-four calls it. South, and deeper than the last. The watch is not asked to move.

**Cohort-Prime Adze, passing below southbound — 07:00**

> This is the floor. It is where I was made for and I am glad of it, which I am told is the part
> people find difficult. If the watch comes down here it will be welcome, and it will be slower
> than it likes, and it will not be able to go back up in time for anything.

**The record, read from the stalls — 17:00**

> Cohort 9-4, stall eleven: water going the wrong way up a stair, and counting, and the counting
> is somebody else's.
>
> Cohort 9-4, stall three: a room with the lights in rows, and the rows are breathing.
>
> Cohort 9-7, stall nineteen: nothing, and then being asked something in a language I do not have,
> and answering it correctly.
>
> Cohort 9-1, stall two: thirty-nine. Then thirty-eight.

**Undermarshal Setha Korrin, at the watch's end — 18:00**

> The reading of the count, per §8, and then one sentence she should not say aloud and does:
> "Six thousand pages, and not one of them a question. Somebody ought to have asked something by
> now."

**First Cantor Vehl Ossary, immediately after — 18:00**

> *Nothing.* The record notes that the First Cantor was present.

The transcript is not evidence and nobody in the mission treats it as any. It is four dreams,
recorded as dreamt, which is what the rite is for; the last of them is the sort of thing the
transcripts have said before and been right about, and the mission neither points that out nor
lets anybody in the water do so ([timeline.md](timeline.md);
[culture.md](culture.md) §6).

Each line fails [culture.md](culture.md) §3 for the other three factions, which is that document's
own test (§6): Ossary's formula makes an expectation out of humility, which the Consortium would
cost and the Knights would make courteous; Korrin's briefing states a penalty for a sentence
rather than for an act, which no other register would think to write down; Adze names their own
happiness as a fact about equipment, which the Commune could not say without turning it into a
question; and Korrin's last line is a complaint about eight generations of her own institution's
discipline, spoken by the person whose job is to enforce it, in front of the person who would have
to act on it — and the Knights, who would agree with every word, could not have stayed quiet
afterwards the way Ossary does.

## 13. Scaffold Status

What exists against this document and what does not, continuing the list
[mission-asset-recovery.md](mission-asset-recovery.md) §13 started and
[mission-tend.md](mission-tend.md) §13 extended — the format finding out, one mission at a time,
what it was missing.

| Requirement | Status |
| --- | --- |
| The mission format — beats, predicates, registry, private rooms | **Built** (#190). `say`, `objective`, `endure`, `quiet` and `resolve` cover most of §9's schedule |
| The silence order — ceiling, debt, the withdrawn dome | **Built**, and reused unchanged: `silenceCeilingSig`, `debtCapS`, `arrayTag` and the `courtSlot` withdrawal are the prologue's, and the Cantor they move is already a Cantor ([mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) §4) |
| The Cantor's dome — +25 HYD, 1,200 m, cap 95 | **Built** — `STRUCTURE_AURAS.CANTOR`. The seven-per-cent finding in §5 is a consequence of the shipped cap and this document's hull, not a change to either |
| Silent Running, and a dive breaking it | **Built** ([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §2) — the SIG floor of 72 on descent, the 45 m/s down and 15 m/s up, and the fact that re-asserting silence mid-dive buys nothing. The mission's whole decision is made of these three and none of them is new |
| PR-3 across the Abyssal, with no floor under it | **Built** — `requiredPressureRating` is band-derived, so nothing on this map crushes a Directorate hull at any depth the map authors. Stated because a reader will ask what 4,100 m costs, and the answer is nothing but time and noise |
| The map, its eight regions, the benches that lift | Not started. Nothing here needs terrain the format cannot author |
| **The arrivals — an emitter that starts and stops at authored ticks** | **Not in the format.** `MissionEmitter` is periodic from tick zero forever, which is right for the taps and wrong for nine one-shot arrivals: all nine would sound at 00:00. The ask is small — a `fromTick`/`untilTick` pair on the emitter, or an emitter-shaped beat — and it is this mission's minimum |
| **The attended count — a tally over the player's own resolution of an authored emitter** | **Not in the format**, and it is the *easy* half of Tend's ask. Tend's filed/unfiled is a fact about what a scripted party heard; this is a fact about what the observer's own force heard, so it stays inside the information-safety wall the predicate union already enforces. A `{ kind: 'attend'; count: number }` predicate, plus a per-arrival tally at Tier 2, is the whole thing |
| **A listening role** | **Not in the format.** `MissionRole` is `'escort'` and `'tender'` — Sorrowgate's two words — and the silence ceiling is measured over the escort set. A shift is neither. The roles want to be authored per mission rather than enumerated once, which is the same finding the other two mission documents arrive at from their own directions |
| **The transcript as a variable close** | **Not built.** `epilogue` is one authored string per `MissionOutcome`, which covers §8's three readings exactly. A close that reads back *which* arrivals were attended, line by line, needs an epilogue that assembles rather than one that is chosen |
| The sixty-second telegraph as the rite's own structure | Ambient authoring and `say` beats; no new mechanism. The existing telegraph test measures the gap between the last loud beat and the resolve, and this mission satisfies it nine times over |
| The Drift, absent | **Built** — `fauna: false`, which the format already carries for a mission whose only creatures are authored. This is the first that authors none |
| In-mission character speech, heard | Text only, the standing status ([mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) §13) |
| The dreams as audio — the stalls, breathing, and a city that announces itself by subtraction | Not started ([audio-direction.md](audio-direction.md)). The mission reads correctly as text and is the one in the bible that reads *right* only with the mix |
