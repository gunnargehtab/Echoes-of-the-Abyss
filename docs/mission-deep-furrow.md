# The Second Seeding 4 — Deep Furrow

> The fourth mission of the Commune campaign ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §5), specified — one of
> the fourteen documents that complete the bible's campaign, written to the pattern
> [mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) sets: everything here is authored — the forces,
> the water, the beats, the numbers and the text — and code transcribes this document.

**Setting:** the Furrow, a cleft under Anholt's terrace where the scar branches beneath the
Rift's north shoulder, 214 PC, four tides after *Convocation* ([world-map.md](world-map.md);
[timeline.md](timeline.md); [characters.md](characters.md)).

**Mission id:** `seeding-deep-furrow` — namespaced by campaign after `seeding-tend`,
`seeding-thin-water` and `seeding-convocation`, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §1.

**Premise, for the entry that offers it:** *The bloom that proved the deep can be gardened is
ten years old and one furrow wide. Today the plateaus carry the Kell seed down to it and plant
the next one.*

**This is the first mission that takes the Commune under the layer, and the first in the bible
that repaints a biome.** Three Commune missions have been played in water the plateau can hear
all of; this one goes 2,200 m down a cleft nobody was born to, and the plateau will not hear a
second of it. [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10 has said since the epic opened that a beat may name
a region and the biome its water becomes, and that no shipped mission does. This document spends
that beat on a garden rather than a dome, and §4 says why the garden is the teaching spend.

## 1. What the Furrow Is

**A cleft under Anholt Plateau, where the scar branches beneath the north shoulder, dropping
from the Mid-Water lanes to a floor at 2,200 m — and the one piece of Abyssal water in the Rift
that nobody was born to.**

The Furrow is new canon, placed here rather than assumed, because a reader of
[world-map.md](world-map.md) should not have to guess where a 2,200 m floor under the plateaus
comes from. §1 of that document calls the Rift "a branched tectonic scar"; §3 steps the plateaus
down the northern rise as Shelf terraces and puts the deep water mid-Rift and south; and
[timeline.md](timeline.md)'s 204 PC entry says the proof was made at 2,200 m. So the proof needed
a 2,200 m floor within a working day of the seed-store — the one dry room on a plateau, whose only
key the Bloomwright holds ([habitats.md](habitats.md) §2) — and the programme found the one place
the scar branches under the shoulder: a cleft below the lanes *Tend* heard from above
([mission-tend.md](mission-tend.md) §7), a doorway no plateau's lane had ever gone down. Ten years
ago Sefa Anholt seeded one furrow of its floor. It grew, it has been tended since whatever any
plateau was turning, and today it is a green terrace on bare rock, lit from its own pigment, one
row wide. §13 says the gazetteer should gain the entry.

Three facts about that water decide the whole mission, and none of them is a fence.

**The furrow is under the layer.** The thermocline sits at 1,200 m with its duct a hundred
metres either side ([systems-echo.md](systems-echo.md) §3), and here it lies across the cleft's
upper water: the Foot, where the plateau's lane comes down, is at 900 m and above it; the garden
is at 2,200 m and below it; the sill is at 2,600. Sound crossing the layer is multiplied by 0.3.
The plateau's whole information economy — hear everything, be heard nowhere
([mission-tend.md](mission-tend.md) §1) — is switched off at the duct. Home cannot hear the
furrow and the furrow cannot hear home: §3's deep field, lived for the first time by the faction
whose entire safety was hearing.

**Seeded ground is quiet ground and rated ground.** The ten-year furrow is a trench floor
painted Kelp Forest — biome is acoustics, not band, the freedom
[mission-asset-recovery.md](mission-asset-recovery.md) §11 took for a scar and this document
points at a garden — so a hull idling on it at 8 carries 4.4 where the bare cleft beside it
would carry 12.8. And it holds a PR-2 hull in PR-3 water. That is the Deepbloom conversion
[systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §3 has described since the document existed — "Deepbloom
structures slowly convert Abyssal tiles to habitable ground; they don't survive the deep, they
change it" — and §4 is where it is written down as a rule.

**The cleft is Hollow country, and the garden is below it.** A Hollow works trench walls at
1,700 m and pursues within 1,250–2,150 m ([bestiary.md](bestiary.md) §4). The furrow is at
2,200 m: fifty metres under the floor of the last band that hunts. A Draymaw's band stops at
1,300 m, a Rasp's at 1,300, and there is no Sounder on this map. The bestiary's own line is that
"depth is cover from some of the Drift and exposure to the rest"; at 2,200 m in this cleft it is
cover from all of it, which no entry in the bestiary is given and a seeding buys.

Nobody on the working day says any of this. It is the ground the mission stands on.

## 2. Whose Hulls the Player Commands

**The player commands the working day the plateaus send down to the furrow: three tenders, one
of them carrying the Kell seed, and the programme's two proof scouts.**

The programme has six deep-rated hulls and has never had a seventh: two scouts refit in 204 PC
for the proof, and four seed hulls — the barge and three tenders — refit for the seeding. Deep
Furrow fields the two. The four are in the pressure lab's yard for the seeding, and the tenders
on the water today are the plateau's ordinary ones, unrefit, PR-2 on the hull. That is the
argument, not a shortage: a garden that needs a refit hull to stand on it is a refit, and the
whole of Anholt's claim — that the deep can be made available to everybody
([characters.md](characters.md)) — is tested by whether an ordinary tender lives at 2,200 m
because the ground holds it.

**Nobody commands the day.** Tidespeaker Ysolde Marr speaks the briefing at the cleft's mouth,
at 900 m — the last water above the layer and the last place she can be heard from — and orders
nobody to do anything, because she cannot. Bloomwright Sefa Anholt is in the furrow, aboard the
second proof scout, the programme's own hull, and speaks twice. Ottilie Marr crews the sower. The
watch calls what it hears. Warden Juno Teel is not here, and neither is the concern: the
Consortium was the Commune's foil in the first three missions, the Directorate is its foil in the
fourth and fifth, and this mission keeps to that.

**Engine bound, stated so nobody corrects it into a bug.** The working day carries the Commune
faction value; the observer at the sill carries the Directorate's. The court slot is reserved
and empty, as every literal reserves it. The Drift is not a party: two Hollows, three jelly
clusters and a Draymaw pack are authored creatures on the Drift's own slot, and `fauna` is off.
Two factions, one mission, no lobby — a mission seats its own parties
([mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) §2 established the pattern).

### What the day does not carry

1. **No weapons.** Not struck, as the court struck them — simply not grown, the force list
   [mission-tend.md](mission-tend.md) §2 established. The five ordnance locks carry one reason
   in register: *not grown — a garden's watch is ears, and a garden's freight is seed*.
2. **No construction.** Locked, with the reason: *nothing is built on a furrow; it is sown*.
   There is no Bastion, no economy and no draw; the day earns nothing, because the work today is
   being somewhere.
3. **No throttle**, for the fourth Commune mission running and for
   [mission-tend.md](mission-tend.md) §3's reason: the Commune's working figures are bred into
   the equipment. The sowing's 45 is authored on the sounding, not set on a lever.
4. **Active sonar, available and priced.** This is mission 4, the button arrived in mission 3
   ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §10), and it is not locked. A ping's aggro contribution is tripled
   ([bestiary.md](bestiary.md) §2), so 95 × 3 through the cleft's 1.6 is Commit-loud to a
   Hollow's HYD 80 from **1,434 m** — the throat is 1,500 m wide, so a ping anywhere in it is
   Commit-loud to both walls at once — and the self-reveal hands the sill a Track from anywhere
   under the layer (§7). The plateaus have never asked the deep anything. The button is on the
   panel anyway.

**Silent Running is present, and this mission prices it in both directions.** It **stops the
sowing outright**: a sounding needs a hull that is not silent (`runtime.ts`, `holdingSounding`),
so a sower that goes quiet on the rock loses the hold and keeps paying for the rock. On the dive
it is the wrong button and does nothing: an order deeper than the hull clears silence
(`match.ts`, `applyDepth`) and the descent floors SIG at 72 regardless. On the climb it is the
right button and costs only speed: a shallower order leaves silence standing, ascent adds no SIG,
and the Commune's multiplier is 0.8 — a silent tender climbs the doorway at 32 m/s reading 4.5
by the shipped curve, a silent scout at 96 reading 3.5.

## 4. The Sowing

The system this mission teaches, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §2: one system, introduced in
the first three minutes and load-bearing by the last five. The system is **seeding — the
Commune's terraforming, and the +1 PR zone it writes** — and it lands in four movements.

### 1. The ground holds

A Harvester is PR-2. At 2,200 m the water requires PR-3 (`requiredPressureRating`; the line is
1,800 m), and a hull one band under its rating pays **4 × deficit² = 4 HP a second**, unhealable,
to zero ([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §2; `crushAttritionPerSecond`). Three hundred
points of hull is seventy-five seconds.

Inside the furrow it pays nothing. The standing furrow is a **region pressure grant** — every
hull inside its rectangle operates at PR + 1, exactly as a Sounding Spire's aura grants
`Pressure.bonus` within 600 m of it, and evaporating the same way when a hull leaves. The day
dives from the lanes into the garden in the first two minutes and the tenders do not crush
there, because the ground holds them. That is the system introduced, by the water doing it,
before anybody says a word about it.

**Which mechanism this is, stated so nobody builds the other one.** It is
[systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §3's Deepbloom conversion — "Deepbloom structures slowly
convert Abyssal tiles to habitable ground" — written by a sowing as a grant on a region that
stays. It is **not** §4's *Seeding* ability — a 400 m radius, +1 PR for sixty seconds — which is
a commander ability with no grant mechanism behind it
([mission-convocation.md](mission-convocation.md) §13), neither built nor asked for here. A zone
that lasted a minute would be a stunt; a furrow is somewhere people sleep.

### 2. The water absorbs

The furrow is Kelp Forest paint at PF 0.55 on a trench floor whose bare rock carries at 1.6.
Against the observer's HYD 85 (§6), the same idle tender at 8:

| Ground | SIG × PF | Contact | Bearing | Classification | Track |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The furrow, kelp | 8 × 0.55 | 1,166 m | 905 m | 658 m | 490 m |
| The bare cleft beside it | 8 × 1.6 | 2,272 m | 1,764 m | 1,282 m | 955 m |

The garden is half as audible as the rock it grows on, in every tier. *Tend* taught that the
plateau's hush is partly grown ([mission-tend.md](mission-tend.md) §4); this is the same fact at
2,200 m, with nothing but pigment doing it.

### 3. The sowing writes both

The second furrow is bare rock at 00:00 — Abyssal Trench, 1.6, PR-3 water. It is sown by a
**sounding**: the sower holds within 250 m of the point 2625, 2125, bow on it, not silent, for
**sixty seconds at SIG 45** — the working figure of a Standard cut ([economy.md](economy.md)
§3), the figure the jelly lift was authored at in [mission-tend.md](mission-tend.md) §9 — and a
broken hold resets to zero (`accrueSounding`). The point is chosen so that the hold's whole
radius lies outside the standing furrow's grant: the sower stands at x ≥ 2,375 and the bed's
aura ends at x 2,300. Every second of the sowing is paid for on unseeded ground at four points of
hull.

The arithmetic, said here so nobody does it for the first time on the rock:

| | Seconds | Hull |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The walk from the bed's edge to the hold's edge, 75 m at 40 m/s | 1.9 | 7.5 |
| **The hold** | **60** | **240** |
| The walk back under the bed | 1.9 | 7.5 |
| Left, of three hundred | | **45** |
| A hold broken once, and begun again | 60 more | none left |
| A sower that climbs instead of walking back west — 400 m to the line at 15 m/s | 26.7 | 107, and it is dead at eighteen hundred |

When the hold completes, **the ground beat fires**: the second furrow's water turns from Abyssal
Trench to Kelp Forest on one tick, and — once the row lands — the region gains its grant. The
water over the sown ground goes from carrying to absorbing in the same instant the observer's
Track on the sowing falls to a Bearing on a garden. That is the campaign's one biome spend, and
the teaching one: every other repaint the bible has proposed is a dome coming down, and this is
the only one that makes ground *better*.

### 4. The layer is the other wall

Home cannot hear the furrow. The Foot's ears have the observer's idle at Tier 1 only inside
1,785 m across the layer, and the sill is 2,500 m off; the day learns what is at the sill only by
going under the layer to it, and learns in the same second that the sill has had the day since
the dive began — Classification from its first second (72 through 1.6 and 0.3 is ratio 3.01 at
2,125 m) and Track from its fifth, once the hull is in the duct and the pair factor is 1.0
(ratio 10.0). The layer does not hide the day from the deep. It hides the deep from home, and
the plateau has never once been on that side of the arrangement.

### Where you dive is the whole lesson

Descent is 45 m/s at a SIG floor of 72; ascent is 15 m/s and silent
([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §2). Three facts about this cleft turn that rule into a
route:

- **The floors step, and terrain never lowers a hull.** The Foot's floor is 900 m, the cleft's
  1,800, the furrow's 2,200. A hull cannot dive below the ground under it, so the descent is
  two legs: 900 → 1,750 m over the cleft's mouth, 18.9 s, and 1,750 → 2,200 m over the furrow,
  10 s — 28.9 s of loud, and the run down the throat between them at 1,750 m, quiet. Not 1,800.
  The cleft's floor is exactly the Abyssal line, `depthBandFor(1800)` is Abyssal, and a PR-2
  hull run along the floor pays four a second for the whole throat.
- **A dive is stood still.** A hull that dives on the move carries 72 down the throat with it
  at 40 m/s — 750 m of loud hull sliding past two ambushes. The mouth is where there is room.
- **The strike is a sphere.** A Hollow coils at Interest (45) and strikes only at Commit (70)
  *and* within 500 m in three dimensions ([bestiary.md](bestiary.md) §4). Against a dive at 72
  through 1.6 that is coil from 800 m and Commit-loud from 607 m, so the sphere is the whole
  strike. The walls' Hollows sit at x 1,350 and 2,650: a dive at the mouth's edge, 820 m from
  either, is not even coiled (ratio 43.3); a dive down the throat's middle, 650 m from both,
  coils both and is struck by neither; a dive inside 500 m of a wall is bitten at 55 a second
  for as long as it stays loud. And from 15:00 the Hollows are in the middle (§9), so the
  corridor turns inside out: the walls are the quiet way up and the middle is theirs.

### The SIG budget

**SIG budget: 45** — a working level, the sowing's own figure, and the loudest the Commune
campaign has authored: *Tend*'s twenty and *Convocation*'s twenty-six were ceilings, *Thin
Water*'s thirty a ceiling the column broke by firing. This one is the number the day is *for*.
It is playtested, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10, against the player who exceeds it: the one
who dives in the throat, the one who dives on the move, and the one who pings the cleft to find
out what is on the walls and is answered by both of them.

do anything, and today it does two things: it enters what it hears, and it goes below to say
so.**

is somebody's by birth, that the plateaus were told so in writing because the plateaus keep

## 8. The Objective

**Sow the second furrow, and be somewhere when the tide turns.**

Three rows, all of them queries over the player's own hulls, as the union requires:

| Objective | Predicate | Terminal | Revealed | Marker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

**sown.** Text: *The second furrow wants sowing. Sixty seconds on the bare rock at the working
figure, and the sower's hull is seventy-five. We're saying both numbers here so nobody has to
do the sum down there.* Met: *Two furrows. The second one sounds like the first one now, which
is to say like home.* Unmet: *One furrow, the way it was this morning. The seed's still aboard
or it's on the rock, and we're not going to say which is worse.* The predicate is `sound` over
the one authored sounding of §4; it is monotone, it names no hull, and it is met the tick the
hold completes.

**tended.** Text: *The furrow is home tonight, for anyone who'd rather. We're not saying stay.
We're saying it holds.* Met: *Two of ours were still at twenty-two hundred metres when the
doorway woke, and it's a garden.* Unmet: *Nobody slept below. The doorway was open both ways and
we came up through it.* The region is both furrows together, 1250, 1750, 1500, 750, so a day
that sleeps in the old furrow and a day that has moved into the new one are the same answer.

**The reveal is the row's whole honesty, and it is stated rather than tidied.** `extract` is
not a standing predicate — `predicates.ts` lists `quiet` and `survive` and nothing else — and
the runtime never re-derives a Met non-standing row (`runtime.ts`, `deriveObjectives`). A
*tended* revealed at 00:00 would latch Met at about 02:00, the moment two tenders first entered
the garden, and would read "it's a garden" at the tide over a day that went home at 15:30. So
the row is revealed at **15:30**, on the beat that releases the Hollows into the throat, and is
scored from that tick to the tide — the roll idiom [mission-intake.md](mission-intake.md) §9
built, where a finding is filed in the last minutes or not at all. The residual is real: a day
still in the garden at 15:30 that climbs at 16:00 reads met, and the tide's business is the
tide's. A day that leaves the garden at 15:54 is home by 18:00 — thirty seconds to the top of
the garden's water, thirty-nine up the throat silent, fifty-seven to the Foot — so the residual
is a twenty-four-second window in which a plateau can be read as having stayed and be gone.
Every other reading of the row is worse, and §13 says which.

**the-day.** Text: *Three went down. We'd like three read, whichever water they're read in.*
Met: *Three. Sixteen aboard, by household.* Unmet: *Fewer than three. The cleft has walls and
the walls have a schedule, and one of ours found it.* Non-terminal, with a reading: it prints
beneath whichever outcome the day earned and touches the ladder not at all — Intake's
neutrality guard, spent here so the count of people never becomes a score.

**The mission runs its length.** Both terminal rows can be met at 15:30 — a day that sowed by
09:00 and is in the garden when *tended* is revealed meets both on that tick — and the court's
rule would close the mission there, before the Hollows have gone quiet, before the watch has
said there is room, and three minutes before the tide. `runsItsLength` is authored, for
[mission-intake.md](mission-intake.md) §13's reason: only the `resolve` at 18:00 closes the day,
and the day is read where it stands.

## 10. What It Teaches

One system, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10: **seeding** — the zone, the paint, the sowing
that writes both, and the layer that is the other wall — in the four movements of §4, landing
in order across the beat table: the ground holding (~02:00, taught by three ordinary tenders
not crushing), the water absorbing (03:00, heard before anybody says so), the sowing (~09:00,
paid for in hull on rock that carries), and the night (15:30 to the tide), where the system is
load-bearing — because without the zone the last five minutes are a crush ledger, and with it
they are a choice about where home is.

**Underneath it, the campaign's subject continued.** *Tend* taught that quiet is a livelihood;
*Thin Water* taught what open water does to it; *Convocation* taught that the arrangement cannot
decide anything faster than people can walk. Deep Furrow takes the same quiet people below the
one line their whole safety was built on — the Shelf, above every hunter — and finds that for
the first time in the campaign the Commune is safe by depth rather than by shallowness. It is
genuinely beautiful. It is genuinely a provocation. Both are heard by the one listener on the
map, in the same second, and the mission never says which it is.

- **The Spore Veil as an instrument** — mission 5, [mission-in-writing.md](mission-in-writing.md),

### The reading at the tide's turn

The close reads one of three, verbatim from §8, in Marr's voice, carried up the cleft by the
watch because the mouth cannot hear the furrow; *the-day*'s reading prints beneath it, met or
unmet.

| **Partial** | "One of the two. Either it's planted and nobody stayed, or it isn't and everyone did, and we've been both of those things before at three hundred metres. This is the first time we've been either at two thousand two hundred." |
| **Lost** | "The rock's still bare and the garden's empty tonight. If anybody paid for that, they paid it on the ledger that doesn't heal. We agreed the number in daylight. The number was seventy-five and it's still seventy-five." |

document's own test (§6). Marr's briefing states an arithmetic and a preference and gives no

Ottilie counts people and seed in one breath and refuses to round either, which the Consortium
has no field for; the observer's two lines are the passive doing the whole work — nothing is
threatened, nothing is claimed, a sentence is kept — and no other register keeps a sentence for
nine years without acting on it; and Marr's last line is sentiment stated beside opposition as
one fact, the Consortium's worst insult spent on purpose by the person who will be charged for
both halves.

### One question this document does not settle

**Whether a furrow is a region or a structure.** [systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §3 says
*Deepbloom structures* convert Abyssal ground; this document writes the conversion as a grant
on a region with no structure in it, because the format has no beat that spawns a structure
and a sowing that raised one would be a build order in a mission whose construction is locked.
The other shape is a Deepbloom structure kind — a bed that grants PR the way a Spire does, sown
rather than built, with no hum — and a repaint that follows it rather than a region row that
carries it. Both serve this mission. They diverge in *In Writing*, where beds already stand
over the furrows and a Directorate cohort corrects them, and at the rim, where the sown lip is
asked to hold a hull with no structure standing on it. It is a decision for whoever builds the
row above, and it is recorded here rather than made here, the way
[mission-standing-wave.md](mission-standing-wave.md) §13 records whether the node is the Spire.
