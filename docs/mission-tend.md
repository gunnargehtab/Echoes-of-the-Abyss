# The Second Seeding 1 — Tend

> The first mission of the Commune campaign ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §5), specified. The
> third mission document the bible carries, to the pattern
> [mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) sets: everything here is authored — the
> forces, the water, the beats, the numbers and the text — and code transcribes this document.

**Setting:** Marr Plateau, the Rift's north shoulder, 214 PC ([world-map.md](world-map.md);
[characters.md](characters.md)).

**Mission id:** `seeding-tend` — namespaced by campaign, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §1.

---

## 1. What a Working Day Is

Marr Plateau is one of the great garden terraces: kelp columns forty metres tall, farm rows
marked in bioluminescent trail, the pale ceiling of the Lid glowing overhead at midday — the
closest thing to a sky anyone in the Rift has stood under ([world-map.md](world-map.md)). It is
the quietest inhabited water in the setting, and this mission is one tide of its ordinary work.

**Nothing attacks the player in this mission**, and the campaign row says so in advance because
the design depends on the player slowly believing it. Three facts about the water make it true,
and none of them is a fence:

**The Drift cannot climb here.** The plateau's working water is 250–320 m. A Draymaw pack's
pursuit band tops out at 500 m, a Hollow's country is the trench walls, and a Sounder's is the
deep basins ([bestiary.md](bestiary.md) §4). The pack the player will hear working the foot of
the drop all day is real, is exactly what it sounds like, and physically cannot arrive. The
Shelf's safety from the Drift's predators is not this mission being gentle — it is the depth
rules doing what they always do, and it is why the Rift's food grows where it grows.

**The drop carries.** The plateau's southern edge falls away toward the Mid-Water lanes, and
the bare slope carries sound the way bare rock does. Everything that moves on the lanes below
is audible from the gardens minutes before it matters — which is the other half of the
plateau's safety, and the mission's whole information economy. Quiet ground beside loud ground:
the Commune heard everything and are heard nowhere, and one tide of working that arrangement
teaches the faction better than any brief could.

**What listens does not shoot. It files.** The Consortium runs a charted survey along the drop
lane today, under grid right-of-way, entirely legally. What its instruments hear, its ledgers
keep — and a garden in a ledger is halfway to being an asset. The stake of this mission is not
a hull. It is whether the day ends *unfiled*, and the campaign will spend six more missions on
what that word was protecting.

Nobody on the plateau says any of this. It is the ground the mission stands on.

## 2. Whose Hulls the Player Commands

**The player commands Marr Plateau's working day: three tenders and two watch scouts, grown by
the plateau, crewed by the people whose plateau it is.**

The Commune does not field a mission force; it *is* one, on days when the work needs doing
together. The tenders are the bloom's own freight — Harvester hulls in the Commune manner,
chitin and pigment, dredge gear grown for lifting rather than cutting. The watch is two Light
Scouts, the Commune's own hull, whose sensor suite is most of the ship
([units.md](units.md)).

**The watch is unarmed because the day is.** No hull in the player's force carries a weapon —
not struck ceremonially, as the court struck them ([mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md)
§3), but simply not grown, because a garden's watch is ears. This is the Commune's doctrine
stated as a force list: they lose any fight they did not choose ([factions.md](factions.md)),
and the campaign's second mission is about exactly that. The first is about the life that
arrangement buys.

**Engine bound, stated so nobody corrects it into a bug.** The player's force and the plateau
carry the Commune faction value; the survey pair carries the Consortium's. Two factions, one
mission, no lobby — a mission seats its own parties ([mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md)
§2 established the pattern).

## 3. The Working Force

| Hull | Count | Stats | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tender | 3 | Harvester hull, Commune-grown · **SIG 18 working** / 8 idle · HYD 30 · PR-1 ([units.md](units.md); [economy.md](economy.md) §6) | The bloom's freight. 18 is the Commune's whole economic identity in one number — the figure everyone else harvests at 50 against — and this mission is where the player learns to feel the difference rather than read it |
| Light Scout — the watch | 2 | SIG 6 idle / 12 cruise · **HYD 70** · PR-1 | The best mobile ears outside the Directorate. The watch's job is the mission's verb: listen down the drop, and give the plateau its minutes of warning |

### What the force does not carry

1. **No weapons** — §2. Nothing in this mission is solved by shooting and nothing shoots, so
   the force list makes it a property of the data, the prologue's own argument.
2. **No active sonar.** [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10 withholds the ping until mission 3, and
   the Commune's diegetic form is the simplest of the four: the plateau has never owned one.
   An instrument whose entire function is to be heard is not a thing a garden grows.
3. **No throttle.** The tenders' working figure is authored, not set — the bloom is brought in
   at the pace the bloom offers, and the four-position lever stays out of the player's hands
   here for the same reason it stayed out of Asset Recovery's: it is another faction's lesson
   ([mission-asset-recovery.md](mission-asset-recovery.md) §3), and the Commune's economy is
   not a machine with a volume knob. Their answer to "how loud should the work be" is a
   number they bred into the equipment.

**Silent Running is present, priced, and taught — this is the mission the button was saved
for.** The prologue deliberately withheld it: on a hull with no weapons, no shields and no
dredge, the toggle costs a Commune hull a little speed and nothing else, and a trade with no
cost teaches nothing ([mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) §4). A tender is the hull
where the cost arrives: Silent Running **stops the work** — cannot mine, build or repair
([systems-echo.md](systems-echo.md) §6) — so going silent spends the day itself. SIG falls to
single digits, the share stops accruing, and the tide does not wait. Quiet, which the player
has had all mission for the price of being Commune, turns out to have an absolute form with a
real invoice, and the mission presents the invoice twice (§9).

## 4. Quiet, as an Economy

The system this mission teaches, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §2: one system, load-bearing by
the last five minutes. The system is the Commune's — **the quiet economy**, the Veil lived in
rather than fought from ([factions.md](factions.md)) — and it lands in four movements:

1. **Income without a broadcast.** Bloom-share yields continuously, without a harvester loop,
   provided the plateau is theirs ([economy.md](economy.md) §6). The tenders carry the share
   home at 18; the loudest routine object in the player's whole economy is quieter than every
   other faction's idle. The player spends the first minutes waiting for the catch, and the
   catch is not acoustic — it is that this income is anchored to the most reachable ground on
   the map, which is mission 2's subject.
2. **Quiet buys hearing.** The mix inversion the prologue taught as a rule
   ([audio-direction.md](audio-direction.md)) becomes a standard of living: a force this quiet
   hears *everything*. The working day comes with the whole Shelf audible — Teel's day to the
   east, the pack at the foot of the drop, the lanes below — and the mission's one loud task
   (§9, the jelly lift) takes it all away for ninety seconds, so the player learns by brief
   contrast what they have been standing in.
3. **Quiet is tended, not possessed.** The plateau's hush is partly *grown*: Tetherjelly
   clusters lower local PF by 0.10 ([bestiary.md](bestiary.md) §4), and the Commune farms
   them. Re-seating the west lane's jellies is one of the day's tasks — the player spends part
   of the mission literally gardening the acoustics — and the doc states the design intent
   plainly: masking terrain, on this ground, is a crop.
4. **Quiet has an absolute, and the absolute has a price.** Silent Running, §3. The share
   stops. The day shortens. The player pays it anyway, twice, because of what is listening —
   and because sound persists: the work leaves an industrial hum that a listener with HYD ≥ 40
   reads long after the hulls go still ([systems-echo.md](systems-echo.md) §7), so stillness
   is only clean if it starts *early*. That is why the plateaus go still minutes before a
   sweep and not seconds, why nobody has to order it, and why the watch's four minutes of
   warning are the mission's most valuable cargo.

Asset Recovery is this mission's mirror, and the two are designed to be played in either
order: the Consortium learns that loudness is survivable and aims it; the Commune learns that
quiet is a livelihood and tends it. Same detection maths, opposite lives
([campaign.md](campaign.md) §2, rule 2).

### The SIG budget

**SIG budget: 20** — a ceiling again, the prologue's number with a livelihood attached instead
of a courtesy. The working day is tuned to expect the player under it: tenders at 18, the
watch at 12, the one authored loud task scheduled and finite. It is playtested against a
player who exceeds it, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10, because most will — by cruising the
watch when it should drift, by lifting jellies while the sweep is inbound, by treating the
gift run as a delivery instead of a visit. Exceeding it costs no hull and fails nothing. It is
simply *heard*, and what hears it keeps books.

## 6. The Sweep

**Two Consortium corvettes run the drop lane twice today, charting. They do not deviate, they
do not threaten, and they hear at 50 ([units.md](units.md)) — which is enough to read a
working garden, and enough to read yesterday's hum off the seabed if the stillness starts
late.**

The survey is the mission's pressure, and it is pressure of a kind no other campaign opens
with: patient, legal, and incapable of being fought because there is nothing to fight. The
Consortium is not wrong to chart a lane it holds right-of-way on. The Commune is not wrong to
go still while it passes. The ledger is not malice; it is what the concern *is* — and a garden
entered in it acquires the one property no garden survives: a value someone else can cite.
Nobody is wrong, which is the campaign rule, load-bearing as ever
([campaign.md](campaign.md) §2, rule 1).

Mechanically the sweep is a scripted transit with ears. While either corvette can resolve a
player hull or a fresh work-mark above the plateau's ambient, the mission counts it — quietly,
without interrupting anything — and the pair's course bends a few degrees toward what it
heard, which is the only feedback the player gets and the only feedback the fiction permits.
The plateau's answer, practised for two centuries, is the **stillness**: work stops early,
hulls drift, and the hum decays before the instruments arrive. Nobody orders it. The briefing
tells the player to watch how everybody does it anyway.

**The stillness is the silence order's older, softer sibling** — the court counts silence in
seconds because Sorrowgate is where the Rift's courtesy became procedure
([mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) §4); the plateaus never wrote it down. The
campaign teaches the formal rule first and the living custom second, and a player who took the
prologue's lesson will recognise this one the moment the watch calls the sweep.

## 7. What Is Heard

**Everything. That is the mission.**

The working day is scored by the Shelf itself, and every sound in it is diegetic, true, and
none of the player's business to act on:

- **The pack at the foot of the drop** — a Draymaw pack working the lane traffic's leavings at
  900 m, audible all day, physically unable to climb ([bestiary.md](bestiary.md) §4). The
  player's first hour of Commune life includes learning that a predator you can always hear
  and never meet is the sound of safety, not of threat — the exact inversion of what the
  prologue's basin taught, on purpose.
- **Teel's day**, east: another plateau's work, faint and ordinary. When it goes still,
  mid-mission, the player's water is about to go still too — the plateaus hear each other's
  hush travel ahead of the sweep like weather.
- **The lanes below**: freight on the grid corridors, far off, the concern's ordinary thunder.
  The world the Commune lives above, priced per decibel.
- **Lampfry through the gardens** — the scatter tell, the one piece of information a silent
  hull cannot suppress ([bestiary.md](bestiary.md) §4). In this mission it is texture; in
  mission 5 it is the whole fight.
- **The turning, arriving** — §9, 15:00. The only sound in the mission that is aimed at the
  player, and it is a question.

No Sounder, no combat, no hazard event. The mission's discipline is refusing every temptation
to *add a threat* to a design whose entire point is that the threat is a filing cabinet.

## 8. The Objective

**Bring the day home: the share in, the jellies seated, the gift carried — and the plateau
unfiled.**

The first three are the work, and they are genuinely completable by an unhurried player. The
fourth is the lesson, and it is graded by everything §6 counts: the day ends **unfiled** if the
sweep resolved no working hull and no fresh mark on either pass, and **filed** otherwise.

**The tide ends at 16:00, and the day is read as it stands.** This is a conclusion, not a
timer failure — [glossary.md](glossary.md), *Mission Outcome*: a partial outcome is an outcome,
read as what it is, never scored as a soft failure the player is asked to replay. Work not
done is share not brought in, and the reading says so without punishing it, because the
mission's other currency — stillness — is *supposed* to spend the day. A player who went still
early and often, and finished two loads instead of three, played the Commune correctly, and
the reading tells them so.

**Tend cannot be failed. Stated flatly so it stays true.** The precedent is the prologue's own
("the silence order cannot fail this mission"), and the argument is [campaign.md](campaign.md)
§10 read honestly: failure states must be audible sixty seconds out, and a mission whose only
threat is a ledger has no failure to make audible — it has outcomes, and it has consequences
that arrive later, which is what a ledger *is*. The campaign intends the filed/unfiled result
to reach forward: mission 2's column is caught in the open by Consortium heavies, and a filed
Tend changes *Thin Water*'s briefing — the concern moves on charted water — never its mission.
That is [campaign.md](campaign.md) §1's briefing-variant rule given its first concrete pair,
and it depends on progression tracking that does not exist (§13).

### Results

| Result | Condition | Marr's reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The day held** | Share in, jellies seated, gift landed, unfiled | "The day came back the way we lent it — quiet, fed, unfiled. We'd like you to remember what that felt like. We think you're going to need it." |
| **The day was spent** | Work incomplete, unfiled | "Less came in than the bloom offered, and the quiet is where it went. We've paid that price on purpose for two hundred years. We're still not sure the concern understands it *is* a price." |
| **Filed** | The sweep resolved the gardens on either pass | "The sweep heard us. Nobody was hurt, and we'd rather you didn't learn to think of it that way. A ledger is patient. We will be meeting that entry again." |

Filed and unfiled cross with the work freely — a filed day with the share in is read with both
sentences, and the mission does not pretend one cancels the other. The Commune closes nothing
([culture.md](culture.md) §3); its readings are positions, still turning.

## 10. What It Teaches

One system, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10: **the quiet economy** — quiet as income,
hearing, crop and absolute, in the four movements of §4, landing in order across the beat
table: the share (00:00), the lift's contrast (04:30), the jellies (04:30, the same act), the
stillness and the button (06:30), and the second invoice with the gift under way (11:30),
which is where the system is load-bearing.

What this mission deliberately does not teach:

- **Losing** — mission 2, *Thin Water*, where the arrangement this day runs on meets a column
  of Consortium heavies in open water. Tend is the *why* of that mission, played first: the
  campaign wants the player to have had one whole ordinary day before it prices one.
- **Convocation and mass mobility** — mission 3, Marr's own ability, in the one situation her
  office exists for. Specified in [mission-convocation.md](mission-convocation.md), on this same
  plateau and this same map literal: the rows the player spends today working are the rows the
  vote is walked along.
- **The Spore Veil, seeding, and the bloom as an instrument** — missions 4 and 5. This
  mission shows the bloom only as a livelihood; the campaign's argument is that the player
  should love the garden before anyone asks them to plant one as a provocation.
- **The throttle** — §3. Authored out, as in Asset Recovery, and for the Commune perhaps
  permanently: a lever their economy is built on not needing.

## 12. The Briefing

Spoken by Tidespeaker Ysolde Marr at dawn tide. The Commune's register is defined in
[culture.md](culture.md) §3, and its refusal of the imperative mood makes this briefing
genuinely harder to parse than an order would be. [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10 says that is
the point, and this document agrees in writing.

> We're not going to tell you what to do today. That isn't the arrangement, and today of all
> days the arrangement is the point.
>
> The bloom is ready on the north gardens and the share wants bringing in — we think three
> loads is a day. The west lane's jellies have walked in the current again, the way they do,
> and the lane is louder than we like it. And Teel's landing took the storm badly last tide.
> We have bread that remembers being grain. Somebody could carry it over, if they were going
> that way.
>
> The concern is running the drop today, charting. They call it a survey, and it is one. What
> their instruments hear, their ledgers keep, and a garden in a ledger is halfway to being an
> asset. When the sweep comes up the lane, the plateaus go still. Nobody orders that. Watch
> how everybody does it anyway.
>
> Nothing out there means you harm. We'd like the day back the way we're lending it to you:
> quiet, fed, and unfiled.

### Objective readings, in play

The Commune cannot command, so its objectives arrive as statements of what the day holds, and
the player learns to hear the ask inside them:

- *The share wants bringing in. We think three loads is a day.*
- *The jellies would sit better off the lane. It's loud work; the water's ours for another
  hour.*
- *Teel's landing is short of bread. We're not asking. We're saying it's short.*
- *The sweep is on the drop. The plateaus are going still now.*
- *That's the turning, arriving. Nobody has to answer tonight.*

### The voices on the water

**The watch, calling the sweep — 06:00**

> Two on the lane, coming up the chart. Four minutes, we'd say. Teel's people have gone quiet
> already — you can hear the hush walking.

**Warden Juno Teel, at the landing — 13:00**

> You tend well, and we'll eat well, and I'm grateful. Now hear me on one thing: the storm
> wasn't the only weather that's coming up that drop, and bread won't be what the next one is
> short of.

**The turning, carried — 15:00**

> The plateaus are asked to turn a second seeding. Anholt asks it. The count will wait for
> every garden, and the water will not.

**Tidespeaker Ysolde Marr, at the tide's end — 16:00**

> The reading of the day, per §8, and then one sentence she should not say aloud and does:
> "We held a whole day, once, and nobody had to be brave in it. That used to be the ordinary
> kind."

Each line fails [culture.md](culture.md) §3 for the other three factions, which is that
document's own test (§6): the watch's call names no bearing and no classification and is
sufficient anyway, which the Consortium would price and the Directorate would formalise;
Teel's warning refuses to become an order at the exact moment an order would be easier, which
no other register would resist; the turning is a request addressed to a *count*, grammar only
the Commune possesses; and Marr's last line is sentiment stated as fact — the Consortium's
worst insult, spent deliberately, by the person the campaign will charge for it.
