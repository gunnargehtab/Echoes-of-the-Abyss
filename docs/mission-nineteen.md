# The Second Chord 3 — Nineteen

> The third mission of the Knight campaign ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §7), built — and one
> of the fourteen documents that complete the bible's campaign. Written to the pattern
> [mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) sets and the two Chord documents before it
> continue: everything here is authored — the forces, the water, the beats, the numbers and the
> text — and code transcribes this document.

**Setting:** the Rest, the head of a trench on the southern margin of the Resonance Fields where
crystal country meets trench country, 214 PC, between *Standing Wave* and the conclave interval
([world-map.md](world-map.md) §4; [timeline.md](timeline.md), 211 PC).

**Mission id:** `chord-nineteen` — namespaced by campaign after `chord-standing-wave`, per
[campaign.md](campaign.md) §1.

**Premise, for the entry that offers it:** *The Order goes back to the trench where it lost
nineteen, to play nineteen intervals over ground it cannot reach.*

**This is the first mission in the bible that gives every hull the player commands its own name,
its own row and its own reading.** [mission-aptitude.md](mission-aptitude.md) §10 promised that
mission 3 would teach the second half of a lesson its own mission taught the first half of — *six
is a small number to work with* against *six is a small number to have* — and the format's answer
to "have" is not a new predicate. It is six roles, six standing `survive` rows and six authored
sentences, which spends [mission-intake.md](mission-intake.md) §13's singular-role rule six-fold
and is the first time any document has wanted it that way. What the engine cannot do is carry the
loss out of the match, and §13 is honest about that in its first row rather than in its last.

## 1. What the Rest Is

A trench head the Order renamed and does not discuss.

The First Chapter-House and the trench country **meet** ([world-map.md](world-map.md) §4), which
is the sentence this whole mission descends from: it is why the 211 PC crystal raids cost the
Order its junior cadre, and it is why the ground those raids were fought over is a tide's transit
from crystal country rather than an expedition. The Rest is the head of one of those trenches, on
the Fields' southern margin, and until 211 it was named for the interval it rang at. A rest is
"written down, played, and not an absence — the part of a chord that is silent on purpose"
([mission-aptitude.md](mission-aptitude.md) §8), and a chapter that renames a piece of water for
one has said something it will not say again. Nobody in the Order discusses the renaming and this
document does not either.

Those below have a number for the trench, in a record the Order is not shown. **This document
does not give it.** [world-map.md](world-map.md) §4 carries all the geography the mission needs,
and §3 counts the nine trenches downward from the First by their axes and never says which of them
heads here. The bible numbers cohorts, and numbers them often — the Fourth on a picket
([mission-baffle.md](mission-baffle.md) §6), the Ninth on the rim
([mission-second-chord.md](mission-second-chord.md) §5), the Second at a furrow posted north of
every trench there is ([mission-in-writing.md](mission-in-writing.md) §5) — and numbering a cohort
is not numbering a trench. Numbering *this* trench inside a Knight document would author a
Directorate fact from the wrong side of the water.

Nineteen Knights went down here in 211 PC and none of them has been committed. The Order plays the
interval a person's name resolves to at the committal ([culture.md](culture.md) §5), and a
committal is played *over* the body — so nobody could go down and get them, nobody could play it,
and the reason has been signed twice and stands in the Ninth's book. This is the tide the
Choirmaster stops signing it.

**Three facts about this water decide the mission, and none of them is a fence.**

**The floor is four hundred metres under the bench.** The trench floor is 2,150 m; the Order's
hulls are PR-2 and the Mid-Water band ends at 1,800 m ([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §1),
so the party works from **1,750 m** — the last fifty metres of its own rating — over water it may
not enter. The nineteen are uniformly, everywhere out of reach, and §11 is arranged so that a
player who goes looking for a corner where the arithmetic improves finds the floor getting further
away rather than nearer.

**The walls between are Hollow ground.** A Hollow works 1,700 m and reaches 1,250–2,150 m, on
trench walls and abyssal overhangs ([bestiary.md](bestiary.md) §4). Seven coil on the two walls of
this trench at **1,700 m** — the species' own working depth, which is the depth a placed animal
holds and the only one it can be authored at (§13) — at SIG 3, which is the Drift's own Silent
Running: they do not move and do not get louder until they strike. It is also fifty metres above
the water the party has to work in, so the animal that guards the descent is standing in it. The
party will not see most of them and will play nine of the nineteen intervals inside their reach.

**The trench carries.** Abyssal Trench is PF 1.60 axial — "no secrets, only distances"
([environments.md](environments.md)). A held tone at 80 over this ground is a Contact to an
Abyssal Submersible from 9,582 m and a Classification from 5,404 m, which is more map than there
is. Every interval the Order plays here is heard by everything on the axis, and the Directorate is
on the axis.

The Order knows all three and goes anyway, which is not defiance but the committal's own
arithmetic.

## 2. Whose Hulls the Player Commands

**The player commands the Ninth's committal party: the same six hulls, under the same officer,
with an order from the Choirmaster that the Order has owed for three years.**

Voice Ren Kalliso is thirty-one, b. 2,050 m, the Order's best field officer and its most ordinary
person ([characters.md](characters.md)). She commanded the survivors in 211 PC; the nineteen were
her own chapter's junior cadre; nineteen berths at her house have been empty since 211 PC
([habitats.md](habitats.md) §5); and in both built Chord missions she speaks exactly once, to
nobody, and does not mention them. **This is the mission that lets her mention them, once, at the
ninth minute, to nobody** — and the line is [culture.md](culture.md) §3's own Knight line, given a
mouth at last. She speaks twice here and only twice: the first interval, whenever the party plays
it, and the ninth minute. The first is about an interval and the second is about nineteen people,
and the mission spends its whole middle putting the one distance between them.

**Choirmaster Ivane Sull orders the committal from the Ninth and reads the count at the close.**
Her reason is not sentiment and the briefing does not pretend it is: she has a raid plan on her
desk she has not authorised ([characters.md](characters.md)), and she will not spend the Order at
the Mouth's rim with nineteen of it unentered. Mission 6 stands on this tide having happened.

**Chapter-Master Vrey is not in this mission.** The conclave he does not call is mission 4
([campaign.md](campaign.md) §7), and a mission 3 that put the Order's internal politics on the
channel would spend mission 4's only material on a committal order.

**Engine bound, stated so nobody corrects it into a bug.** Three slots. Slot 0 is the player and
carries the Knight faction value; slot 1 is the court — no party, no faction, nothing in the water
— and this mission never uses it; slot 2 is a Directorate watch of two hulls, weapons-cold. **The
Drift is not a party.** `fauna` is `false` and every animal in the mission is authored by a
`creature` beat on the Drift's own slot, in the placed-and-not-driven idiom
[mission-intake.md](mission-intake.md) §13 built, because the default seeder is a skirmish roster
and cannot put seven ambushers on two named walls.

### What the party carries, and what it does not

1. **Active sonar, unlocked — and this is the handover.**
   [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10 withholds the ping until mission 3 of every campaign, and this
   is mission 3. The Order's own reading of the button does not change on being handed it: §5 of
   [systems-echo.md](systems-echo.md) fixes it at SIG 95 **omnidirectional**, and §8 exempts it
   from the directional term, so **it is the one emission the Order owns that has no bow**. The
   briefing says so in Sull's own words. §4 prices it and never fences it: it is the only way to
   see a coiled Hollow at once, and everything within 1,434 m hears it at Commit.
2. **Construction locked** — *a committal builds nothing*. The single `AbilityLock` this mission
   authors.
3. **No refit and no Spire.** The Order reaches depth by projection rather than by rating
   ([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §3) and this party brought no Sounding Spire, so the
   1,800 m line is exactly where [systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §1 puts it. That is the
   mission; a Spire here would delete it.
4. **Weapons live. Torpedoes, mines, depth charges and noisemakers unstruck.** The walls have to
   be cleared or worked around, and unlike [mission-aptitude.md](mission-aptitude.md) §3's tuning
   party this one is not asked to avoid a fight it did not arrange — the Order arranged this one,
   with an animal, three years late.
5. **Silent Running is present and is priced twice.** Mission 1's arithmetic still holds — a
   cruising Knight pointed away is quieter than a silent Knight pointed at you — and the trench
   adds a second price on top of it. A hull at 8 is heard by a Hollow at Interest from **203 m**
   and the same hull showing its wake at cruise is heard at Interest from **105 m**, so silence is
   *louder to the walls than facing is*. And silence stops a sounding outright: a hull that runs
   quiet to play an interval loses the interval ([systems-echo.md](systems-echo.md) §6).

`startingNodules` is 0. There is nothing to buy and nothing to spend.

## 4. The Count

The system this mission teaches, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §2 rule 2: one system, introduced
in the first three minutes and load-bearing by the last five. The system is **permanent loss —
the hull as an unreplaceable, named thing** — and it lands in four movements, each of which is a
number rather than a doctrine.

**1. Every hull has a name and its own row, and a loss is read alone.** Six roles, one hull each;
six `survive` rows at count 1; six authored met and unmet sentences. The rows are **standing** —
re-derived every mission tick, and able to go from Met back to Pending
([mission-intake.md](mission-intake.md) §8's correction) — so a hull the walls take at 09:00 reads
as *entered* at 18:00 and not as *home*. The player is shown all six at 00:00, all six reading
Met, and is shown that they are the kind of row that can stop. That is the whole teaching beat and
it costs one panel.

**2. The walls are a decision the player cannot see.** A coiled Hollow at SIG 3 in PF 1.60 water
is, to the Voice at HYD 65, a Contact at 1,041 m, a Bearing at 808 m, a **Classification at 587 m**
and a Track at 438 m. Its strike fires within 500 m in three dimensions
(`DRIFT.HOLLOW_TRIGGER_RANGE_M`), which from the bench at 1,750 m to a coil at 1,700 m is **497 m
horizontal**: the party has fifty metres of depth to put between itself and an animal that works at
the depth it works at, and the strike spends the other four hundred and ninety-seven on the ground.
So:

- **A Corvette classifies a coil with less than a metre to spare** — Classification at 498.3 m
  against a reach of 497.5 — and its Track at 371 m is a hundred and twenty-six metres too late.
  **The Voice hears one ninety metres earlier than that**, at 587 m, and gets her Track at 438 m,
  sixty metres inside the reach. Ninety metres is two seconds at the Voice's own 45 m/s, and the
  Voice is the hull carrying the party's ears.
- **Clearing one is 640 HP.** Under all six guns that is 2.4 seconds (60/s from the Voice, 41.7/s
  from each Corvette, 268/s together); under one Corvette, 15.4. **A wound springs the strike** —
  a Hollow shot from anywhere lunges at strike loudness (#353; [bestiary.md](bestiary.md) §4) —
  and the lunge covers 400 m in 5.3 seconds at 75 m/s. The strike is 55/s: a Corvette dies in 7.6
  seconds and the Voice in 21.8.
- **Or it is answered by facing.** A sounding at 80 in the cone commits a Hollow from 648 m and
  interests it from 855 m; a Corvette at cruise commits one from 336 m; the wake at 2.8 never
  commits one at all. And **loudness alone never springs one**: `hollowStage` reads Commit *and*
  the 500 m in three dimensions together, so a hull that is Commit-loud from a kilometre away is a
  hull the wall watches and does not answer. Proximity is the trigger; facing only decides whether
  proximity counts. What does move a coil is a wound, and a wounded one closes.

Nine of the nineteen intervals have a coil inside 497 m of their own metre (§6 says which), and
**the player does not know which nine.** Every interval is playable from the far edge of its own
400 m disc, bow at the wall, and every one of the nine is fatal from the point itself. The mission
never says which is which, and that is what makes movement 3 a decision instead of a button.

**3. The discourteous instrument, priced rather than fenced.** One ping is Tier 4 on everything
within 900 m for three seconds — every coil on a wall, exactly, at once, with its health and its
facing. The cost is three numbers and one rule. Fauna aggro is **tripled** for the three seconds
the ping lasts (`ACTIVE_SONAR.FAUNA_AGGRO_MULTIPLIER`), so the hull that pressed it reads to a
Hollow as 285: Commit-loud out to **1,434 m** and Interest-loud out to 1,891 m. The basin reads it
at Commit from **1,479 m**. The watch resolves the pinger at **Track from 4,486 m** down 1.6 water,
which is the whole trench. The rule is the one the movement above ends on: Commit-loud is not a
state a wall stays in, so a ping does not arm the trench. What it does is make one hull the loudest
thing in the water for three seconds — which springs, at once, any coil already inside 497 m of it,
and which takes a basin that has been listening straight from Interested to Committed at a
kilometre and a half. So the button is cheap from ground the party has cleared, expensive from
ground it has not, and the only instrument on the map that says which of the two it is standing on.
**It is not a mistake and it is not free, and the briefing says so rather than the mission
punishing it in the dark.**

**4. An interval is played by the hull it was given to.** Nineteen `MissionSounding` rows, each
naming a carrier — the Voice one, the Corvettes four, four, four, three and three — fixed at
authoring, and the runtime's own comment names the consequence: a hull the mission has lost is
"the ultimate broken hold". **So a hull entered at 09:00 takes its intervals with it, and they are
unplayable for the rest of the mission.** The count at 18:00 is not what the player attempted; it
is what the Order has left.

### The SIG budget

**SIG budget: 80 — the interval's own figure.** The prologue's twenty is a ceiling, Asset
Recovery's sixty-five a working level, Attendance's eight a description, Aptitude's twenty-eight a
figure that means nothing until you say where the listener stands, and Standing Wave's seventy
what a building emits. This one is **the loudest thing the mission asks the player to do, stated
as the thing it is for**: a sounding held at 80 for twenty seconds, nineteen times, with no
quieter version of the mission available, because a quieter committal is a committal that was not
played.

It is playtested against two players, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10. The first pings the walls
to save time and learns what the map costs: it is exact, it lasts three seconds, and it is paid for
by whichever coil was already inside four hundred and ninety-seven metres of the hull that pressed
the button. The second plays every interval bow-on from the point itself, because that is where the
marker is, and loses the Third's hull and the Third's four names in the same eight seconds.

## 7. What Is Heard

**The trench, and everything on it, and nothing of the walls.**

- **The Fields, above and behind.** The Head and the shoulders are Resonance Field at PF 0.70,
  ringing faintly the way crystal country always does ([world-map.md](world-map.md)) — the pitched
  ambient bed [mission-aptitude.md](mission-aptitude.md) §13 first asked the mix for.
- **The axis, and what it does to everything.** PF 1.60 down the trench: a sounding at 80 is a
  Contact to the watch from **9,582 m** and a Classification from **5,404 m**, and reaches the
  Voice's own ears from 8,103 m. There is no distance on this map at which an interval is private.
- **The watch, walking.** 22 idle, 28 under way, on six authored legs at 60 m/s — 1,300 m of leg
  in twenty-two seconds, then two and a half minutes of standing. Audible from the first tick and
  classified from the first minute.
- **The walls: nothing.** Seven animals at SIG 3, which is under the Voice's Contact threshold at
  anything beyond 1,041 m and under a Corvette's at 883 m. **The player will cross this map
  hearing any of the seven only when they are already inside a Classification of it.** This is the
  inverse of [mission-intake.md](mission-intake.md) §7's opening: that mission's silence was a
  search, and this one's is a room the party has to work in.
- **A strike, when one comes.** SIG 60. To the Voice, through 1.6 water, that is a Contact from
  **6,769 m**, against a map whose longest diagonal is 6,403 m: a strike anywhere is heard
  everywhere. Every Hollow kill and every Hollow strike tells the entire trench where it happened,
  including the watch, which enters it.
- **The basin, from 16:30.** A Sounder cruising at 45 is a Contact to the Voice from 5,655 m; the
  same animal calling at 100 is one from **9,316 m**, and to the watch from 11,016 m. It is the
  loudest thing in the game and it is under the Head at the close.
- **The party's own descent.** 1,600 m to 1,750 m is 150 m at 45 m/s — three and a third seconds
  at a SIG floor of 72 ([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §2) — which is a Track to the watch
  from 3,772 m. The Order arrives on the bench announced, every time, and the climb home at 15 m/s
  is silent.

And what is not here: no hazard site, no second Order party, no Consortium anywhere, no structure
of any kind, and no thermocline — the shallowest ground is 1,600 m and the duct ends at 1,300, so
every pair on this map is Below-to-Below and the layer's factor is 1 throughout, arranged as
[mission-aptitude.md](mission-aptitude.md) §11 arranged it so that the mission's one system is the
only system talking.

## 10. What It Teaches

One system, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10: **permanent loss** — a hull as a named,
unreplaceable thing rather than a counter. It lands in order across the beat table: the six named
rows on the panel at 00:00, the walls the party cannot hear from 00:00, the first free interval at
01:30, the first contested one whenever the player reaches it, the ping whenever the player decides
they have to see, and the basin at 16:30 taking a decision about the Voice while the count may
still be short.

Underneath it, the campaign's real subject, and the same trick rule 2 plays on every faction:
**the Order is told nothing about being few, poor or mortal, and is now made to say the number out
loud.** [mission-aptitude.md](mission-aptitude.md) taught six hulls inside one afternoon and
[mission-standing-wave.md](mission-standing-wave.md) a budget that does not refill; this one gives
the six hulls names and reads them out one at a time, and the player is the person who has to hear
"the Third is entered" said about a thing they were commanding four minutes ago.

And a third thing, which is why the mission is called what it is. **The title is a count, and the
count is the mission.** Nineteen is the number of people the Order lost in one year and cannot
replace, the number of intervals, and what the player is asked to enter; at the close it is set
beside a second number that is at most six. Neither is explained and neither is ever compared to
the other by anybody in the water. A player who works out that the two numbers are the same kind
of number has understood the Hadron Knights, and the mission never says it.

What this mission deliberately does not teach:

- **The Order's internal politics** — mission 4, *Conclave* ([mission-conclave-chord.md](mission-conclave-chord.md)),
  where Vrey has the standing to call a vote and does not. Sull orders this committal alone and
  nobody argues with her on the channel, which is the last time that is true.
- **The First, the Three, and what the Order does not discuss** — mission 5
  ([mission-the-three.md](mission-the-three.md)). This mission stands over the Order's dead and
  says nothing about the Chord, the transcripts or the reply.
- **Projected depth access** — mission 6 ([mission-rim-deposits.md](mission-rim-deposits.md)),
  where the Sounding Spire's PR+1 is finally the system. This is the campaign gap
  [mission-aptitude.md](mission-aptitude.md) §10 and
  [mission-standing-wave.md](mission-standing-wave.md) §3 both flagged, and this mission makes it
  *hurt* rather than filling it: four hundred metres of unreachable floor, for eighteen minutes,
  with no instrument aboard that would fix it.
- **Resonance Collapse and the transmission** — mission 7
  ([mission-second-chord.md](mission-second-chord.md)). The ping handed over here is the same
  button pressed at the last, for the opposite reason.
