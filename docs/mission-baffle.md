# The Ledger 3 — Baffle

> The third mission of the Consortium campaign ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §4), specified. Written
> to the pattern [mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) sets and the Ledger documents
> continue: everything here is authored — the forces, the water, the beats, the numbers and the
> text — and code transcribes this document.

**Setting:** The Fourth Trench — the freight shortcut between the west wall and the deep — and
the Deep Yard at its southern mouth, 214 PC ([world-map.md](world-map.md)).

**Mission id:** `ledger-baffle` — namespaced by campaign, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §1.

---

## 1. What the Fourth Trench Is

The Fourth Trench is the freight shortcut between the west wall and the deep, and both the
Consortium and the Directorate claim it, and neither patrols it alone
([world-map.md](world-map.md)) — until three tides ago, when the Sorrowgate exchange went wrong
in front of a Directorate observer and the Directorate closed the shortcut to chartered freight
while the inquiry runs. They did not seize it. They *attend* it: pickets on the axis, in the
water their claim has always stood on, counting what enters. Under their law this is restraint.
Under the Consortium's it is a hand on the grid's throat. Both readings are correct, which is
how this campaign builds its collisions ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §2, rule 1).

At the trench's southern mouth sits the **Deep Yard** — the Consortium's chartered freight yard
at 1,650 m, the deep staging the concern has run for forty years and the forward ground every
later descent will stage from. Its draw plant is failing. The replacement compressor is crated
at the north staging, the long way around misses the yard's air arithmetic by two tides, and
the trench is closed. Forty-one people are on the yard's complement, and the yard's plant is
already missing beats.

Two facts about the water decide the whole mission:

**A trench carries everything.** PF 1.6 down the axis: the corridor is a megaphone, no secrets
down its length, only distances ([systems-echo.md](systems-echo.md) §3). A column in the Fourth
Trench is heard entering it, heard transiting it, and heard arriving — by ears bred for exactly
this water. Stealth is not on the table and the mission never pretends it is.

**The lay-bys mask.** Two vent pockets notch the trench walls — the chartered lay-bys freight
crews have used for forty years, PF 0.45 against the axis's 1.6 — and the concern's riggers
have moored a **Baffle station** in each, because that is what the freight charter always did
on a contested transit. Inside a bubble in a pocket, the trench's arithmetic inverts: the
loudest corridor in the region holds two chambers of genuinely quiet water, and they are the
only two, and everybody knows where they are.

## 2. Whose Hulls the Player Commands

**The player commands a relief convoy under writ: a Klaxon escort, the compressor barge, and
the two moored stations already in the water.**

The Board will not file a transit request, and the reason is stated in the writ's own register:
a request enters the yard's failing plant into a record the inquiry can read, and the exposure
of the grid's weakness is priced higher than the exposure of the convoy. The Ninth Board has
run that arithmetic before. The player is the shape it takes in the water.

Lift Foreman Dessa Vail rides the compressor barge — a compressor lift is her trade, and the
campaign keeps its people. The yard is on the channel throughout, because its plant is.

**Engine bound, stated so nobody corrects it into a bug.** Three parties and a court slot: the
convoy, the picket cohort, and the yard — whose only asset in the water is a sound. The Drift
is not a party.

### What the convoy carries, at last

**Active sonar.** [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10 withholds the ping until mission 3, and this
is mission 3: the writ funds the survey array, and the escort carries it. Two missions of
being attended by things the player could not resolve on demand are the tuition; this corridor
is the exam. The doc teaches the doctrine straight ([systems-echo.md](systems-echo.md) §5):
ping late, briefly, and just before committing — a ping is the last thing you do before you
are already winning the fight, it grants Tier-4 firing solutions for its window, and in a
PF 1.6 corridor its self-reveal carries even further than its reputation. The pickets already
know the convoy is there. What they do not have, until it transmits, is a *solution* — and
neither does the convoy.

**No construction.** The trench is closed water under somebody else's claim; the writ moves
one plant through it and builds nothing over it.

## 4. The Baffle

The system this mission teaches, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §2: **masking as engagement
geometry**. The campaign has shown masking twice as inherited ground — the Works in mission 1,
the layer in mission 2 — and now hands it over as a thing the Consortium *builds*, with the
correction the doctrine actually rests on:

1. **The bubble does not hide the column. It moves the line where the column is fought.**
   Against the trench cohort's ears (HYD 85, the best in the game), a Klaxon convoy in PF 1.6
   water is tracked from kilometres — bubble or none. What the bubble changes is the geometry
   of resolution: inside station water, bearings blur, solutions degrade, and the fight happens
   at the pocket's mouth, on the convoy's terms, at the range the escort's guns prefer.
2. **Quiet ground is made, in advance, by riggers.** The stations were moored before the
   convoy sailed. Masking is logistics — the Consortium way: they do not train quiet crews,
   they *install* quiet water, and it costs six hundred nodules a berth and has to be defended.
3. **Between bubbles, the corridor owns you.** The transit legs are played loud, tracked, and
   committed — a Klaxon push in its purest form, with the pocket ahead as the objective and
   the pocket behind as the ground already spent.
4. **A moored station is a stake.** It cannot run, it sings its own position at thirty SIG,
   and the party that objects to it can find it. §8 prices exactly that.

Advance bubble to bubble; fight at the mouths; never at the middle of a leg. That sentence is
the mission, and the last five minutes are load-bearing on it.

### The SIG budget

**SIG budget: 65** — the Klaxon working level, mission 1's figure returned to, because this is
mission 1's posture aimed down a corridor. The playtest adversary is again the player who
creeps: a convoy that runs silent between bubbles arrives at the same fights with its weapons
offline and its escort's solutions cold.

The pickets are not villains and the mission does not need them to be
([campaign.md](campaign.md) §2, rule 1). They hold a closed trench under a claim two centuries
old, against a convoy that is — under their law — running contraband through an inquiry's
water. They announce themselves, state the terms, and engage only what stands into their
watch. Every hull they field is somebody's cohort, and the campaign will hand the player those
cohorts' side of this water in another campaign entirely.

## 6. The Picket

**Two standing watches, gates across the corridor: one at the trench's first bend, one below
the second lay-by. Between them, patrol legs on the axis — filed with nobody, because the
Directorate does not file.**

The watches are authored transits, and their law is simple and stated up front, in their own
register, at the first challenge: the trench is closed; what enters is counted; what stands
into a watch is engaged. There is no hunting behaviour and no scripted pursuit — a picket
holds its water, which is the truth of the doctrine ([factions.md](factions.md)): the
Directorate knows first and arrives in numbers, and in a trench, *arriving* was done years
ago. The corridor is their dome. The convoy is inside it from the first bend.

What that means at the table:

- **The transit legs are timing.** The watches walk their beats on their own clock. A convoy
  that reads the pattern moves between bubbles while the near watch is at the far end of its
  leg, and meets the gate fight at the pocket mouth it chose.
- **The gate fights are the Klaxon bill.** A watch that is stood into engages — nearest, in
  range, no drama. Two submersibles against a braced escort at a bubble mouth is an exchange
  the Consortium wins loud; the same two in open corridor against a strung-out convoy is how
  the barge dies.
- **The ping is the difference between those two fights.** A watch at rest is quiet — silent
  hulls in trench shadow, smudges at best. The transmit resolves the gate before the convoy
  commits to it: who, where, how many, for three seconds, at the price of telling the whole
  trench — which already knew — and the Drift, which now cares.

## 7. The Cost

**"The first push that works. It costs more than it should"** ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §4)
— and the mission authors the second sentence rather than hoping the combat provides it.

At 13:00, after the convoy is past it, the northern Baffle station goes off the chart. A
cutter cohort takes it at its mooring — not as an escalation but as a correction: the mooring
was never in the charter, the inquiry's water is not to be re-rigged, and the Directorate
removes what was installed against it. Their announcement afterwards is one sentence in the
passive voice. The convoy's road home now has one quiet chamber in it instead of two, and
every player will feel exactly what was lost, because they stood in it twenty minutes ago.

The rest of the cost is emergent and expected: the gate fights are real, the barge is soft,
and the escort is three hulls against four in ground that carries every mistake to every ear.
The mission is tuned so the push *works* — a read schedule, held mouths, and a spent ping
bring the plant through — and so that it never works clean.

## 8. The Objective

**Deliver the plant. Bring the column out of the fights it takes to do it.**

**The plant.** The compressor barge from the north staging to the Deep Yard's berth before the
yard's own plant fails at 20:00. The barge is deaf and moves only with an escort in earshot —
Sorrowgate's hold, with guns added: the writ prices the barge's deafness as an escort
requirement, because a corridor full of firing solutions is not the Scar, and here it matters.

**The column.** The escorts that fight the convoy through are on the writ too, and the close
prices them by name-count: an exchange entered at cost is still entered.

### Results

| Result | Condition | The reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Relief delivered** | The plant berthed at the yard | "The plant is berthed and turning. The yard's air arithmetic closes in the black. The trench remains, formally, closed; the registry records a delivery it does not record a route for." |
| **The yard goes dark** | The plant lost, or still in the trench at the whistle | "The yard's plant fails on schedule. Evacuation under Directorate observation begins at the next tide, and the registry opens a file it has opened before. The Board is advised that the shortfall was not arithmetical." |

The plant is the keystone: machinery, escort and honour delivered to the yard without the
compressor read as "the yard goes dark", because forty-one people do not breathe a moral
victory. Beneath whichever reading the run earns, the close enters the column's own line —
whole, or at cost — and the station's loss is already in the water where the player left it.

### The failure, and the sounds that precede it

The failure is the yard's plant, and the plant has been audible since the first tick — a
periodic beat down the trench's own carrying water, missing strokes, the mission's clock and
its stakes in one diegetic sound, exactly as the taps were ([mission-asset-recovery.md](mission-asset-recovery.md)
§6). Its schedule is stated in the briefing. And in the last ninety seconds the trench adds
the older warning: the pack, drawn up the axis by twenty minutes of gunfire and transmission,
loud, rising, and arriving — the corridor reminding the column what all that noise was priced
in ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §10).

## 10. What It Teaches

One system, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10: **masking as made ground** — the Baffle
bubble, what it does and does not buy, and the engagement geometry of bubble-to-bubble
advance. Load-bearing by the last five minutes: the second gate is only winnable braced at a
mouth, and the road home lost its northern chamber at 13:00.

The ping is handed over here, per §10's schedule, and taught inside the system rather than
beside it: masking decides where the fight happens, the ping decides how much of it the
escort sees, and the two together are the Consortium's whole tactical grammar — install quiet
ground, advance loud between it, transmit once, commit.

What this mission deliberately does not teach:

- **Reading someone else's water** — mission 4, *Exposure*, where the ears point outward and
  the register starts pricing what the concern does not own.
- **Depth as a bleed** — the trench floor stops at Mid-Water's edge and the convoy is rated
  for all of it. The crush ledger is mission 5's.
- **The Directorate from inside** — the pickets are written as they see themselves, and the
  campaign that hands the player their galleries is [campaign.md](campaign.md) §6, not this
  one.

## 12. The Briefing

The relief writ, read to the convoy at muster. Signed for the Ninth Board. The Consortium's
register is defined in [culture.md](culture.md) §3.

> The Deep Yard's plant fails in twenty minutes of transit time. The replacement is crated and
> the escort is funded. The long route misses the arithmetic by two tides. This writ takes the
> short one.
>
> The Fourth Trench is closed to chartered freight while the exchange inquiry runs. The
> concern has reviewed the closure and finds it is not the concern's. No transit request has
> been filed: a request enters the yard's condition into the inquiry's record, and that
> exposure is priced above yours. You are advised of the pricing so that nobody mistakes it
> for an oversight.
>
> Two baffle stations are moored at the chartered lay-bys. Advance station to station. Fight
> at the mouths, where the bubble bends the water your way; do not fight in the open corridor,
> which is theirs from end to end and carries everything. The escort carries the survey array.
> Transmit once, late, and commit on what it returns. The trench will hear the transmission.
> The trench hears everything; that is what a trench is.
>
> The picket will announce the closure. It will be correct. Proceed.
>
> Forty-one berths are on the yard's complement. The plant you are hauling is the line item
> above them. Exposure is authorised. Sentiment is not. Signed for the Board.

### Objective readings, in play

- *Deliver the plant to the yard's berth. The arithmetic closes at the whistle.*
- *Bring the column out of what the corridor costs. The exchange is entered either way.*

### The voices on the channel

**Lift Foreman Dessa Vail, on the compressor barge — 01:30**

> Hear that beat missing? That's forty-one people's plant asking where we are. I've rigged
> lifts for this concern for nineteen years and that is the first cargo that ever wrote back.

**Picket-Speaker, Fourth Trench Cohort — 04:00**

> The trench is closed while the inquiry is open. What enters it is not being threatened. It
> is being counted.

**Picket-Speaker, after the northern station — 13:00**

> A mooring was found in closed water. It was not in any charter. It has been corrected.

**Yardmaster Brann Holt, on the yard channel — 17:30**

> Yard to convoy: we can hear you. We have been able to hear you for ten minutes. Nobody down
> here is calling that a defect in the plan.

Each line fails [culture.md](culture.md) §3 for the other three factions, which is that
document's own test (§6): the Picket-Speaker never claims agency in two sentences of enforcing
a closure, which no other register could sustain; Vail prices nineteen years and forty-one
lives in freight terms without a gram of sentiment; Holt's line is the Klaxon doctrine spoken
by its beneficiaries — being heard as reassurance — which the Commune could not say without
irony and the Knights not without making it a courtesy.
