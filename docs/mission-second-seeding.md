# The Second Seeding 7 — The Second Seeding

> The seventh and last mission of the Commune campaign ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §5),
> specified and built — the campaign's convergence slot and its ending: the plateaus' arrival
> at the eleven kilometres every ending is fought over ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §8), and an
> ending that is not canon, not ranked, not merged, and not a fight ([campaign.md](campaign.md)
> §9). One of the fourteen documents that complete the bible's campaign, written to the pattern
> [mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) sets and the Commune documents continue:
> everything here is authored — the forces, the water, the beats, the numbers and the text —
> and code transcribes this document.

**Setting:** The Rim — the Mouth's northern edge, 4,410 m below everything, 214 PC, on the
concern's day of the rim week — day D of the week's calendar (§1, §6) — from under a bed on the
western lip ([mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md); [world-map.md](world-map.md);
[campaign.md](campaign.md) §8).

**Mission id:** `seeding-second-seeding` — namespaced by campaign after `seeding-radicals`, per
[campaign.md](campaign.md) §1.

**Premise, for the entry that offers it:** *The plateaus were on the rim before anybody, under
a bed, with the Kell seed and a garden's whole argument. Today the concern comes down loud to
read six faces, and somebody has to plant.*

**This is the first Commune briefing not spoken by Marr, and the first Commune mission on
ground three other campaigns play.** Tidespeaker Ysolde Marr cannot order anyone and cannot be
heard from eighty kilometres north and a layer up, so Bloomwright Sefa Anholt speaks it, aboard
the barge, under the bed, in the plural anyway — and it is the only briefing in the campaign
that says what the day is for and whose day it is. The map is `mouth-rim`, unchanged: the same
terrain [mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) surveyed from the staging, played a second
time from the far end of it, and to be played twice more by other navies on other tides. The
rule is [campaign.md](campaign.md) §8's — the same eleven kilometres four times, never the same
mission — and §11 says what this mission adds to the literal, which is everything but geometry.

## 1. What the Rim Is, From Below

**The Mouth's northern edge, seen from a bed on its western lip, on the day the concern came
down.**

At the southern terminus of everything, an eleven-kilometre depression that returns pings
before they should arrive, on a cycle that shortened from 43 hours to 39 in 213 PC and is still
shortening; its edge carries the richest resonant-crystal deposits ever surveyed, and since
this year, two returns that a Consortium survey has filed twice as equipment fault, and will
file a third time at the close of this day ([world-map.md](world-map.md);
[timeline.md](timeline.md); [bestiary.md](bestiary.md) §4;
[mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) §1). The Commune calls the Mouth a wound
([world.md](world.md)). The lip is as far as the chart goes, and as far as this document
goes: nothing below is described, approached or explained ([culture.md](culture.md) §6).

**The week has a calendar, and this is its first day.** Four navies arrive at the rim in one
week of 214 PC for four reasons that do not reconcile ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §8), and the
four documents that play it agree on the order. The concern's day — the survey of
[mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md), its four hulls coming down loud at 02:00, six faces
read by hand, the writ turning north at 22:00 — is **D**, and this mission is played on D. The
Directorate's column walks the lip the next tide, **D+1**, and it is
[mission-first-arrival.md](mission-first-arrival.md)'s. The Order's raid on the deposits comes
two tides after, **D+2** ([mission-rim-deposits.md](mission-rim-deposits.md)), and the Chord the
tide after that, **D+3** ([mission-second-chord.md](mission-second-chord.md)). Nothing here
shows either: no Directorate column and no Knight raid is on this water on D, because the
other two documents do not put them here. What this document establishes for those two to
inherit is one fact, stated in §6.

Three facts about this water decide the whole mission, and none of them is a fence.

**The plateaus were here first, and mentioned it to nobody.** Two of the programme's six
deep-rated hulls — the proof scouts of 204 PC — have been on the terraces since before the
concern had ears on the rim, and [mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) §5 fixed that
before this document existed: *they arrived first and mention it to nobody*. What the survey
did not carry is the rest of it. The seed column that went south through the drowned city
([mission-radicals.md](mission-radicals.md)) has been on the western lip since the tide before
last, at 3,000 m, under a Spore Veil grown the way the furrow's beds were grown
([mission-in-writing.md](mission-in-writing.md) §4), silent, with the Kell seed aboard. The
concern's four hulls descend at 02:00 into water the Commune has already been living in for a
tide.

**The lip is the loudest water in the Rift, and the Commune has chosen to plant on it.**
Abyssal Trench, PF 1.6: it carries like a trench because it is the beginning of one with no far
wall ([mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) §11). A sowing at the working figure, 45,
carries at 72 here where it carried at 24.75 under kelp; under a bed it is 45 × 0.4 = 18, and
18 through 1.6 is 28.8, which is a Track to a Directorate submersible's 85 from 1,586 m and a
Classification from 2,127 ([systems-echo.md](systems-echo.md) §3, §8). Every navy on the rim
is inside some tier of a veiled sowing at some point in the day, which is why the day is a
schedule before it is anything else (§4).

**Below 2,700 m the column is empty of animals, and the rim is not the Drift's.** Nothing lives
on the lip that the bestiary has a row for ([bestiary.md](bestiary.md) §4): no Hollow on a
wall, no pack, no shoal. What is here is two returns breathing on a count, which the Commune
does not file because it files nothing, and — at 20:30, when the week's noise comes due —
something large lifting off the lip's floor and rising toward the terraces
([mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) §7). It ignores everything the plateaus own,
because its line passes 1,750 m east of the bed — the barge is the one hull of theirs a
Sounder would grind, and it is never within a body and a radius of the line. The one thing on
this map that can hurt a hull is the water, and it hurts exactly the hulls the plateaus are
about to make ground for (§4).

Nobody under the bed says any of this. It is the ground the mission stands on.

## 2. Whose Hulls the Player Commands

**The player commands the whole of the Commune's deep navy on one water for the first time:
the seed column under the bed, the charting pair on the terraces, and Warden Juno Teel's three
corvettes at the staging, struck.**

The programme has six deep-rated hulls and has never had a seventh: two scouts refit in 204 PC
for the proof, and four seed hulls — the barge and three tenders — refit for the seeding.
[mission-deep-furrow.md](mission-deep-furrow.md) fielded the two;
[mission-radicals.md](mission-radicals.md) fielded the four; this mission fields all six, and
the sentence is repeated on purpose, because the count is the argument. Teel's element is not
rated for this water and cannot be: a Corvette is PR-2, the lip is PR-3 ground, and 1,800 m is
as far down as a Commune gun goes without the ground's help — the staging, whose floor is
1,500, and the upper slopes, and nothing on the terraces or the lip at all (§4). The
guns are struck, as they were under the bed in the furrow
([mission-in-writing.md](mission-in-writing.md) §3): *nobody strikes first, and not on this
water*. Teel has never struck first ([characters.md](characters.md)) and this mission keeps
that for her.

**Nobody orders the column, and the column has already arrived.** In the basin the
Bloomwright's order stood again every half-minute whatever the player said
([mission-radicals.md](mission-radicals.md) §4). On the lip the Bloomwright's order is the one
the column is already obeying — to be here — and the table re-orders nothing: the momentum
has reached the ground it was momentum toward, and what stands again every half-minute now
is the bed. Bloomwright Sefa Anholt is aboard the barge and speaks the briefing (§12), because
the Tidespeaker cannot be heard from here. Marr reads the close, at home, when the count comes
up the lanes a tide later; the epilogue says so.

**Engine bound, stated so nobody corrects it into a bug.** Five parties and a court slot,
exactly as [mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) seats them, with the player's slot
changed: the plateaus carry the Commune faction value on slot 0; the court slot is 1, reserved
and empty; the concern's expedition is slot 2 (Bathyarch); the watch is slot 3 (Directorate);
the reconnaissance is slot 4 (Hadron); the attendants are slot 5, a party whose only assets in
the water are sounds, carrying the Directorate faction value for the spawn path as Prospect's
literal carries it and reporting no faction at any tier. The Drift is not a party; the riser is
an authored creature on the Drift's own slot, and `fauna` is off. Six slots, one seat, no lobby
([mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) §2).

### What the column does not carry

1. **Weapons, struck.** The escorts' fire control is locked with the reason in register:
   *struck — nobody strikes first, and not on this water*. Torpedoes, mines, depth charges and
   noisemakers are locked with the one word *struck*. Four navies are weapons-cold on D
   ([mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) §5), and the fourth is this one.
2. **No construction.** *The bed is grown; nothing else is built on a wound.* A site broadcasts
   at 70 for its whole build ([units.md](units.md)), and there is no economy, no draw and nothing
   to spend.
3. **No throttle**, for the seventh Commune mission running: the sowing's 45 is authored on the
   sounding, not set on a lever ([mission-tend.md](mission-tend.md) §3).
4. **Active sonar, available and priced.** The rim returns pings before they should arrive,
   the Directorate has never asked it anything, the plateaus never have, and the button is on
   the panel. A ping from under the bed is 95 × 0.4 = 38 through 1.6 — a Track to the watch at
   its western station (ratio 13.9), a Classification to it from its home station (2.55) and to
   the heavy on the terraces (3.72) — and it interests the riser not at all: 38 × 3 through 1.6
   at 1,946 m is 19 against a Sounder's Interest of 55 ([bestiary.md](bestiary.md) §2). Nothing
   on this rim is answered by a ping. Everything on it hears one.

**Silent Running is present, and it is the whole posture.** The veil multiplies; it does not
replace ([mission-in-writing.md](mission-in-writing.md) §4). A tender silent under the bed is
4.5 × 0.4 = 1.8 through 1.6, which is nothing to a submersible's 85 past 894 m; the same tender
idling is 7.2, a Classification from 1,200 m; the barge silent is 3.0, a Contact from 1,239;
the barge idling is 22, a Track from 1,798. The watch's western station is 1,160 m from the
bed. So a column that is silent is a smudge to the watch for five minutes of the concern's
day, and a column that forgot to be silent is a Cruiser, named, with its heading. Silence
costs the Commune the difference between 40 m/s and 32, and there is nowhere to go, so it
costs nothing until the sowing — a sounding needs a hull that is not silent (`holdingSounding`)
— and the sower drops silence to plant. Dropping silence is not firing: `applyFiringSpike` is
the only place the +40 spike is applied, so a sower that stops being quiet goes 1.8 → 7.2 →
18 under the bed and nothing louder, which is a finding this document states because the plan
for it assumed a spike (§13).

## 4. Everything at Once

The system this mission teaches, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §2 and §5 row 7: **everything
at once** — the campaign's six systems converging on one sixty-second act on the lip. Each was
introduced alone in an earlier mission; here all of them are load-bearing at the same time,
and the document maps each to where it is spent, in four movements.

### 1. The quiet economy, on the loudest water in the Rift

*Tend*'s system ([mission-tend.md](mission-tend.md) §4), inverted by address: the bed and
silence, at 3,000 m, on PF 1.6. At 00:00 the column is silent and veiled, the pair on the terraces
has the nearer of the lip's two returns at Contact from its seat and the far one at nothing (1.47
and 0.79, §7), and the concern has not come down. The Commune is already doing everything it knows
before anybody arrives, which is the system introduced in the first three minutes by the plateaus
doing nothing audible in them.

### 2. The schedule — a question carried by hand, past three navies' ears

*Convocation*'s lesson ([mission-convocation.md](mission-convocation.md) §4): a question has to
be carried by hand, and no order the player is given does it for them. The sowing is the
sounding of [mission-deep-furrow.md](mission-deep-furrow.md) §4 further down — the sower within
250 m of 1250, 3250, bow on, not silent, sixty seconds at 45, a broken hold resetting to zero —
on a PR-3 hull that pays no crush for the rock, so the only price is ears. And the ears walk.

The watch walks the lip to 1,160 m from the bed between 09:20 and 14:00 and has a veiled
sowing at Track (ratio 6.60) the whole time. The concern's western reader works face-one from a
station 828 m from the bed from about 05:21 and face-two from one 673 m from it from about
08:40 — the faces are the sounds, the reader is the ear, and the ear stands a hundred metres off
the face it is reading — and it has the sowing at Classification from the first station (3.85)
and at Track from the second (7.54). From 14:00 the nearest ears are the reader at face-three,
1,512 m off (Bearing, 1.52), the heavy on the terraces, 1,820 m off (Bearing, 1.76), and the
watch back at its station, 3,350 m off (Contact, 1.21). Before the concern comes down at 02:00 the
nearest ear is the watch at home, and the lip is as quiet as it will be all day. So the day has
two quiet windows and one loud one, and §6 prints the table. A plateau that plants in the loud
window plants into three navies' accounts; one that plants in either quiet window plants into
nobody's; and the ledger (§8) reads which, without ranking them, because
[campaign.md](campaign.md) §9 says both are the ending.

**These figures are path means, not the lip's.** The shipped model integrates PF along the
line between emitter and listener (`pathPropagation`, one sample per 250 m cell), so a pair
with both ends on the lip is priced at 1.6 and a pair from the lip to the terraces is priced
between 1.6 and 0.7 by how much of the line is which. The watch's figures are all-lip; the
concern's are means over lip and terrace cells, which is why the reader at face-three, 1,512 m
off through 0.957, has the bed at nothing (0.68) where the watch would have it at a name, and
why §7's tables carry a PF column. The tests should re-derive every cross-biome figure from
`pathPropagation` rather than from a biome constant (§13).

### 3. The zone — habitable depth, manufactured, as terrain

*Deep Furrow*'s system ([mission-deep-furrow.md](mission-deep-furrow.md) §4), spent once more
and further down. When the sowing completes, the western lip — `the-rim-furrow`, 1000, 3000,
500, 500 — becomes a **region pressure grant**: every hull inside its rectangle operates at
PR + 1, as a Sounding Spire's aura grants `Pressure.bonus` within 600 m and evaporating the
same way when a hull leaves ([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §3's Deepbloom conversion, as
[mission-deep-furrow.md](mission-deep-furrow.md) §13 asks for it). The sown lip is then the only
rectangle on the map where a PR-2 hull lives at 3,000 m — which is what *habitable depth
becomes manufacturable* ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §9) means as terrain.

**The lip is not repainted.** The judge caps the biome repaint at two missions — the second
furrow turning Kelp Forest in *Deep Furrow*, and the galleries' dome coming down in *The
Attending* 6 — and this mission is not one of them. The lip stays Abyssal Trench at 1.6 after
the sowing as before it, every figure in §7 is priced at 1.6 both sides of it, and *holds a
hull the deep never rated* is the zone's sentence and not a PF write. A garden that took the
lip's sound away would be a different argument: this one is that the plateaus can make ground
that holds people, on water that carries everything they do on it, and stay.

### 4. The riser's minute — withdrawal's arithmetic, done by somebody else

*Thin Water* priced leaving ([mission-thin-water.md](mission-thin-water.md) §4) and *Prospect*
priced the round trip ([mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) §4): the concern comes down
at 72 and goes up at 15, and the Commune does neither. At 20:30 the basin's riser lifts off the
lip's floor, loud, and the concern begins its ascent — seventy-three seconds of silent climb
from 2,500 m to 1,400 — and in that minute Teel's element is released. Descent is 45 m/s at a
SIG floor of 72 ([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §2), and the floors step down under the
escorts as they run south: held at 1,500 over the staging, diving to 2,200 over the slopes,
to 2,600 over the terraces, to 3,000 on the lip. From the staging's far west the furrow is
2,864–3,044 m at 85 m/s — thirty-four to thirty-six seconds — and an element ordered south on
its release crosses 1,800 m about fifteen seconds later, is over the furrow at about
thirty-three, and is at 3,000 m at about forty-two: in the furrow by about **21:05** and on its
floor by **21:15**. Between 1,800 m and the furrow's edge a Corvette pays 4 HP/s of what does
not heal — seventeen and a half seconds, seventy points of 420 — and inside the furrow, once
the row is built, it pays nothing. Without the row it has 105 seconds from 1,800 m and is dead
at about 22:30, before the tide (§13).

The whole rim hears the plateaus' army come down inside the noise the concern is already
leaving by: 72 through open water is a Contact to a Cruiser's 65 from 5,655 m and to a
submersible's 85 from 6,688; from the middle of the slopes — about 800, 1,480, fifteen seconds
after the release — the concern's flagship and its western reader, both climbing north by then

at Bearing (1.98), the Order's reconnaissance at Contact (1.15) from the seat it withdrew to at
17:00 — 4,402 m off, and a Bearing at 2.01 from the inner station it had left — and the pair in
home water at Track from 696 m. Over the lip, at 1.6, the watch has it at Track (4.72) for the
nine seconds of the last dive. That is *the arrangement that kept the Directorate from having to
fight* ending ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §9), as a sound — a Commune gun descending, struck,
into ground that will hold it — and the close counts, by household, the people it is over for
first.

### The SIG budget

## 10. What It Teaches

One system, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10: **everything at once** — the campaign row's own
words. Each of the six systems the Commune campaign taught alone is spent here where §4 puts
it: the quiet economy at 00:00, by a column doing nothing audible on the loudest water in the

bed all day, everything under it at 0.4 and deaf; the momentum, arrived; withdrawal's
arithmetic at 20:30, done by the concern and answered by Juno's element coming down inside it;
and the ping, available and never needed. It is load-bearing by the last five minutes because
the last five minutes are three Commune guns descending into ground that only holds them if
the sowing was done, into noise that only covers them because somebody else is leaving.

**Underneath it, the campaign's whole subject.** *Tend* taught that quiet is a livelihood;
*Thin Water* that open water stops protecting it; *Convocation* that the arrangement cannot
decide anything faster than people can walk; *Deep Furrow* that a garden can be safe by depth;
*In Writing* that a plateau can grow a silence other people are deaf inside and the deaf people
have a clock; *Radicals* that the Commune cannot stop its own momentum and can only be louder
than it on purpose. The Second Seeding takes all of that to the site four armies are converging
on and asks the plateaus to garden. The plateaus garden. It is genuinely the seeding, and it is
genuinely the end of the arrangement, and the mission never says which it was.

What this mission deliberately does not teach:

- **Which ending was right.** [campaign.md](campaign.md) §9, kept to the letter: the three
  readings of §8 are priced identically by the register and ranked by nobody, and the one
  sentence Marr should not say aloud is spent inside the Complete reading, where it costs the
  most.
- **What the Mouth is.** The lip is the chart's edge and the mission's. Two returns are filed
  by the Commune as breathing on a count, which is not a classification; the concern's file
