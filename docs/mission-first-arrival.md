# The Attending 7 — First Arrival

> The seventh and last mission of the Directorate campaign ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §6),
> specified and built — the campaign's convergence slot ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §8) and its
> ending ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §9): not canon, not ranked, not merged, and not a fight. One of
> the fourteen documents written together that complete the bible's campaign, to the pattern
> [mission-sorrowgate.md](mission-sorrowgate.md) sets: everything here is authored — the forces, the
> water, the beats, the numbers and the text — and code transcribes this document.

**Setting:** The Rim — the Mouth's northern edge, the lip at 3,100 m over a floor 4,410 m
below everything ([world-map.md](world-map.md) §3), on the tide after
the concern's survey went home, in the same week of 214 PC that three other navies arrive for
three other reasons ([world-map.md](world-map.md); [campaign.md](campaign.md) §8;
[mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) §1).

**Mission id:** `attending-first-arrival` — namespaced by campaign after
`attending-conclave`, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §1.

**This is the first mission in the campaign that Korrin closes without spending her one
sentence.** Attendance ended on one, Intake on another, and the four missions between them
spent one apiece ([mission-attendance.md](mission-attendance.md) §12;
[mission-intake.md](mission-intake.md) §12). The campaign has been saving the seventh since
88 PC, and the ending is that she reads the count and stops. Everything below is arranged so
that the silence has something to be a silence *about*: a rim that is held by standing on it,
an instrument that is aboard and locked, and two sounds on the lip that get entered whatever
the column does.

## 1. What the Rim Is, From Below

The rim is the edge of an eleven-kilometre depression that returns pings before they should
arrive, on a cycle that shortened from 43 hours to 39 in 213 PC and is still shortening
([world-map.md](world-map.md); [timeline.md](timeline.md)). That edge — the lip — stands at
3,100 m over a floor at 4,410 m ([mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) §11), and
carries the richest resonant-crystal deposits ever surveyed, and — since this year — two
returns that a Consortium survey has filed three times as equipment fault
([mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) §1; [mission-item-nine.md](mission-item-nine.md)
§1). Every other campaign arrives at it from above or across. The Directorate arrives along
it, out of trench country, on the only side of the rim anybody was ever born on.

This document never explains the Mouth, describes it, or approaches it
([culture.md](culture.md) §6). The lip is as far as the chart goes, as it was for the concern,
and the map declines to author the depression ([mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) §11).

Three facts about this water decide the mission, and none of them is a fence.

**The lip carries like a trench, because it is the beginning of one with no far wall.** PF 1.60
along the whole southern kilometre of the map ([environments.md](environments.md);
[mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) §11). A column walking it is heard from one end of
it to the other, and hears the lip's own sounds from anywhere on it: the attendants at SIG 24
are contact to a Chorister from 4,175 m and bearing from 3,241, and under the Cantorate's dome
4,840 and 3,757. The Directorate's advantage here is not stealth. It is that the ground the
column has to cross is also the ground that reports itself.

**The terraces are Resonance Field, and bearings there lie a little.** PF 0.70 across the whole
crystal bench ([environments.md](environments.md)). A cohort standing on the faces is quiet to
everything above it — a silent Chorister, at the 4.3 its own idle SIG places it at in the 3–8
band, is contact to a Corvette's 50 only inside 663 m and bearing inside 515 — and is 400 m
higher than the water it walked in on, because the column crosses the lip at 3,000 m, the
deepest any hull is ever ordered (§11), and the terrace floor is 2,600. The rock differs by
five hundred, and the hundred metres under the column is water nothing stands on. The rim is
held from the quietest water on the map, and getting there is the loudest thing the cohort
does.

**The tide is the whole advantage, and it was bought with a record.** The watch heard the
concern descend at 72 for three minutes and sound six charted faces at 80, and entered both;
it entered the plateaus' bed on the western lip on the same day. The account went up the
Ninth, which is what put a calling at the head of it
([mission-conclave-attending.md](mission-conclave-attending.md) §1). The calling was not put
about a record, though: it was put because something is happening to the thing the cohorts
attend ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §8), and the cycle at the head of this section is what that
sentence means. The record only bought the tide. Knowing a tide early is worth exactly what the
slowest hulls in the game can convert it into, which is ground.

Nobody in this mission says any of that. It is what the column is standing on.

## 2. Whose Hulls the Player Commands

**The player commands the rim column: twelve Choristers and six Abyssal Submersibles on the
eastern lip, with the Cantorate's dome stood over them and their weapons locked.**

Cohort-Prime Adze commands in the water; Undermarshal Setha Korrin assigns from Sufficiency and
reads the count. The twelve are the cohort grown at the shallow band
([mission-trench-awakening.md](mission-trench-awakening.md) §6) and called at the head of the
Ninth ([mission-conclave-attending.md](mission-conclave-attending.md) §8) — authored fresh here,
because nothing carries a roster between missions
([mission-conclave-attending.md](mission-conclave-attending.md) §13), and named so the walk is
the same hulls arriving. The 9th Trench Cohort joins at the sill, exactly as Adze said it would
([mission-conclave-attending.md](mission-conclave-attending.md) §12), and the rim's own two
submersibles are the pair that have been on the lip since before anyone had ears here
([mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) §5). First Cantor Vehl Ossary is not on the channel
until the close, when the record notes that he was present.

**Engine bound, stated so nobody corrects it into a bug.** Four parties and a court slot: the
column, the plateaus' charting pair and their bed, the Order's reconnaissance and its party,
and the rim's own attendants — a party whose only assets in the water are two sounds. The Drift
is not a party, and what lifts off the lip at 18:30 lifts out of it.

**Every navy on this rim is weapons-cold, again.** Prospect's whole convergence was played in
contacts and registers rather than fire ([mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) §5), and
D+1 keeps it: the Order's four hulls carry no `armed` flag, the pair carries none, the bed is
unarmed, and the column's guns are locked. The only thing on this map that kills anything is
the riser, and only what stands on its line.

### What the column does not carry

1. **No weapons, and no torpedoes.** Locked, with the tide's own reason attached: *the law of

2. **No mines and no depth charges.** *Nothing is left in water that is attended.*
3. **No noisemakers.** *A countermeasure is a sentence, and the rim is not lied to.* The
   Cantorate put one sound in the water in mission 3
   ([mission-the-dome.md](mission-the-dome.md)); the ending is the mission where the faction
   does neither of the two things a transmission can be (§13).
4. **No active sonar.** Carried, live, and locked: *aboard, live, and not used — the rim is
   attended, not asked.* The campaign's oldest argument, arriving at the place it was always
   about ([mission-attendance.md](mission-attendance.md) §3). Withheld to mission 3, handed over
   at the Fourth's foot, priced in a basin
   ([mission-trench-awakening.md](mission-trench-awakening.md)), and here a lock with a reason on

   ([mission-item-nine.md](mission-item-nine.md) §2).
5. **No construction.** *The rim is not built on.*

**Silent Running is present, and the column opens under it.** Eighteen `silent` beats at tick
zero; the cohort walks the lip at 22 m/s instead of 40 and hears exactly as well either way.
**Every silent figure in this document is the hull's own, not the band's ceiling**
([mission-shallow.md](mission-shallow.md) §13): `silentRunningSig` places a hull in the 3–8 band
by its idle SIG, so a Chorister runs silent at **4.3** and an Abyssal Submersible at **4.8**, and
only a hull idling at 60 or more ever reaches the eight.

## 4. Information, as Tempo

The system this mission teaches, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §2: **information into tempo**
— what a record is worth when the thing that holds it moves at forty metres a second. Four
movements.

1. **The column already knows what the rim heard, and is told so before it is told anything
   else.** The briefing enters the concern's descent, its six soundings, and the plateaus' bed
   as facts the watch filed on D. Nothing in the first three minutes is a search. The one
   thing the column does not have is *position*, and position is the only thing the assignment
   asks for.
2. **The returns are entered from the seat, before the first order.** Both attendants are
   already at Bearing or better from where the column stands: attendant-b at 1,301 m reads a
   ratio of 6.46 — Track — and attendant-a at 2,600 m reads 2.13, which the dome lifts to 2.70.
   The attending row is met on the first pass and is not terminal, by design (§8).
   The attending is a condition of the water, not a task, and this is the only mission in the
   bible that says so by making the row unloseable.
3. **The first order is the walk, and the walk is the whole tempo.** From the eastern seat to
   the sixth face is 583 m — fifteen seconds. To the first face, 4,571 m: **a hundred and
   fourteen seconds at forty metres a second, two hundred and eight under silence.** Then the
   climb: the terraces stand 400 m above the lip, terrain lifts and never lowers
   ([systems-depth.md](systems-depth.md) §2), so a hull crossing onto the bench is raised at
   the ascent rate — 26.7 seconds, silent, free. The slowest hulls in the Rift cross the whole
   rim inside four minutes of the twenty-one they have, because they left a tide early.
4. **And the way back down is the only shove in the mission.** The ground caps a depth order;
   it does not cancel one. Install seats the column with no order standing, so a hull that
   climbs and then walks back off the bench simply stays at 2,600 m and says nothing about it —
   but a player who has asked for three thousand at any point owns that order still, and walking
   back onto the lip resumes it: 400 m at 45 m/s, **8.9 seconds at a SIG floor of 72**, which is
   the one thing on this map that breaches a ceiling of 25. A cohort that climbs once and stays
   is silent all tide. A cohort that dives back to the lip's own depth pays nine seconds of
   transmission, nine of debt, and nine more standing dark while the debt decays (§6).

Same detection maths, seventh life: Attendance spent it on a god, Intake on a pay slip, The
Dome on a lie, Shallow on a vote it could not hear, Trench Awakening on a colossus, Conclave on
an empty terrace, and First Arrival on a head start.

### The SIG budget

**SIG budget: 24** — a Chorister's cruise, and for the third time in this campaign a
description rather than a ceiling ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §10). Twenty-four is what the
column sounds like doing the only thing it is asked to do, and it is one under the silence
order the galleries wrote for it, which is not an accident and is not remarked on by anybody.

The playtest adversary is the player who dives. Every metre of depth this mission spends is
spent going *up*, and a player who reaches for the descent order — to get under the Order, to
follow the riser, to come off a terrace in a hurry — has found the only seventy-two in the
water and put it into a rim that is being listened to by three other navies.

## 6. The Hold

**The rim is held by standing on it. Twelve Choristers, two to a face, six faces, and nothing
raised, transmitted, or asked.**

The six faces are the concern's own sounding points, unchanged: 900, 2400 · 1700, 2650 · 2500,
2300 · 3500, 2600 · 4300, 2350 · 5100, 2700 ([mission-prospect.md](mission-prospect.md) §6).
The Directorate does not read them. It stands on them, which is a different verb doing a
different job: the concern proved a field by transmitting at it, and the column holds a rim by
being on it when the tide turns.

**The order the cohort walks under is Attendance's, carried to the rim.** A ceiling of SIG 25
per hull over the twelve, debt at 0.2 s per mission tick, capped at 45 s, and the dome withdrawn
from the whole cohort while any debt stands ([mission-attendance.md](mission-attendance.md) §5).
Read against the hull: a Chorister idles at 16, cruises at 24, and is 4.3 under Silent Running,
so **the cohort can do everything this mission asks of it and never breach.** The +40
break-silence spike is a *firing* spike and every weapon on the hull is locked, so nothing in
this mission can produce one. A descent is 72 and costs a second a second, and a second a second
to clear afterwards. The ceiling is not a fence around the mission; it is a fence around exactly
one manoeuvre, and the manoeuvre is changing your mind about a terrace.

**The other order the cohort stands under is the one at 18:00, and it is Adze's to hold.** The
lock in §3 carries a statement of fact — *nothing has stood into the watch* — and at 18:00 the
Order stands into it, which is the tick the fact stops being one and the lock becomes a
refusal. [characters.md](characters.md) names this and no mission has spent it: Adze has never
disobeyed, and the campaign puts one order in front of them that they might. This is that
order, and they attend it, on the channel, at the tick it arrives (§12). The ending pays the
line off by having the order held rather than crossed — which is the only way a faction whose
whole argument is that it never asks could pay it off at all. Nothing in the union can read a
refusal (§8), so it is authored as a voice and an epilogue clause and is not counted.

**The spread is the hold, and the ground says so without being told to.** Drift Health runs on
1,500 × 1,000 m cells here ([bestiary.md](bestiary.md) §6). The seat's cell holds twelve
Choristers, six submersibles and the dome: **116 of summed SIG while every one of them is
silent, against a threshold of 60 — 1.12 a second, and the cell is dead in seventy-nine
seconds.** Idle rather than silent it is 359 and dies in fifteen. Two Choristers on a face sum
32 and wear nothing; faces four and five share a cell at 64 and wear it at eight hundredths of a
point a second; six Choristers on one face are 96 and 0.72. Nothing in this mission is paid in
Biomass, so none of that costs the player anything at all — and the document states it rather
than hiding it, because the ledger is on the snapshot and a reader will do the arithmetic
([mission-intake.md](mission-intake.md) §13).

**What the column hears of the plateaus' bed, and what it does with it.** The Spore Veil at
1250, 3250 emits at 20 and sits inside its own symmetric cloud, so it carries 8
([units.md](units.md); the aura is the engine's, applied last and to everything inside the
radius). Through the lip's 1.6 that is contact to a Chorister from 2,101 m and
bearing from 1,631 — 2,436 and 1,891 under the dome. From the first face, 919 m away over
mixed water, the ratio is 2.17 and the reading is a bearing; from the second, 750 m, it is 3.25
and the reading is a classification; from the third, 1,570 m, it is 0.95 and there is nothing.
The watch classified it from its western station on the concern's day at 1,160 m and a ratio of
2.93, and entered it as a bed ([mission-second-seeding.md](mission-second-seeding.md)).

**It is entered again, as a bed, and nothing further is said about it.** Whether the lip was
sown and whether the people who sowed it stayed is the Commune's ending and not this document's
to carry. A Chorister that walks within 350 m of it listens at HYD 5 and hears nothing at all,
which the record enters as the bed's doing and not as a fault.

## 8. The Objective

**Hold the rim at the tide's turn, keep the ears whole, and enter what the lip says.**

Four rows. Two decide the count and two are read out beneath it.

| Objective | Text, as the panel carries it | Predicate | Standing |

| `the-rim` | *The rim is held at the tide's turn. Eight of twelve on the terraces is a hold, and the Undermarshalcy does not round up.* | `{ kind: 'extract'; role: 'cohort'; region: 'the-terraces'; count: 8 }` | **Terminal.** `revealAtTick` T(19), marker `terraces` (3000, 2500, r 2,500 — Prospect's own, over the same six faces), region `the-terraces` = 0, 2000, 6000, 1000 |

| `the-attending` | *The rim is attended. What is heard is entered, and the gap is entered too.* | `{ kind: 'attend'; count: 2 }` | Non-terminal, monotone. Read out, never ranked |
| `the-record` | *What is heard of the column is entered in accounts that are not ours.* | `{ kind: 'tolerance'; ticks: 3600; tier: Classification }` | Non-terminal. Sixty seconds, cumulative |

**`the-rim` is revealed late, and it has to be.** An `extract` row latches Met the first pass it
is satisfied and is never re-derived, so a row read "at the close" must be revealed by a beat at
the tick the close asks the question — Intake's roll idiom, and the reason the stalls' line at
19:00 and the reveal share a tick. Met: eight Choristers stand inside the terraces rectangle
when the tide turns. Unmet: fewer. **Nothing on this map can kill a Chorister**, so the only way
to be short is to be somewhere else.

**`the-watch` is standing, so it reads what is true at the close.** Met: four of the six ears
are whole. Unmet: the lip took three or more of what stood on the riser's line. Neither terminal
row carries a `reading` pair; their met and unmet are the ladder, and the ladder is the Results
table below.

**`the-attending` is met from the seat on the first pass, and that is the design** (§4). It is
non-terminal for exactly that reason: the attending continues whatever the column does, which is
the second clause of [campaign.md](campaign.md) §9's Directorate row, and a clause is not a task.
It is read out at the close and can never touch Complete, Partial or Lost. Met — *"Entered: both
returns. The attending continues."* Unmet — *"Not entered: a return. The attending continues from
the lip regardless."*

**The two attendants are the only attendable sounds in the water, and the Order's sounding
deliberately is not.** An emitter counts toward `attend` only if it carries a reading; the
Order's twenty seconds at 80 carries none, so the loudest thing on the rim this tide cannot be
banked, which is what keeps the count the lip's own.

**`the-record`'s readings.** Met: *"The column was classified for a minute of the tide by three
navies' ears. It was heard arriving; everything is. What it was doing on the terraces is in
three registers and none of them is this one."* Unmet: *"The column was not held at
classification for long. The slowest hulls in the Rift were on the rim before anybody had ears
on them, which is what tempo is."*

**What the union cannot say here, carried to §13.** Three things this mission would state if it
could: that the hold is a *depth* as well as a rectangle — no predicate reads a hull's depth, so
a Chorister lifted onto the bench and one hovering over it at 3,000 m count alike; that the
Order's sounding was heard and not answered — no predicate reads what the player declined to do;
and that this ending happened at all after the tide turns, which is the progression row every
ending document leans on and none owns.

## 10. What It Teaches

One system, per [campaign.md](campaign.md) §10: **information into tempo** — knowing first, and
what the slowest hulls in the game can turn that into. It lands in order across the beat table:
the record handed over in the briefing (00:00), the returns entered from the seat before an
order is given (00:00), the walk west (00:30 onward), the climb that is free and the descent
that is not (§4), the arrival of a navy that is faster and later (16:00), and the count taken on
ground the column has been standing on for eighteen minutes (19:00).

Underneath it, the campaign's argument, finished: **attendance is owed to what speaks, and the
Directorate is the navy that never asks.** Attendance taught that doing nothing is sufficient.
Intake taught that the living is loud and has to be walked to. The Dome put one sound in the
water and priced it. Shallow lost to altitude. Trench Awakening called something and did not
steer it. Conclave held a calling nobody crossed. And this mission takes the button all six of
those built up to, and locks it, with the reason written on the lock.

What this mission deliberately does not teach:

- **The Mouth.** The lip is as far as the chart goes, for the seventh time
  ([culture.md](culture.md) §6). The two returns are entered by bearing and period and it is not
  said what they are.
- **The war.** Four navies, one rim, nobody fires. What the situation becomes is the other three
  endings' business and none of them is canon ([campaign.md](campaign.md) §9).
- **The correction.** The Order stands into the watch at 18:00 and the watch does not answer it.
  What the Order raises and what the cohort does about it is D+2, in
  [mission-rim-deposits.md](mission-rim-deposits.md) and
  [mission-second-chord.md](mission-second-chord.md) — the law of these tides, read on two more
  of them.
- **The bed.** It is entered as a bed. Whether the lip was sown, and whether the people who sowed
  it stayed, is [mission-second-seeding.md](mission-second-seeding.md)'s ending.
- **Active sonar.** Aboard, live, locked, and the lock is the ending.

## 12. The Briefing

Spoken by Undermarshal Setha Korrin from Sufficiency, on the channel the column carries down the

> it before the armies, and the returns on the lip are still there to be entered.

**Undermarshal Setha Korrin, assigning the rim — 00:00**

> The rim is assigned. It has been attended for two centuries from the water it is attended
> from, and this tide it is attended from the ground it is attended over, which is a difference
> of posture and not of doctrine.
>
> What the watch heard on the concern's tide is given to the column, because a column that is
> not told what its own record holds is a column being asked to find it twice. A descent at
> seventy-two for three minutes. Transmissions at eighty against six charted faces, in an
> account that is not theirs. And a bed on the western lip, entered as a bed. The account went
> up the Ninth as well, which is why the column left a tide early and walked.
>
> Eighteen hulls are given to the rim. Twelve are the cohort and six attend the lip, and the
> dome is stood where the column stopped, because a dome is not carried and there was nothing on
> the rim to carry it to.
>
> The rim is held by standing on it. Six faces are charted on the terraces and the cohort is
> spread two to a face. Nothing is raised and nothing is put into the water: the arrays are
> aboard, they are live, and they are not used. The rim is attended. It is not asked.
>
> Nothing has stood into the watch. That is the law of these tides and it is entered as the law
> of these tides, and it is entered again at the count, whatever the tides have done with it by
> then.
>
> What is heard is entered. What is not heard is not entered, and the gap is entered too.
>
> Eight of twelve is a hold. The Undermarshalcy does not round up.

closes by declining to spend it, in front of the one person who would have had to act on it —
and Ossary, who has closed every mission the campaign has put him in by saying nothing, says
nothing again, and this time they mean the same thing by it.
