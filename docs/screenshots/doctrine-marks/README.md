# Doctrine marks

Frames for [ui-ux.md](../../ui-ux.md) §12.5's doctrine marks, from a solo match on The
Ventfront Divide, 1440 × 900, driven by the `run-game` skill. The player is the Consortium,
the first free navy.

- [klaxon-pinged.jpg](klaxon-pinged.jpg): the Caisson selected just after a ping, at SIG 95.
  The selection card prints the Consortium's plate glyph in iron grey beside `SIG 95`. The
  SELECTION and COMMANDS rules take the same grey; FLEET and PRODUCTION keep the cyan rule.
- [klaxon-loud-silent.png](klaxon-loud-silent.png): the same hull at SIG 64, mark lit,
  above the same hull in Silent Running at SIG 8, mark gone. Twice actual size.

- [silent-speed-penalty.png](silent-speed-penalty.png): the same hull in Silent Running with
  §7's `SPEED −45%` on a line of its own, unmarked: −45% is the general case. An earlier
  pass put it on the first line, where `TORP 2` ran off the card's right edge in Chromium.
  Twice actual size.

- [engage-shot-price.png](engage-shot-price.png): the Consortium's squad page with the
  Caisson selected. `ENGAGE` carries §9's `+25 SIG`, unmarked: a kinetic shot is the
  general case. Twice actual size.

The Commune's, the Directorate's and the Knights' marks are not pictured: a drive cannot
choose a navy before it readies. Both are held by `rendererSmoke.test.ts`, "doctrine marks".
