# Making a place, not filling empty space

Read the shared brief, `docs/world.md`, `docs/habitats.md`,
`docs/habitats-art-brief.md`, and the mission's account of the place.
Sorrowgate is a drowned civic transit court, not a Bathyarch mining base.

## Produce a place-to-kit sheet

Describe the former function, present use, construction, ageing and water in concrete
nouns. Map each to an existing approved environment prop or a material family.
Record why an intentionally empty area stays empty.

For the tutorial, the output lives in `docs/visual-reboot.md` §4: coral-set civic stone,
dome fragments, the quieter western vent approach and the dark basin. Its fauna
population stays exactly what the mission authored.

## Work inside the world's information boundary

- Source dressing from public terrain, not enemy positions, occupancy or future beats.
- Reuse `environment.ts`'s placement and budget rules before creating another scatterer.
  Keep props off forbidden ground and roofed passages as their registry rows require.
- Approved props keep their metre scale, geometry and licensed light. A material pass
  is not approval to enlarge them or add new emissive windows.
- No ambient creature meshes in a mission whose first lesson depends on quiet water.
  Gameplay animals remain earned contacts, not scenic props.
- No sunlight, unlimited glow, volumetric spectacle or new world-light family without
  an explicit change to the art brief and its governing document.

A new prop shape follows [unit design](../unit-design/SKILL.md)'s shape lane and the
hull author/intake/reviewer split. A retained kit follows
[material design](../material-design/SKILL.md). The world specialist approves neither.

## Prove it arrived

Count the props in the real client, not just in the placement function. A correct
registry with a broken URL or a lost load notification still draws an empty world.
Exercise first load, a pending-load rebuild, a ground delta and teardown.

Return the kit sheet, light sources, intentional absences, instance/triangle counts and
the live frames to [art direction](../art-direction/SKILL.md).
